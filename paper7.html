摘要 / Abstract
Achieving broad magnetic tunability within a single alloy, without compromising saturation magnetization (Ms) and mechanical performance, remains a significant challenge. Inherent trade-offs between magnetic and mechanical properties have severely constrained the design of such materials. Here, we achieve synergistic optimization of coercivity, saturation magnetization, and yield strength by introducing a hierarchical heterostructure strategy in Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5 multi-principal element alloy. By systematically varying the aging time, we modulate the evolution of alternating BCC and L12 nano-lamellar structures whose evolution fundamentally governs the magnetic response. These structures induce pronounced nano-lamellar boundary strengthening while, paradoxically, reducing the coercivity by an order of magnitude to an ultra-soft magnetic state (~0.233 kA m⁻¹), thereby overturning the long-standing paradigm where strengthening phases typically increase coercivity. Extended aging further drives the structural evolution into a highly anisotropic nano-lamellar architecture, enabling a deterministic switch to a robust hard magnetic state (~21.452 kA m⁻¹) and spanning two orders of magnitude in coercivity. Remarkably, this unprecedented tunability is accompanied by a synergistic enhancement of Ms to 158 Am² kg⁻¹, effectively mitigating the conventional magnetic dilution effect. The underlying mechanism is attributed to distinct roles of the nano-lamellar BCC FeCo-rich phase, which exhibits an ultra-soft magnetic Ms (252 Am² kg⁻¹) and transitions from acting as an exchange-spring softening agent to serving as a potent domain wall pinning network with strong shape anisotropy. This work establishes a "composition-invariant, structure-tailorable" paradigm for multifunctional magnetic materials, offering direct implications for next-generation electromagnetic technologies.
在不牺牲饱和磁化强度（Ms）和力学性能的前提下，于单一合金中实现宽广的磁性可调性，仍是一项重大挑战。磁性与力学性能之间固有的权衡严重制约了此类材料的设计。本文在 Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5 多主元合金中引入分级异质结构策略，实现了矫顽力、饱和磁化强度和屈服强度的协同优化。通过系统改变时效时间，我们调控交替 BCC 与 L12 纳米片层结构的演化，而这种演化从根本上支配磁响应。这些结构一方面带来显著的纳米片层界面强化，另一方面却又反常地将矫顽力降低一个数量级，达到超软磁态（约 0.233 kA·m⁻¹），从而颠覆了"强化相通常增大矫顽力"的长期范式。进一步延长时效驱动结构演化为高度各向异性的纳米片层构型，实现向稳健硬磁态（约 21.452 kA·m⁻¹）的确定性切换，矫顽力跨越两个数量级。值得注意的是，这种前所未有的可调性还伴随 Ms 协同提升至 158 Am²·kg⁻¹，有效缓解了传统的磁稀释效应。其机理归因于纳米片层富 FeCo 的 BCC 相的不同角色——该相具有超软磁的 Ms（252 Am²·kg⁻¹），并从充当交换弹簧的软化剂，转变为具有强形状各向异性的强效畴壁钉扎网络。本工作确立了多功能磁性材料的"成分不变、结构可裁"范式，对下一代电磁技术具有直接意义。

1. 引言1. Introduction

Magnetic materials are indispensable to modern technologies, driving advancements in power conversion, transportation electrification, and information processing. They are generally categorized as soft, semi-hard, or hard magnets based on their coercivity and energy products. Soft magnets, characterized by their low coercivity, high permeability, and high saturation magnetization, are crucial for the efficient operation of transformers, inductors, and recording devices. In contrast, hard magnets exhibit high coercivity, large remanence, and high energy products, making them essential for applications such as motors, generators, and storage media. The ongoing demand for greater efficiency and durability continues to drive the development of next-generation magnetic materials. Particularly, emerging dynamic applications, such as high-speed traction motors, impose stringent mechanical constraints, thereby exposing the extreme brittleness and limited machinability of conventional commercial magnets.
磁性材料对现代技术不可或缺，推动着功率转换、交通电气化和信息处理的进步。依据矫顽力和磁能积，它们通常分为软磁、半硬磁和硬磁体。软磁体以低矫顽力、高磁导率和高饱和磁化强度为特征，对变压器、电感器和记录器件的高效运行至关重要。相比之下，硬磁体具有高矫顽力、大剩磁和高磁能积，对电机、发电机和存储介质等应用不可或缺。对更高效率和耐久性的持续需求不断推动新一代磁性材料的开发。尤其是高速牵引电机等新兴动态应用施加了严苛的机械约束，从而暴露出传统商用磁体的极端脆性和有限的可加工性。
However, a fundamental challenge persists: the intrinsic trade-off between key properties. For soft magnetic materials, increasing mechanical strength often results in a decrease in magnetic softness—in metallic systems, lattice defects hinder dislocation motion and thereby enhance mechanical strength, but simultaneously act as domain wall pinning sites, resulting in increased coercivity and diminished magnetic softness. Therefore, soft magnetic design generally aims to minimize defects, whereas greater mechanical strength necessitates their deliberate introduction. Similarly, in permanent magnets, the simultaneous improvement of coercivity and saturation magnetization has been a longstanding challenge arising from the inherent inverse relationship between magnetization and the magnetocrystalline anisotropy field. Multi-principal element alloys (MPEAs) challenge traditional alloy design paradigms by offering unprecedented opportunities to tailor functional properties through their broad compositional space and unique core effects, providing a promising avenue for overcoming the traditional performance trade-off inherent in magnetic materials. Recent advances show that the incorporation of coherent, ordered nanoprecipitates within a ferromagnetic matrix can significantly enhance mechanical strength without compromising magnetic softness. For example, Han et al. developed non-equiatomic Fe-Co-Ni-Ta-Al alloys with paramagnetic coherent nanoparticles embedded in a ferromagnetic matrix, exhibiting ultralow coercivity (78 A m⁻¹), high tensile strength (~1.3 GPa) with 54% tensile ductility, moderate Ms (100 Am²/kg), and high resistivity (103 μΩ·cm).
然而，一个根本挑战始终存在：关键性能之间的固有权衡。对软磁材料而言，提高力学强度往往导致磁软性下降——在金属体系中，晶格缺陷阻碍位错运动从而增强力学强度，但同时又充当畴壁钉扎位点，导致矫顽力增大、磁软性减弱。因此，软磁设计通常力求最小化缺陷，而更高的力学强度又必然要求刻意引入缺陷。类似地，在永磁体中，同时提高矫顽力和饱和磁化强度长期以来都是难题，源自磁化强度与磁晶各向异性场之间固有的反比关系。多主元合金（MPEA）以其宽广的成分空间和独特的核心效应，提供了前所未有的调控功能性能的机会，从而挑战了传统合金设计范式，为克服磁性材料固有的性能权衡提供了有希望的途径。最新进展表明，在铁磁性基体中引入共格有序纳米析出相，能在不损害磁软性的前提下显著提升力学强度。例如，Han 等开发的非等原子比 Fe-Co-Ni-Ta-Al 合金，在铁磁性基体中嵌入顺磁性共格纳米颗粒，表现出超低矫顽力（78 A·m⁻¹）、高抗拉强度（约 1.3 GPa）与 54% 拉伸塑性、中等 Ms（100 Am²/kg）和高电阻率（103 μΩ·cm）。
The design concept based on MPEAs has led to the development of excellent new soft magnetic materials with exceptional properties, including gigapascal-class mechanical strength, even up to 2 GPa, along with excellent ductility, thermal stability, and ultra-high electrical resistivity. This strategy typically involves the incorporation of non-magnetic or weakly magnetic strengthening elements (such as Al, Ta, and Nb) that exhibit large negative mixing enthalpies (ΔHmix) with the primary ferromagnetic elements (Fe, Co, and Ni). This thermodynamic driving force facilitates the formation of coherent, ordered intermetallic nanoprecipitates. While this approach successfully combines ultra-low coercivity with outstanding mechanical properties, it introduces a fundamental limitation: the addition of these non-magnetic elements inevitably dilutes the net magnetic moment, thereby reducing the saturation magnetization (Ms). A low saturation magnetization directly constrains motor torque and power density, hindering advancements toward lightweight and miniaturized designs. Conversely, significant progress has also been made in engineering microstructures for magnetic hardening in rare-earth-free MPEAs. For instance, thermomagnetic processing can produce fine nano-lamellar structures that serve as strong pinning sites for both magnetic domain walls and dislocations, thereby simultaneously enhancing coercivity and mechanical strength in the Co-Fe-Ni-Al system. Recent studies further demonstrate that nanoscale chemical shape anisotropy (CSA) serves as an effective mechanism for achieving hard magnetic behavior. Collectively, these findings underscore the critical importance of precise microstructural control in tailoring magnetic properties.
基于 MPEA 的设计理念已催生出性能卓越的新型软磁材料，包括吉帕级、甚至高达 2 GPa 的力学强度，兼具优异塑性、热稳定性和超高电阻率。这一策略通常引入与主要铁磁性元素（Fe、Co、Ni）具有较大负混合焓（ΔHmix）的非磁性或弱磁性强化元素（如 Al、Ta、Nb）。这种热力学驱动力促进共格有序金属间化合物纳米析出相的形成。虽然该方法成功地将超低矫顽力与出色的力学性能结合起来，却带来了一个根本局限：加入这些非磁性元素不可避免地稀释净磁矩，从而降低饱和磁化强度（Ms）。低饱和磁化强度直接制约电机扭矩和功率密度，妨碍向轻量化、小型化设计的推进。反过来，在无稀土 MPEA 的磁硬化组织工程方面也取得了重要进展。例如，热磁处理可产生细小的纳米片层结构，充当磁畴壁和位错的强钉扎位点，从而在 Co-Fe-Ni-Al 体系中同时增强矫顽力和力学强度。最新研究进一步表明，纳米尺度化学形状各向异性（CSA）是实现硬磁行为的有效机制。总的来说，这些发现凸显了精确组织控制在调控磁性能中的关键作用。
Despite notable progress, significant challenges persist in the development of magnetic MPEAs. First, most reported MPEAs are specifically designed for either soft-magnetic or hard-magnetic applications, and the ability to selectively program distinct magnetic states from a single precursor through conventional thermal processing remains exceptionally rare. Second, although the role of coherent nanoprecipitates in achieving a synergy between mechanical strength and soft magnetic properties is well established, conventional paradigms in magnetic alloy design imply a rigid coupling between microstructure and coercivity; achieving an ultra-soft magnetic state and high saturation magnetization in a precipitation-strengthened alloy remains a major challenge. Third, direct, in-situ evidence linking complex multiphase structures to the underlying domain wall dynamics across the soft-to-hard transition is still limited. Here, we achieve synergistic optimization of coercivity, saturation magnetization, and yield strength by introducing a "composition-invariant, structure-tailorable" design strategy based on nano-lamellar heterostructures. Specifically, the nano-lamellar structure is engineered to transition from acting as an exchange-coupling channel (facilitating magnetic ultra-softness) to functioning as a potent pinning center (inducing magnetic hardness), while simultaneously serving as an effective barrier to dislocation motion to ensure high yield strength. Crucially, the high saturation magnetization is intrinsically derived from the ultra-high magnetic moment of the FeCo-rich lamellae. We utilize a Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5 (at.%) MPEA as a model system.
尽管进展显著，磁性 MPEA 的开发仍存在重大挑战。其一，已报道的多数 MPEA 都是专门为软磁或硬磁应用设计的，而通过常规热处理从单一前驱体有选择地"编程"出不同磁态的能力极为罕见。其二，尽管共格纳米析出相在力学强度与软磁性能协同中的作用已被充分确立，传统磁性合金设计范式却暗示组织与矫顽力之间存在刚性耦合；在析出强化合金中同时实现超软磁态和高饱和磁化强度仍是重大挑战。其三，将复杂多相结构与软-硬转变过程中畴壁动力学相联系的直接原位证据仍然有限。本文通过引入基于纳米片层异质结构的"成分不变、结构可裁"设计策略，实现了矫顽力、饱和磁化强度和屈服强度的协同优化。具体而言，纳米片层结构被设计成从充当交换耦合通道（促成磁超软性）转变为充当强效钉扎中心（诱发磁硬性），同时充当阻碍位错运动的有效障碍以确保高屈服强度。关键在于，高饱和磁化强度本征地来自富 FeCo 片层的超高磁矩。我们以 Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5（原子分数）MPEA 作为模型体系。

2. 实验方法2. Experimental methods

The bulk alloy ingot (nominal Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5, at.%) was prepared by arc-melting high-purity raw metals (>99.9 wt.%) under high-purity argon (a Ti getter was pre-melted to remove residual oxygen and nitrogen); ingots were remelted at least five times for chemical homogeneity, then cut into 7-mm cubes. Phase diagrams were generated using Pandat with the PanHEA2023 database. Plates were solution-treated at 1323 K for 8 h and water-quenched (SS-MPEA); heterogeneous precipitation was driven by aging at 763 K for 4 h, 24 h, and 64 h (A4-MPEA, A24-MPEA, A64-MPEA). Bulk ingots corresponding to the FCC matrix, the nano-lamellar L12 phase, and the nano-lamellar BCC phase were individually arc-melted for reference. Characterization used XRD (Cu-Kα), EBSD, TEM/HAADF-STEM (aberration-corrected, geometric phase analysis GPA), and APT (LEAP 6000 XR). Magnetic response was measured by MPMS with VSM (field along the out-of-plane direction); Ms was obtained by fitting the high-field data to the law of approach to saturation. Electrical resistivity was measured by a four-probe Seebeck instrument (ZEM-3). Compression and tensile tests (Instron) and nanoindentation (Agilent G200) were performed at room temperature.
块体合金锭（名义成分 Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5，原子分数）在高纯氩气下由高纯原料金属（>99.9 wt.%）电弧熔炼制备（预熔 Ti 吸气剂以去除残余氧、氮）；铸锭重熔至少五次以保证化学均匀，再切成 7 mm 立方块。相图用 Pandat 配合 PanHEA2023 数据库生成。板材在 1323 K 固溶处理 8 h 后水淬（SS-MPEA）；通过在 763 K 时效 4 h、24 h、64 h 驱动异质析出（分别为 A4-MPEA、A24-MPEA、A64-MPEA）。另外单独电弧熔炼了对应 FCC 基体、纳米片层 L12 相和纳米片层 BCC 相的块体锭以作参照。表征采用 XRD（Cu-Kα）、EBSD、TEM/HAADF-STEM（球差校正，几何相分析 GPA）和 APT（LEAP 6000 XR）。磁响应用带 VSM 的 MPMS 测量（磁场沿面外方向）；Ms 通过将高场数据拟合到趋近饱和定律获得。电阻率用四探针塞贝克仪（ZEM-3）测量。压缩、拉伸试验（Instron）和纳米压痕（Agilent G200）在室温进行。

3.1 组织表征3.1 Microstructural characterization

The Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5 alloy was designed by exploiting differential chemical affinities to tailor solid-state phase separation. Guided by thermodynamic calculations, the alloy was solution-treated at 1323 K and quenched to obtain a supersaturated solid solution; aging at 763 K within the L12 + BCC (B2) dual-phase region drives phase decomposition. This rationale is founded on the distinct hierarchy in binding affinities for Al and Ti: their mixing enthalpies with Ni and Al (−35 and −22 kJ/mol) are significantly more negative than those of Co and Fe. Owing to this, Ni preferentially associates with Al and Ti, promoting the ordered L12 phase; the remaining Fe and Co (near-zero mutual mixing enthalpy) partition into adjacent regions, stabilizing the BCC phase. The ideal configurational entropy is ~1.36 R, which is thermodynamically important for counterbalancing the strongly negative mixing enthalpies and suppressing premature intermetallic compound formation. XRD shows SS-MPEA and A4-MPEA are single-phase FCC, whereas A24-MPEA and A64-MPEA develop BCC peaks; BCC precipitation shows a pronounced kinetic delay (higher nucleation barrier). Quantitatively, ~20% BCC forms in A24-MPEA, increasing to ~67% in A64-MPEA.
Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5 合金的设计利用了元素间不同的化学亲和力来裁剪固态相分离。在热力学计算指导下，合金在 1323 K 固溶并淬火以获得过饱和固溶体；在 763 K、L12 + BCC（B2）两相区内时效则驱动相分解。其依据在于 Al、Ti 结合亲和力的明显层级：Ni 与 Al、Ti 的混合焓（−35 和 −22 kJ/mol）比 Co、Fe 的负得多。因此，Ni 优先与 Al、Ti 结合，促进有序 L12 相；剩余的 Fe、Co（彼此混合焓近零）分配到邻近区域，稳定 BCC 相。理想构型熵约为 1.36 R，这对抵消强烈的负混合焓、抑制金属间化合物的过早形成在热力学上很重要。XRD 显示 SS-MPEA 和 A4-MPEA 为单相 FCC，而 A24-MPEA 和 A64-MPEA 出现 BCC 峰；BCC 析出表现出明显的动力学滞后（形核势垒较高）。定量上，A24-MPEA 中约形成 20% BCC，到 A64-MPEA 增至约 67%。
SS-MPEA is a disordered single-phase FCC. After short-term aging (A4-MPEA), coherent L12 nanoprecipitates (~2 nm, near-spherical, uniformly distributed) form in the FCC matrix and effectively redistribute the severe internal stress of SS-MPEA. In A24-MPEA, BF-STEM reveals two structural features: L12 nanoprecipitates (2–6 nm, spherical) within equiaxed FCC grains, and nano-lamellar regions where BCC and L12 lamellae alternate. The BCC nano-lamellae are enriched in Fe and Co (near 1:1), whereas the L12 lamellae are enriched in Ni, Al, and Ti; most lamellar BCC is shorter than 120 nm (average length ~47 nm) and <35 nm wide (average ~15 nm), following a Kurdjumov–Sachs (K–S) orientation relationship with the L12 lamellae. In A64-MPEA, a dual-phase BCC + L12 lamellar microstructure with high aspect ratio (~35, length ~485 nm, width ~14 nm) dominates, following the Nishiyama–Wassermann (N–W) relationship ({111}//{011}, ⟨110⟩//⟨100⟩); {111} twins form within the L12 lamellae to accommodate residual strain, and the BCC/L12 lattice mismatch is ~23–24% (semi-coherent). The lamellar morphology is dictated by a solid-state eutectoid reaction whose configuration is governed by the strong anisotropy of interfacial and elastic strain energies. APT gives the A24 BCC and L12 lamellae as Co48.1Fe47.6Ni3.3Al0.8Ti0.2 and Ni66.2Ti13Al10.2Co6.9Fe3.7 (at.%), and the A64 phases as Co47.3Fe47Ni4.2Al1Ti0.5 and Ni66.4Ti11.2Al10.1Co7.4Fe4.9 (at.%).
SS-MPEA 为无序单相 FCC。短时时效后（A4-MPEA），FCC 基体中形成共格 L12 纳米析出相（约 2 nm，近球形，均匀分布），有效重新分配了 SS-MPEA 的严重内应力。在 A24-MPEA 中，BF-STEM 揭示两种结构特征：等轴 FCC 晶粒内的 L12 纳米析出相（2–6 nm，球形），以及 BCC 与 L12 片层交替的纳米片层区域。BCC 纳米片层富集 Fe、Co（接近 1:1），而 L12 片层富集 Ni、Al、Ti；多数片层 BCC 短于 120 nm（平均长约 47 nm）、宽 <35 nm（平均约 15 nm），与 L12 片层遵循 Kurdjumov–Sachs（K–S）取向关系。在 A64-MPEA 中，以高长径比（约 35，长约 485 nm、宽约 14 nm）的 BCC + L12 双相片层组织为主，遵循 Nishiyama–Wassermann（N–W）关系（{111}//{011}，⟨110⟩//⟨100⟩）；L12 片层内形成 {111} 孪晶以协调残余应变，BCC/L12 晶格错配约 23–24%（半共格）。片层形貌由固态共析反应所决定，其构型受界面能与弹性应变能强各向异性的支配。APT 给出 A24 的 BCC 与 L12 片层成分分别为 Co48.1Fe47.6Ni3.3Al0.8Ti0.2 和 Ni66.2Ti13Al10.2Co6.9Fe3.7（原子分数），A64 的两相为 Co47.3Fe47Ni4.2Al1Ti0.5 和 Ni66.4Ti11.2Al10.1Co7.4Fe4.9（原子分数）。

3.2 磁性能与力学性能3.2 Magnetic and mechanical properties

After 24 h of aging (A24-MPEA), the coercivity Hc decreases by nearly an order of magnitude compared to the solution-treated condition, dropping from 2.390 ± 0.025 to 0.233 ± 0.094 kA m⁻¹. With aging increasing to 64 h (A64-MPEA), Hc increases dramatically to 21.452 ± 0.057 kA m⁻¹, representing a ~92-fold rise relative to A24. Meanwhile, Ms increases monotonically: 134 ± 1 (SS-MPEA), 142 ± 1 (A24-MPEA), and 158 ± 1 Am² kg⁻¹ (A64-MPEA). This trend reflects changes in intrinsic magnetic behavior: A24-MPEA shows two ferromagnetic phases (Curie temperatures Tc1 = 723 K and Tc2 = 973 K, corresponding to the FCC and high-moment BCC phases), whereas SS and A4 show a single ferromagnetic phase; in A64-MPEA an abnormal increase in magnetization occurs above 723 K, consistent with a "two-phase ferromagnet" mechanism (above Tc1 the FCC becomes paramagnetic and the magnetic response is dominated by the high-Tc, high-moment BCC phase). The yield strength increases continuously with aging: 370 ± 20 (SS), 455 ± 4 (A4), 840 ± 7 (A24), and 2360 ± 27 MPa (A64), with nanoindentation confirming that the BCC + L12 lamellae essentially drive the strengthening. Thus the soft-magnetic A24 shows a significant increase in both Ms and yield strength accompanied by a drastic reduction in coercivity, while the hard-magnetic A64 exhibits simultaneous enhancements in Ms, coercivity, and strength.
时效 24 h 后（A24-MPEA），矫顽力 Hc 相比固溶态下降近一个数量级，从 2.390 ± 0.025 降至 0.233 ± 0.094 kA·m⁻¹。当时效延长到 64 h（A64-MPEA），Hc 急剧增至 21.452 ± 0.057 kA·m⁻¹，相对 A24 提高约 92 倍。与此同时，Ms 单调升高：134 ± 1（SS-MPEA）、142 ± 1（A24-MPEA）、158 ± 1 Am²·kg⁻¹（A64-MPEA）。这一趋势反映了本征磁行为的变化：A24-MPEA 出现两个铁磁相（居里温度 Tc1 = 723 K、Tc2 = 973 K，分别对应 FCC 和高磁矩 BCC 相），而 SS 和 A4 为单一铁磁相；A64-MPEA 在 723 K 以上磁化强度反常升高，符合"两相铁磁体"机制（Tc1 以上 FCC 变为顺磁，磁响应由高 Tc、高磁矩的 BCC 相主导）。屈服强度随时效持续升高：370 ± 20（SS）、455 ± 4（A4）、840 ± 7（A24）、2360 ± 27 MPa（A64），纳米压痕证实 BCC + L12 片层实质上主导了这种强化。因此，软磁的 A24 在 Ms 和屈服强度显著提高的同时矫顽力急剧下降，而硬磁的 A64 则在 Ms、矫顽力和强度上同步增强。
The tunable magnetic properties are highlighted by comparison with representative materials: A24-MPEA achieves relatively high Ms and low Hc, with an Ms superior to amorphous and nanocrystalline alloys (though still below Fe-Co and Fe-Si), and a relatively high electrical resistivity ρ of 86 μΩ·cm (exceeding conventional Fe-Co and Fe-Si alloys, attributed to synergistic electron scattering from multiple crystal defects and the high density of phase boundaries in the nano-lamellar heterostructure that reduces the electron mean free path—advantageous for minimizing eddy-current losses). The A64-MPEA exhibits an excellent combination of extremely high Ms (158 ± 1 Am² kg⁻¹) and moderate coercivity (21.452 ± 0.057 kA m⁻¹), classifying it as a hard magnetic material (Hc ≥ 10 kA m⁻¹); its Ms exceeds existing commercial permanent magnets and most reported MPEAs. However, the extensive precipitation of the BCC phase inevitably leads to compressive brittleness, so the broad trade-off between magnetic and mechanical performance persists. Notably, this single-precursor paradigm offers distinct manufacturing advantages: by decoupling shaping from hardening, complex components can be machined in the relatively ductile precursor and then given a final aging treatment to impart hard-magnetic properties and high strength—circumventing the machinability constraints of brittle magnets such as sintered NdFeB.
通过与代表性材料对比可凸显这种可调磁性：A24-MPEA 兼具较高 Ms 和低 Hc，其 Ms 优于非晶和纳米晶合金（虽仍低于 Fe-Co 和 Fe-Si），并具有较高的电阻率 ρ（86 μΩ·cm，超过传统 Fe-Co 和 Fe-Si 合金）——这归因于多种晶体缺陷带来的协同电子散射，以及纳米片层异质结构中高密度相界减小电子平均自由程（有利于降低涡流损耗）。A64-MPEA 兼具极高 Ms（158 ± 1 Am²·kg⁻¹）和中等矫顽力（21.452 ± 0.057 kA·m⁻¹）的优异组合，可归类为硬磁材料（Hc ≥ 10 kA·m⁻¹）；其 Ms 超过现有商用永磁体和多数已报道的 MPEA。然而，BCC 相的大量析出不可避免地带来压缩脆性，因此磁性与力学性能之间的总体权衡依然存在。值得注意的是，这种单前驱体范式带来独特的制造优势：通过将成形与硬化解耦，复杂部件可在相对韧性的前驱体上加工，再经最终时效处理赋予硬磁性能和高强度——从而规避了烧结 NdFeB 等脆性磁体的可加工性限制。

3.3 磁态可调的机理3.3 Mechanisms for tunable magnetic states

To elucidate the mechanisms, the magnetocrystalline anisotropy constant K1 was calculated via the law of approach to saturation, M = Ms(1 − a/H − b/H² − …) + χp·H with b = (8/105)(K1²/μ0²Ms²). The K1 values rise monotonically: 5.2, 6.8, 9.9, and 18.3 kJ m⁻³ for SS, A4, A24, and A64. The significant enhancement in effective anisotropy in A64 is primarily attributed to the microstructural evolution—a dense, percolating network of high-aspect-ratio BCC nano-lamellae introducing strong shape anisotropy alongside the intrinsic magnetocrystalline anisotropy. In-situ Lorentz TEM tracks domain evolution. The broad tunability is attributed to the evolving role of the high-moment, Fe-Co-rich BCC phase, which transitions from a soft magnetic nucleation agent into a hard magnetic pinning network. Upon short-term aging (A4), ~2 nm coherent L12 precipitates partially alleviate internal stress; chemical ordering enhances magnetocrystalline anisotropy, slightly increasing coercivity to 2.414 kA m⁻¹ (competition between stress-relaxation softening and order-induced hardening).
为阐明机理，通过趋近饱和定律计算磁晶各向异性常数 K1：M = Ms(1 − a/H − b/H² − …) + χp·H，其中 b = (8/105)(K1²/μ0²Ms²)。K1 值单调上升：SS、A4、A24、A64 分别为 5.2、6.8、9.9、18.3 kJ·m⁻³。A64 中有效各向异性的显著增强，主要归因于组织演化——高长径比 BCC 纳米片层形成致密贯通的网络，在本征磁晶各向异性之外引入了强形状各向异性。原位洛伦兹 TEM 跟踪了磁畴演化。这种宽广的可调性归因于高磁矩、富 Fe-Co 的 BCC 相角色的演变——它从软磁形核剂转变为硬磁钉扎网络。短时时效后（A4），约 2 nm 的共格 L12 析出相部分缓解内应力；化学有序化增强磁晶各向异性，使矫顽力略升至 2.414 kA·m⁻¹（应力松弛软化与有序诱导硬化之间的竞争）。
Prolonged aging for 24 h (A24) induces profound magnetic softening, with coercivity dropping to an ultra-low 0.233 kA m⁻¹ via two synergistic mechanisms. First, effective averaging of local magnetocrystalline anisotropy, dictated by the fine scale of the alternating BCC and L12 nano-lamellae: the domain-wall widths δw = π(Aex/K1)^(1/2) are 111 nm (BCC) and 163 nm (FCC), much larger than the physical dimensions of the BCC nano-lamellae (average width ~15 nm, length ~47 nm), so the domain walls span multiple lamellae and effectively average out local anisotropy fluctuations, greatly reducing pinning. Second, and more dominant, exchange-coupled softening: bulk-sample measurements show the nano-lamellar BCC phase has a higher Ms (252 Am² kg⁻¹) and lower Hc, while the L12 lamellae are paramagnetic. The BCC lamellae act as "soft channels" that drag cooperative magnetization reversal of the FCC matrix (an exchange-spring mechanism), serving as effective nucleation sites; in-situ Lorentz TEM confirms that domain walls associated with the BCC nano-lamellae exhibit exceptionally high mobility. A24-MPEA shows minimal hysteresis at low fields but significantly larger hysteresis at high fields—a hallmark of exchange-coupled systems.
延长时效至 24 h（A24）引发深度磁软化，矫顽力通过两种协同机制降至超低的 0.233 kA·m⁻¹。其一，由交替 BCC 与 L12 纳米片层的细小尺度所决定的局部磁晶各向异性有效平均：畴壁宽度 δw = π(Aex/K1)^(1/2) 分别为 111 nm（BCC）和 163 nm（FCC），远大于 BCC 纳米片层的物理尺寸（平均宽约 15 nm、长约 47 nm），因此畴壁横跨多个片层，有效地平均掉局部各向异性起伏，大幅减少钉扎。其二（也是更主导的）为交换耦合软化：块体样品测量显示纳米片层 BCC 相具有更高的 Ms（252 Am²·kg⁻¹）和更低的 Hc，而 L12 片层为顺磁性。BCC 片层充当"软通道"，拖动 FCC 基体的协同磁化反转（交换弹簧机制），充当有效形核位点；原位洛伦兹 TEM 证实与 BCC 纳米片层相关的畴壁具有异常高的可动性。A24-MPEA 在低场下磁滞极小，但在高场区磁滞明显增大——这是交换耦合体系的标志性特征。
In contrast, prolonged aging to 64 h (A64) results in dramatic magnetic hardening, with coercivity increasing to ~21.452 kA m⁻¹. This signals a fundamental shift in the role of the BCC phase, which transitions from a softening to a potent hardening agent. This hardening is governed by a dual mechanism. The primary mechanism is strong domain wall pinning: as the BCC fraction increases to ~67%, it forms a dense, percolating nano-lamellar network creating a high density of interfaces between the high-moment BCC and low-moment L12; within this network, the paramagnetic L12 lamellae, functioning as non-magnetic planar defects, create local energy wells that strongly impede domain-wall motion. Concurrently, the continuous FeCo-rich BCC lamellae physically isolate the phase and decouple the direct exchange interactions between adjacent ferromagnetic regions—this disruption of exchange softening yields a reversal process dominated by pinning (confirmed by in-situ Lorentz TEM, where domain walls are firmly immobilized by the dense network). The second mechanism is shape anisotropy: the BCC nano-lamellae have a high aspect ratio (~35), and magnetostatic energy is minimized when magnetization aligns along their longest axis, compelling the magnetization within the high-Ms BCC to preferentially align along the length. This preferred orientation introduces a strong uniaxial anisotropy—an energy barrier to rotation that directly increases coercivity (analogous to classic AlNiCo magnets). K1 rises monotonically to 18.3 kJ m⁻³ in A64.
相反，延长时效至 64 h（A64）导致显著的磁硬化，矫顽力增至约 21.452 kA·m⁻¹。这标志着 BCC 相角色的根本转变——从软化剂变为强效硬化剂。这种硬化受双重机制支配。主要机制为强畴壁钉扎：随 BCC 分数增至约 67%，形成致密贯通的纳米片层网络，在高磁矩 BCC 与低磁矩 L12 之间创造出高密度界面；在该网络内，顺磁性 L12 片层作为非磁性平面缺陷，形成强烈阻碍畴壁运动的局部能量陷阱。与此同时，连续的富 FeCo 的 BCC 片层将该相物理隔离，并解耦相邻铁磁区之间的直接交换相互作用——这种对交换软化的破坏使反转过程由钉扎主导（原位洛伦兹 TEM 证实畴壁被致密网络牢固固定）。第二种机制是形状各向异性：BCC 纳米片层长径比高（约 35），当磁化沿其最长轴取向时静磁能最小，从而迫使高 Ms 的 BCC 内磁化优先沿长度方向排列。这种择优取向引入强单轴各向异性——即阻碍转动的能垒，直接增大矫顽力（与经典 AlNiCo 磁体类似）。A64 中 K1 单调升至 18.3 kJ·m⁻³。
It is worth noting that, although A64-MPEA achieves a robust hard magnetic state, conventional spinodal alloys with lower compositional complexity (e.g., FeCrCo-based systems, and laser powder bed fusion with tailored spinodal decomposition) also exhibit remarkable hard-magnetic and broad magnetic tunability. However, this single MPEA precursor undergoes a distinctly non-monotonic magnetic transition: first reaching an ultra-soft state (~0.233 kA m⁻¹) at the intermediate aging stage, then transforming to a hard state (~21.452 kA m⁻¹) after prolonged aging. Crucially, this magnetic evolution is accompanied by a simultaneous increase in both saturation magnetization and yield strength. A common limitation of precipitation-strengthened soft-magnetic MPEAs is the reduction in Ms due to dilution from non-magnetic elements; here, chemical partitioning results in Fe-Co-rich BCC and Ni/Al/Ti-rich L12, and since the BCC phase has a higher inherent magnetic moment (bulk BCC Ms = 252 Am² kg⁻¹), increasing the BCC fraction enhances rather than reduces Ms. This multi-phase synergy fundamentally differs from extrinsic composite strategies: the bulk alloy achieves broad magnetic tunability and high yield strength entirely through intrinsic phase decomposition, without extrinsic magnetic fillers or the associated property compromises.
值得注意的是，尽管 A64-MPEA 实现了稳健的硬磁态，成分复杂度较低的传统调幅分解合金（如 FeCrCo 基体系，以及带有定制调幅分解的激光粉末床熔融）同样表现出出色的硬磁性和宽广的磁性可调性。然而，这一单一 MPEA 前驱体经历了明显的非单调磁转变：在中间时效阶段先达到超软态（约 0.233 kA·m⁻¹），延长时效后再转变为硬态（约 21.452 kA·m⁻¹）。关键在于，这种磁演化伴随着饱和磁化强度和屈服强度的同步提高。析出强化软磁 MPEA 的常见局限是非磁性元素稀释导致 Ms 下降；而这里，化学分配形成富 Fe-Co 的 BCC 和富 Ni/Al/Ti 的 L12，由于 BCC 相具有更高的本征磁矩（块体 BCC 的 Ms = 252 Am²·kg⁻¹），增大 BCC 分数反而提升而非降低 Ms。这种多相协同与外源复合策略有本质区别：块体合金完全通过本征相分解实现宽广的磁性可调性和高屈服强度，无需外源磁性填料，也没有随之而来的性能折衷。

4. 结论4. Conclusions

In summary, we have achieved a synergistic optimization among coercivity, saturation magnetization, and yield strength by establishing a "composition-invariant, structure-tailorable" paradigm in the Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5 MPEA. Through systematically varying the aging time to regulate the structural evolution of the nano-lamellar heterostructures, we achieved deterministic switching between distinct magnetic states from a single precursor. The alloy exhibits a unique non-monotonic magnetic transition: it evolves from an initial semi-hard magnetic state (−2.39 kA m⁻¹), first into an ultra-soft magnetic state (−0.233 kA m⁻¹), and subsequently into a robust hard magnetic state (−21.452 kA m⁻¹), spanning two orders of magnitude in coercivity. Crucially, this broad tunability is accompanied by a synergistic enhancement in both yield strength and saturation magnetization (reaching 158 Am² kg⁻¹), effectively mitigating the canonical magnetic dilution problem inherent in precipitation-strengthened alloys. We have elucidated that this unprecedented performance stems from the distinct functional evolution of the high-moment FeCo-rich nano-lamellae, which transition from acting as exchange-coupling channels that facilitate magnetization reversal (softening mechanism) to serving as a potent domain-wall pinning network enriched with strong shape anisotropy (hardening mechanism). This work provides direct, in-situ evidence of how complex multiphase architectures govern domain wall dynamics, offering a comprehensive understanding of the structure–property relationship, and provides a viable pathway for designing high-performance magnetic alloys for next-generation electromagnetic applications, ranging from load-bearing high-speed motors to information storage systems.
综上所述，我们通过在 Fe30Co30Ni30Al5Ti5 MPEA 中确立"成分不变、结构可裁"范式，实现了矫顽力、饱和磁化强度和屈服强度之间的协同优化。通过系统改变时效时间来调控纳米片层异质结构的结构演化，我们从单一前驱体实现了不同磁态之间的确定性切换。该合金呈现独特的非单调磁转变：从初始的半硬磁态（−2.39 kA·m⁻¹），先进入超软磁态（−0.233 kA·m⁻¹），随后进入稳健的硬磁态（−21.452 kA·m⁻¹），矫顽力跨越两个数量级。关键在于，这种宽广的可调性伴随着屈服强度和饱和磁化强度的协同提升（Ms 达 158 Am²·kg⁻¹），有效缓解了析出强化合金固有的典型磁稀释问题。我们阐明，这一前所未有的性能源自高磁矩富 FeCo 纳米片层不同的功能演变——它从充当促进磁化反转的交换耦合通道（软化机制），转变为富含强形状各向异性的强效畴壁钉扎网络（硬化机制）。本工作提供了复杂多相构型如何支配畴壁动力学的直接原位证据，给出对结构–性能关系的全面理解，并为设计面向下一代电磁应用（从承载型高速电机到信息存储系统）的高性能磁性合金提供了可行路径。
