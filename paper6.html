摘要 / Abstract
Multi-principal-element alloys (MPEAs) have attracted considerable attention due to their enhanced possibilities of obtaining superior properties by tailoring chemical compositions in an enormous space. This work developed a series of novel soft-magnetic MPEAs via the cluster formula approach of Al3(Co,Fe,Cr)14. Through deliberately manipulating their microstructures, ultrafine ferromagnetic body-centered-cubic (BCC) nanoparticles (3 ~ 8 nm in diameter) are coherently precipitated in a B2 matrix. These alloys exhibit a high saturation magnetization of 107.4 ~ 167.5 Am²/kg and a low coercivity of 143 ~ 303 A/m in the as-homogenized and aged states. Even after aging for 480 h at 873 ~ 1073 K, the prominent soft-magnetic properties can still be retained, which can be ascribed to the excellent stability of the coherent BCC/B2 microstructure. Importantly, these materials also show excellent soft-magnetic properties at high temperatures. The Al3Co7Fe7 alloy exhibits a saturation magnetization of 134.7 Am²/kg and a coercivity of 167.2 A/m at 973 K. Moreover, they have high Curie temperatures (1254 K for Al3Co7Fe7 and 1052 K for Al3Co6Fe6Cr2) and electrical resistivity (262 ~ 285 μΩ·cm). The outstanding high-temperature magnetic properties of the presently developed alloys is discussed in light of the microstructural stability and evolution with chemical composition and temperature, and the coercivity is found to be closely related to the particle size of BCC nanoprecipitates. With the advantages of the currently developed BCC/B2 MPEAs over conventional soft-magnetic alloys, the coherent precipitation approach opens a new way to design novel high-temperature soft-magnetic materials.
多主元合金（MPEA）因其可在巨大的成分空间中通过调控化学成分来获得优异性能，而受到广泛关注。本工作通过团簇式（cluster formula）方法 Al3(Co,Fe,Cr)14 开发了一系列新型软磁多主元合金。通过有意调控其组织，在 B2 基体中共格析出了超细的铁磁性体心立方（BCC）纳米颗粒（直径 3~8 nm）。在均匀化态和时效态下，这些合金表现出高饱和磁化强度（107.4~167.5 Am²/kg）和低矫顽力（143~303 A/m）。即使在 873~1073 K 时效 480 h 后，仍能保持突出的软磁性能，这归因于共格 BCC/B2 组织的优异稳定性。重要的是，这些材料在高温下也表现出优异的软磁性能。Al3Co7Fe7 合金在 973 K 时饱和磁化强度为 134.7 Am²/kg、矫顽力为 167.2 A/m。此外，它们具有高居里温度（Al3Co7Fe7 为 1254 K，Al3Co6Fe6Cr2 为 1052 K）和高电阻率（262~285 μΩ·cm）。本文结合组织随化学成分和温度的稳定性与演化，讨论了所开发合金优异的高温磁性能，并发现矫顽力与 BCC 纳米析出相的颗粒尺寸密切相关。凭借所开发的 BCC/B2 多主元合金相对传统软磁合金的优势，这种共格析出方法为设计新型高温软磁材料开辟了新途径。

1. 引言1. Introduction

Soft-magnetic materials with high saturation magnetization (or saturation induction intension) and permeability as well as low coercivity have been widely applied in power generation, electronic, and aerospace fields. Commercial soft-magnetic alloys, such as Fe–(3~4.5)Si and Fe–(30~50)Co (wt.%) alloys, are prevalent among industries due to their high saturation magnetization (Bs = 1.9 ~ 2.4 T) and low coercivity (Hc < 150 A/m). Nevertheless, their low electrical resistivity (ρ < 50 μΩ·cm) leads to a high eddy current loss, limiting their applications in high-frequency fields. Fe- and Co-based amorphous/nanocrystalline alloys have high electrical resistivity of 100 ~ 150 μΩ·cm due to their random atomic packing in a long range. Meanwhile, this kind of alloys possess high Bs (1.2 ~ 2.0 T) and low Hc (< 10 A/m), which is ascribed mainly to the uniform distribution of ultrafine ferromagnetic body-centered-cubic (BCC) nanoprecipitates (less than 20 nm) in the amorphous matrix, as exemplified by the Fe-based Finemet Fe73.5Si13.5Nb3B9Cu1 alloy. However, the metastable nature of the amorphous matrix limits their high-temperature applications to below 573 K (Curie temperature Tc ~ 850 K). The partial substitution of Co for Fe can increase the Bs, Hc, and Tc, as evidenced by the Hitperm Fe44Co44Zr7B4Cu1 (Bs = 2.0 T, Hc = 160 A/m, Tc = 1253 K). Moreover, this series of alloys have the largest resistivity (ρ ~ 150 μΩ·cm) among all existing conventional soft-magnetic alloys.
具有高饱和磁化强度（或饱和磁感应强度）和高磁导率、以及低矫顽力的软磁材料，已广泛应用于发电、电子和航空航天领域。商用软磁合金，如 Fe–(3~4.5)Si 和 Fe–(30~50)Co（wt.%）合金，因其高饱和磁化强度（Bs = 1.9~2.4 T）和低矫顽力（Hc < 150 A/m）而在工业界广泛使用。然而，它们较低的电阻率（ρ < 50 μΩ·cm）导致高涡流损耗，限制了在高频领域的应用。Fe 基和 Co 基非晶/纳米晶合金因其长程随机原子堆积而具有 100~150 μΩ·cm 的高电阻率。同时，这类合金还具有高 Bs（1.2~2.0 T）和低 Hc（< 10 A/m），这主要归因于非晶基体中均匀分布的超细铁磁性体心立方（BCC）纳米析出相（小于 20 nm），以 Fe 基 Finemet 合金 Fe73.5Si13.5Nb3B9Cu1 为代表。然而，非晶基体的亚稳性质将其高温应用限制在 573 K 以下（居里温度 Tc ~ 850 K）。以 Co 部分替代 Fe 可提高 Bs、Hc 和 Tc，Hitperm 合金 Fe44Co44Zr7B4Cu1 即为例证（Bs = 2.0 T，Hc = 160 A/m，Tc = 1253 K）。此外，这一系列合金在所有现有传统软磁合金中电阻率最大（ρ ~ 150 μΩ·cm）。
Similarly, multi-principal-element alloys (MPEAs), also known as high- and medium-entropy alloys (H/MEAs), possess high electrical resistivity due to the long-range disordered occupations of atoms on lattice sites. The high-entropy alloying strategy has attracted much attention since it can endow MPEAs with both prominent mechanical and functional properties, including corrosion/oxidation resistance, irradiation resistance, magnetic properties, electromagnetic wave-absorption, and thermoelectric properties. For instance, the Co27.7Fe32.6Ni27.7Ta5.0Al7.0 (at.%) MPEA possesses fascinating mechanical and soft-magnetic properties (a tensile yield strength of 904 MPa, saturation magnetization of 100 Am²/kg, coercivity of 78 A/m, electronic resistivity of 103 μΩ·cm, and Curie temperature of 694 K), which were ascribed to the special microstructure composed of the ferromagnetic face-centered-cubic (FCC) matrix and paramagnetic coherent L12 nanoprecipitates. It is noted that any crystalline defects (grain boundaries, phase boundaries, dislocations, solute elements, etc.) to strengthen alloys would impede the movement of magnetic domains through pinning the domain walls, leading to an increase in coercivity. For instance, the coercivity of the Co27.7Fe32.6Ni27.7Ta5.0Al7.0 alloy is strongly dependent on the particle size of coherent precipitates. As the average particle size increases from 24 to 91 nm, the coercivity decreases from 763 to 78 A/m, because the coherent stress characterized by the specific surface area and lattice misfit between the matrix and nanoprecipitates with a small particle size is higher than that with a medium particle size. If the particle size of nanoprecipitates is comparable to or slightly larger than the magnetic domain width, the coercivity might increase drastically and the soft-magnetic feature would disappear.
类似地，多主元合金（MPEA，也称高熵与中熵合金 H/MEA）因原子在晶格位置上的长程无序占据而具有高电阻率。高熵合金化策略之所以备受关注，是因为它能赋予 MPEA 兼具突出的力学与功能性能，包括耐腐蚀/抗氧化、抗辐照、磁性、电磁波吸收和热电性能。例如，Co27.7Fe32.6Ni27.7Ta5.0Al7.0（原子分数）MPEA 兼具引人瞩目的力学与软磁性能（拉伸屈服强度 904 MPa、饱和磁化强度 100 Am²/kg、矫顽力 78 A/m、电阻率 103 μΩ·cm、居里温度 694 K），这归因于由铁磁性面心立方（FCC）基体和顺磁性共格 L12 纳米析出相组成的特殊组织。值得注意的是，任何用于强化合金的晶体缺陷（晶界、相界、位错、溶质元素等）都会通过钉扎畴壁而阻碍磁畴运动，导致矫顽力增大。例如，Co27.7Fe32.6Ni27.7Ta5.0Al7.0 合金的矫顽力强烈依赖于共格析出相的颗粒尺寸：随平均颗粒尺寸从 24 增大到 91 nm，矫顽力从 763 降至 78 A/m，这是因为小颗粒尺寸时由比表面积和基体/纳米析出相晶格错配表征的共格应力高于中等颗粒尺寸时的情形。若纳米析出相的颗粒尺寸与磁畴宽度相当或略大，矫顽力可能急剧增大，软磁特性随之消失。
Extensive reports have demonstrated that the saturation magnetization is dependent on the concentration of ferromagnetic elements (Fe, Co, and Ni), while the coercivity and electrical resistivity are primarily controlled by the microstructure, including the phase constitution and morphology. In particular, the coercivity, as the fundamental parameter for soft magnetism, is closely related to the size of ferromagnetic precipitates in nanocrystalline alloys or the grain size in conventional polycrystalline alloys. MPEAs open a new avenue to design novel soft-magnetic alloys. For instance, the FCC-FeCoNi alloy exhibits an extremely-high saturation magnetization of 155.7 Am²/kg and low coercivity of 189 A/m, but further alloying with Al and Mn would deteriorate the soft-magnetic properties, as evidenced by the BCC/B2 FeCoNiMnAl alloy (Ms = 132.2 Am²/kg and Hc = 266 A/m) and the dual-phase FeCoNiMn0.5Al0.5 alloy with an FCC + BCC/B2 structure (Ms = 51.9 Am²/kg and Hc = 730 A/m). Recently, we developed a soft-magnetic Al1.5Co4Fe2Cr alloy via the cluster-formula approach with the formula of Al2(Mn14) (M14 = Co8Fe4Cr2), which exhibits a saturation magnetization of 135.3 Am²/kg, coercivity of 244 A/m, Curie temperature of 1061 K, and electrical resistivity of 244 μΩ·cm. The outstanding soft-magnetic properties are ascribed to its unique coherent microstructure with ultrafine, spherical, and ferromagnetic BCC nanoprecipitates with a size of 3 ~ 7 nm uniformly distributed in a B2 matrix. However, when the BCC/B2 coherent microstructure was reversed, i.e., large-sized cuboidal B2 nanoparticles (~100 nm) in the BCC matrix in an Al0.7NiCoFeCr2 alloy designed with the cluster formula of Al2Ni14, the soft-magnetic properties deteriorate seriously (Ms = 28.9 Am²/kg and Hc = 947 A/m). When a weave-like BCC/B2 microstructure appears in an Al0.5NiCoFeCr (Al2M14) alloy, the soft-magnetic feature almost vanishes due to the extremely high coercivity (4035 A/m) and extremely-low saturation magnetization (11.7 Am²/kg). These results demonstrated that the magnetic properties of MPEAs are strongly dependent on the microstructure.
大量研究表明，饱和磁化强度取决于铁磁性元素（Fe、Co、Ni）的浓度，而矫顽力和电阻率主要由组织（包括相组成和形貌）控制。尤其是矫顽力，作为软磁性的基本参数，与纳米晶合金中铁磁性析出相的尺寸、或传统多晶合金中晶粒尺寸密切相关。MPEA 为设计新型软磁合金开辟了新途径。例如，FCC-FeCoNi 合金表现出极高的饱和磁化强度（155.7 Am²/kg）和低矫顽力（189 A/m），但进一步添加 Al 和 Mn 会恶化软磁性能，BCC/B2 的 FeCoNiMnAl 合金（Ms = 132.2 Am²/kg，Hc = 266 A/m）和 FCC + BCC/B2 双相的 FeCoNiMn0.5Al0.5 合金（Ms = 51.9 Am²/kg，Hc = 730 A/m）即为佐证。近期我们通过团簇式方法、以 Al2(M14)（M14 = Co8Fe4Cr2）设计开发了软磁 Al1.5Co4Fe2Cr 合金，其饱和磁化强度 135.3 Am²/kg、矫顽力 244 A/m、居里温度 1061 K、电阻率 244 μΩ·cm。其优异软磁性能归因于独特的共格组织——B2 基体中均匀分布着尺寸 3~7 nm 的超细、球形、铁磁性 BCC 纳米析出相。然而，当 BCC/B2 共格组织反转，即以团簇式 Al2Ni14 设计的 Al0.7NiCoFeCr2 合金中 BCC 基体内出现大尺寸立方 B2 纳米颗粒（约 100 nm）时，软磁性能严重恶化（Ms = 28.9 Am²/kg，Hc = 947 A/m）。而当 Al0.5NiCoFeCr（Al2M14）合金中出现编织状（weave-like）BCC/B2 组织时，由于矫顽力极高（4035 A/m）、饱和磁化强度极低（11.7 Am²/kg），软磁特性几乎消失。这些结果表明，MPEA 的磁性能强烈依赖于组织。
In the present work, we applied the cluster formula of Al3M14 to design a new series of MPEAs in the Al–Co–Cr–Fe system, where M can be tailored and represents different combinations of Co, Cr, and Fe. The cluster formula approach has been successfully applied to design alloy compositions in multi-component systems, in which the alloying elements could be rationally matched and thus the amount of each element could be optimized. More significantly, the BCC/B2 coherent microstructure with spherical or cuboidal precipitates could be well controlled via the cluster formula in Al-TMs (transition metals) alloys to achieve prominent mechanical and functional properties simultaneously. Ni is removed in order to form Fe/Co-rich BCC nanoparticles to maximize the saturation magnetization since the magnetic moment (μH,i) of Ni is smaller than that of Fe and Co (μH,Fe = 2.2 μB, μH,Co = 1.7 μB, and μH,Ni = 0.6 μB; μB is the Bohr magneton). Meanwhile, a proper addition of Al via the cluster formula can avoid the formation of weave-like BCC/B2 microstructure. Cr is introduced because it has the potential to tailor the lattice misfit between the BCC and B2 phases for the formation of ultrafine spherical nanoprecipitates. Under the above considerations, five MPEAs were designed, i.e., S1-Al3Co7Fe7, S2-Al3Co6Fe7Cr1, S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2, S4-Al3Co7Fe4Cr3, and S5-Al3Co4Fe7Cr3. The microstructures and magnetic properties of these alloys in different states were systematically characterized.
本工作应用团簇式 Al3M14 在 Al–Co–Cr–Fe 体系中设计了一系列新型 MPEA，其中 M 可调，代表 Co、Cr、Fe 的不同组合。团簇式方法已成功用于多组元体系的合金成分设计，能合理匹配合金元素、优化各元素含量。更重要的是，在 Al–过渡金属（TM）合金中，可通过团簇式很好地控制带球形或立方析出相的 BCC/B2 共格组织，从而同时获得突出的力学与功能性能。为最大化饱和磁化强度而去除 Ni，以形成富 Fe/Co 的 BCC 纳米颗粒，因为 Ni 的磁矩（μH,i）小于 Fe 和 Co（μH,Fe = 2.2 μB，μH,Co = 1.7 μB，μH,Ni = 0.6 μB；μB 为玻尔磁子）。同时，通过团簇式适量加入 Al 可避免形成编织状 BCC/B2 组织。引入 Cr 是因为它有望调控 BCC 与 B2 相之间的晶格错配，以形成超细球形纳米析出相。基于上述考量，设计了五种 MPEA，即 S1-Al3Co7Fe7、S2-Al3Co6Fe7Cr1、S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2、S4-Al3Co7Fe4Cr3 和 S5-Al3Co4Fe7Cr3，并系统表征了它们在不同状态下的组织与磁性能。

2. 实验方法2. Experimental

The Al3(Co,Fe,Cr)14 MPEA samples were prepared by arc melting and suction-casting into a copper mold (2 mm × 9 mm × 60 mm) under an argon atmosphere; raw metals were better than 99.99 wt.%. Ingots (~10 g) were re-melted at least five times to ensure chemical homogeneity before suction-casting. Plates were homogenized at 1573 K for 2 h and aged for 24 h at 773, 873, 973, and 1073 K; some representative alloys were aged at 873 ~ 1073 K for 480 h to study thermal stability, with water quenching after all heat treatments. Crystal structures were examined by XRD (Cu-Kα, λ = 0.15406 nm) at 2°/min; microstructures by SEM, aberration-corrected TEM with EDS, and EBSD; and near-atomic-scale compositions by atom probe tomography (APT, CAMECA LEAP 5000 XR). Magnetization and coercivity at RT and high temperatures (873 and 973 K) were measured by VSM (max field 1200 kA/m); Curie temperatures were measured from 375 to 1123 K under 800 kA/m. Electrical resistivity at RT and high temperatures was measured with a variable-temperature-resistivity meter (TRT-1000) from RT to 1073 K (heating rate 10 K/min, every 50 K interval).
Al3(Co,Fe,Cr)14 MPEA 样品通过电弧熔炼并在氩气氛下吸铸入铜模（2 mm × 9 mm × 60 mm）制备；原料金属纯度优于 99.99 wt.%。铸锭（约 10 g）在吸铸前至少重熔五次以确保化学均匀。板材在 1573 K 均匀化 2 h，并在 773、873、973、1073 K 时效 24 h；部分代表性合金在 873~1073 K 时效 480 h 以研究热稳定性，所有热处理后均水淬。晶体结构用 XRD（Cu-Kα，λ = 0.15406 nm，2°/min）测定；组织用 SEM、带 EDS 的球差校正 TEM 和 EBSD 表征；近原子尺度成分用原子探针层析（APT，CAMECA LEAP 5000 XR）分析。室温及高温（873、973 K）下的磁化强度与矫顽力用 VSM（最大磁场 1200 kA/m）测量；居里温度在 800 kA/m 下从 375 到 1123 K 测量。室温及高温电阻率用变温电阻仪（TRT-1000）从室温到 1073 K 测量（升温速率 10 K/min，每 50 K 一个间隔）。

3. 结果 —— 软磁性能与组织3. Results — soft-magnetic properties & microstructure

In the as-homogenized state, initial-permeability values μi range from 24.8 (S1-Al3Co7Fe7, lowest) to 36.5 (S5-Al3Fe4Co7Cr3). RT hysteresis loops give saturation magnetization of 107.4 ~ 167.5 Am²/kg and coercivity of 143 ~ 303 A/m; increasing Cr gradually reduces Ms from 167.5 to 107.4 Am²/kg (S1 has the largest Ms), while coercivity stays at a low level. Upon aging at 773–1073 K for 24 h, the soft-magnetic properties of S1-Al3Co7Fe7 are insensitive to aging temperature (Ms 160–165 Am²/kg, Hc 151–183 A/m, comparable to the homogenized state), whereas the coercivity of S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2 (Ms ~129 Am²/kg) increases slightly with aging temperature (150–183 A/m at 773–873 K; 270–374 A/m at 973–1073 K). For S5-Al3Co4Fe7Cr3, coercivity increases drastically after HT aging (811.9 A/m at 973 K, 1974 A/m at 1073 K), implying a remarkable microstructural change. The saturation magnetization is not affected by aging temperature, confirming its close relation to ferromagnetic-element content; the low Ms (103–111 Am²/kg) of S5 is ascribed to its higher non-ferromagnetic Cr content (17.65 at.%). After long-term aging at 973–1073 K for 480 h, S1 still shows excellent soft-magnetic properties (Ms 157.9–165.4 Am²/kg, Hc 135.3–183.1 A/m), while long-term aging at 973 K deteriorates them (Hc up to 1950.2 A/m); S3 still exhibits prominent soft-magnetic properties (Ms 132.0 Am²/kg, Hc 159.2 A/m) after 480 h at 873 K.
在均匀化态，初始磁导率 μi 从 24.8（S1-Al3Co7Fe7，最低）到 36.5（S5-Al3Fe4Co7Cr3）。室温磁滞回线给出饱和磁化强度 107.4~167.5 Am²/kg、矫顽力 143~303 A/m；随 Cr 增加，Ms 从 167.5 逐渐降至 107.4 Am²/kg（S1 的 Ms 最大），而矫顽力保持在较低水平。在 773–1073 K 时效 24 h 后，S1-Al3Co7Fe7 的软磁性能对时效温度不敏感（Ms 160–165 Am²/kg，Hc 151–183 A/m，与均匀化态相当），而 S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2（Ms 约 129 Am²/kg）的矫顽力随时效温度略有增大（773–873 K 时为 150–183 A/m；973–1073 K 时为 270–374 A/m）。对于 S5-Al3Co4Fe7Cr3，高温时效后矫顽力急剧增大（973 K 时 811.9 A/m，1073 K 时 1974 A/m），意味着组织发生了显著变化。饱和磁化强度不受时效温度影响，证实其与铁磁性元素含量密切相关；S5 较低的 Ms（103–111 Am²/kg）归因于其较高的非铁磁性 Cr 含量（17.65 at.%）。在 973–1073 K 长时时效 480 h 后，S1 仍表现出优异的软磁性能（Ms 157.9–165.4 Am²/kg，Hc 135.3–183.1 A/m），而 973 K 长时时效则使其恶化（Hc 高达 1950.2 A/m）；S3 在 873 K 时效 480 h 后仍表现出突出的软磁性能（Ms 132.0 Am²/kg，Hc 159.2 A/m）。
XRD of the as-homogenized alloys shows they consist of disordered BCC solid solution and ordered B2 phases (100 superlattice peak). The lattice misfit ε between BCC and B2 phases increases with Cr content, in the range 0.07 ~ 0.24 %; S1-Al3Co7Fe7 has the lowest (ε ~ 0.07 %). EBSD, SEM and TEM show coarse equiaxed grains (200–400 μm) containing micro-scale cells (120–400 nm); STEM-EDS suggests the cells arise from Co and Fe segregation at cell boundaries. Spheroidal BCC nanoparticles of 3 ~ 5 nm are coherently precipitated in the ordered B2 matrix (DF image and SAED, HRTEM/FFT confirm perfect coherency with ε < 0.2 %). Thus these MPEAs have a hierarchical microstructure of macro-scale equiaxed grains, micro-scale cells, and nano-scale precipitates; the small lattice misfit favors spherical (rather than plate/needle-like) nanoprecipitates. After aging, macro-grains and micro-cells do not change significantly. For S1 aged at 773 K/24 h, ultrafine BCC nanoparticles (3–8 nm) precipitate in an L21 matrix; at 973/1073 K for 480 h they remain coherent at 3–8 nm with a volume fraction f ~ 10 %, showing that the BCC nanoprecipitates resist both aging temperature and time. APT gives BCC nanoprecipitates Al13.1Co45.7Fe41.2 and B2 matrix Al15.7Co43.0Fe41.3 (at.%). For S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2, BCC nanoprecipitates (~90 nm at 973 K/24 h) coarsen markedly and merge into coarse ellipsoidal particles (volume fraction ~28 %, ~150 nm long, ~190 nm wide after 480 h); APT shows the BCC particles are enriched in Cr and Fe (core Cr, shell Fe) while Al and Co partition to the B2 matrix, with a core-shell structure.
均匀化态合金的 XRD 显示其由无序 BCC 固溶体和有序 B2 相组成（100 超晶格峰）。BCC 与 B2 相之间的晶格错配 ε 随 Cr 含量增加，范围为 0.07~0.24%；S1-Al3Co7Fe7 最低（ε ~ 0.07%）。EBSD、SEM 和 TEM 显示粗大等轴晶（200–400 μm），晶内含微米级胞（120–400 nm）；STEM-EDS 表明胞源自 Co、Fe 在胞界的偏析。球状 BCC 纳米颗粒（3~5 nm）在有序 B2 基体中共格析出（DF 像与 SAED、HRTEM/FFT 证实完美共格，ε < 0.2%）。因此这些 MPEA 具有分级组织：宏观等轴晶、微米级胞、纳米级析出相；小的晶格错配有利于形成球形（而非片状/针状）纳米析出相。时效后，宏观晶粒和微米胞变化不大。对 773 K/24 h 时效的 S1，超细 BCC 纳米颗粒（3–8 nm）在 L21 基体中析出；973/1073 K 时效 480 h 后仍共格保持 3–8 nm、体积分数 f ~ 10%，表明 BCC 纳米析出相对时效温度和时间都很稳定。APT 给出 BCC 纳米析出相成分 Al13.1Co45.7Fe41.2、B2 基体 Al15.7Co43.0Fe41.3（原子分数）。对 S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2，BCC 纳米析出相（973 K/24 h 时约 90 nm）明显粗化并合并为粗大椭球颗粒（480 h 后体积分数约 28%、长约 150 nm、宽约 190 nm）；APT 显示 BCC 颗粒富集 Cr 和 Fe（核为 Cr、壳为 Fe），而 Al、Co 分配到 B2 基体，呈核-壳结构。
Curie temperatures are 1254 K (S1-Al3Co7Fe7) and 1052 K (S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2); non-ferromagnetic Cr decreases Tc. The soft-magnetic properties of S1 are insensitive to testing temperature, still showing high saturation magnetization (134–151 Am²/kg) and low coercivity (167–191 A/m) up to 973 K (slightly below the RT Ms of 162.8 Am²/kg). By contrast, the Ms of S3 decreases to 104–115 Am²/kg at 773–873 K and 58.0 Am²/kg at 973 K, while Hc increases sharply to 151 A/m at 773–873 K and 517.4 A/m at 973 K. Therefore S1 retains excellent soft-magnetic properties up to 973 K and S3 up to 873 K. The electrical resistivity of the homogenized MPEAs is 262 ~ 285 μΩ·cm (rising with non-ferromagnetic Cr); for S3 it rises slowly from RT to 300 μΩ·cm at 873 K, then dramatically up to 328 μΩ·cm at 1078 K—surpassing most HEAs (100 ~ 300 μΩ·cm). This high resistivity (about twice that of amorphous/nanocrystalline alloys and five times that of Fe-Si steels), combined with high Tc (>1000 K for S1, S3 and Al1.5Co4Fe2Cr), makes these alloys attractive as affordable high-temperature soft magnets whose HT soft-magnetic properties far exceed those of amorphous and nanocrystalline alloys.
居里温度分别为 1254 K（S1-Al3Co7Fe7）和 1052 K（S3-Al3Co6Fe6Cr2）；非铁磁性 Cr 降低 Tc。S1 的软磁性能对测试温度不敏感，直到 973 K 仍表现出高饱和磁化强度（134–151 Am²/kg）和低矫顽力（167–191 A/m）（略低于室温 Ms 162.8 Am²/kg）。相比之下，S3 的 Ms 在 773–873 K 降至 104–115 Am²/kg、973 K 降至 58.0 Am²/kg，而 Hc 在 773–873 K 急升至 151 A/m、973 K 升至 517.4 A/m。因此 S1 在 973 K 以下、S3 在 873 K 以下均保持优异软磁性能。均匀化态 MPEA 的电阻率为 262~285 μΩ·cm（随非铁磁性 Cr 升高）；S3 从室温缓慢上升到 873 K 的 300 μΩ·cm，随后急剧上升到 1078 K 的 328 μΩ·cm——超过大多数 HEA（100~300 μΩ·cm）。这种高电阻率（约为非晶/纳米晶合金的两倍、Fe-Si 钢的五倍），加之高 Tc（S1、S3 和 Al1.5Co4Fe2Cr 均 >1000 K），使这些合金成为有吸引力的经济型高温软磁体，其高温软磁性能远超非晶和纳米晶合金。

4. 讨论4. Discussion

(4.1 Thermal stability of BCC/B2.) The as-homogenized alloys consist of disordered BCC solid solution and ordered B2, and the low lattice misfit (0.07 ~ 0.24 %) between them produces the coherent microstructure with ultrafine ferromagnetic BCC nanoparticles (3 ~ 8 nm) in the B2 matrix. This renders prominent, incomparable soft-magnetic properties (Ms 107.4 ~ 167.5 Am²/kg, Hc 143 ~ 303 A/m). S1-Al3Co7Fe7 has the highest thermal stability: even after 1073 K/480 h its BCC nanoprecipitates do not coarsen significantly (3 ~ 8 nm), so its soft-magnetic properties remain comparable to the as-homogenized state. In S3 the BCC nanoprecipitates show negligible coarsening at 873 K but coarsen remarkably at 973 K (~90 nm at 24 h to ~150 nm at 480 h), raising coercivity to 1950 A/m. The lattice misfit between coherent phases induces elastic strain energy—the driving force for coarsening—and is closely related to composition difference; adding Cr enlarges the composition/lattice misfit (Cr strongly segregates to BCC), reducing thermal stability. In addition, Cr increases the BCC volume fraction (~28 % in S3 vs ~10 % in S1) and, above 973 K, Cr's fast diffusion (9.26 × 10⁻¹⁸ m²/s at 973 K, 1.55 × 10⁻¹⁶ at 1073 K—two to four times that of Al, Fe, Co) accelerates coarsening. Cr's strong segregation forms the core-shell composite nanoprecipitates (Cr core, Fe shell).
（4.1 BCC/B2 的热稳定性。）均匀化态合金由无序 BCC 固溶体和有序 B2 组成，二者之间低的晶格错配（0.07~0.24%）形成了 B2 基体中超细铁磁性 BCC 纳米颗粒（3~8 nm）的共格组织。这带来了突出且无可比拟的软磁性能（Ms 107.4~167.5 Am²/kg，Hc 143~303 A/m）。S1-Al3Co7Fe7 热稳定性最高：即使 1073 K/480 h 后其 BCC 纳米析出相也不明显粗化（3~8 nm），软磁性能仍与均匀化态相当。在 S3 中，BCC 纳米析出相在 873 K 几乎不粗化，但在 973 K 显著粗化（24 h 约 90 nm 到 480 h 约 150 nm），使矫顽力升至 1950 A/m。共格相之间的晶格错配诱发弹性应变能——这是粗化的驱动力——并与成分差异密切相关；加 Cr 会增大成分/晶格错配（Cr 强烈偏析到 BCC），降低热稳定性。此外，Cr 增大 BCC 体积分数（S3 约 28%，对比 S1 约 10%），且在 973 K 以上 Cr 的快速扩散（973 K 时 9.26 × 10⁻¹⁸ m²/s，1073 K 时 1.55 × 10⁻¹⁶——为 Al、Fe、Co 的两到四倍）加速粗化。Cr 的强偏析形成了核-壳复合纳米析出相（Cr 核、Fe 壳）。
(4.2 Coercivity vs. microstructure.) Coercivity depends on magnetic domains, whose motion is impeded by internal stress from crystalline defects. It is expressed as Hc = (λs·Δσ)/(2μ0·Ms·cosθ) · [3δ/l]/[1+3(δ/l)²] (Eq. 1), where λs is magnetostriction, Δσ and l the amplitude and characteristic wavelength of the internal stress field, and δ the domain wall width, estimated by δ = π(Aex/K1)^(1/2) (Eq. 2). For S1 across the as-homogenized and aged states, the hierarchical microstructure (macro grains, micro cells, nano particles) is very stable, with sizes 292 μm, 354 nm and 4 nm respectively. Coercivity varies with size as Hc ∝ 1/Dp^6 in the nanocrystalline regime (particle size ≪ domain wall width, anisotropy averaged over several structural units) and as Hc ∝ 1/Dp for large-scale variations (D > 100 μm); only when the size is comparable to the domain wall width does Hc rise to a maximum (>3000 A/m, i.e. semi-hard). The domain wall width was estimated at 211 nm (Eq. 2, a0 = 0.2870 nm, Tc = 1254 K, K1 = 6.8 kJ/m³). With interparticle distance λp = √(2/3)·Dp·(√(π/4f) − 1) (Eq. 3, f ~ 10 %), the coercivity contributions of macro grains, micro cells, and nano particles are proportional to their surface areas (Hc = A1·Hc-macro + A2·Hc-micro + A3·Hc-nano, with A1 = 0.0002, A2 = 0.1008, A3 = 0.89). The estimated total coercivity 133.5 A/m agrees with the measured 135 ~ 191 A/m, showing that the nano-scale particles dominate coercivity and that S1's low coercivity is primarily due to the uniform distribution of ultrafine ferromagnetic BCC nanoprecipitates. For S3, coarsening of BCC nanoprecipitates (4 → 90 → 350 nm) increases coercivity; when the particle size becomes comparable to or slightly larger than the domain wall width, coercivity increases drastically.
（4.2 矫顽力与组织。）矫顽力取决于磁畴，而磁畴运动受晶体缺陷内应力阻碍。其表达式为 Hc = (λs·Δσ)/(2μ0·Ms·cosθ) · [3δ/l]/[1+3(δ/l)²]（式 1），其中 λs 为磁致伸缩，Δσ 与 l 分别为内应力场的幅值和特征波长，δ 为畴壁宽度，由 δ = π(Aex/K1)^(1/2)（式 2）估算。对于跨越均匀化态与时效态的 S1，其分级组织（宏观晶粒、微米胞、纳米颗粒）非常稳定，尺寸分别为 292 μm、354 nm 和 4 nm。矫顽力随尺寸变化：在纳米晶区间（颗粒尺寸 ≪ 畴壁宽度、各向异性在若干结构单元上被平均）为 Hc ∝ 1/Dp⁶，在大尺度变化（D > 100 μm）下为 Hc ∝ 1/Dp；只有当尺寸与畴壁宽度相当时，Hc 才升至最大值（>3000 A/m，即半硬磁）。畴壁宽度估算为 211 nm（式 2，a0 = 0.2870 nm，Tc = 1254 K，K1 = 6.8 kJ/m³）。以颗粒间距 λp = √(2/3)·Dp·(√(π/4f) − 1)（式 3，f ~ 10%），宏观晶粒、微米胞和纳米颗粒对矫顽力的贡献与其表面积成正比（Hc = A1·Hc-macro + A2·Hc-micro + A3·Hc-nano，其中 A1 = 0.0002，A2 = 0.1008，A3 = 0.89）。估算总矫顽力 133.5 A/m 与实测 135~191 A/m 吻合，表明纳米级颗粒主导矫顽力，S1 的低矫顽力主要源自超细铁磁性 BCC 纳米析出相的均匀分布。对于 S3，BCC 纳米析出相的粗化（4 → 90 → 350 nm）使矫顽力增大；当颗粒尺寸与畴壁宽度相当或略大时，矫顽力急剧增大。
(4.3 Saturation magnetization vs. composition.) The saturation magnetization Ms (or saturation induction Bs) depends on the amount of ferromagnetic elements (Fe, Co, Ni), whose magnetic moments are 2.2, 1.7, and 0.6 μB, respectively. The mean magnetic moment per ferromagnetic atom is μ̄H = ΣμH,i·xi, where xi is the amount of element i. Bs shows a linear upward trend with the increased mean magnetic moment per ferromagnetic atom. The Bs values of some alloys (e.g., Al1.5Co4Fe2Cr, Hitperm Fe44Co44Zr7B4Cu1, and S1-Al3Co7Fe7) are slightly higher than a linear relationship would predict, probably due to strong short-range interactions among ferromagnetic atomic pairs, which contribute to the magnetic moment per atom effectively.
（4.3 饱和磁化强度与成分。）饱和磁化强度 Ms（或饱和磁感应强度 Bs）取决于铁磁性元素（Fe、Co、Ni）的含量，其磁矩分别为 2.2、1.7、0.6 μB。每个铁磁性原子的平均磁矩为 μ̄H = ΣμH,i·xi，其中 xi 为元素 i 的含量。Bs 随每个铁磁性原子平均磁矩的增大呈线性上升趋势。某些合金（如 Al1.5Co4Fe2Cr、Hitperm Fe44Co44Zr7B4Cu1 和 S1-Al3Co7Fe7）的 Bs 略高于线性关系的预测值，这可能源自铁磁性原子对之间强烈的短程相互作用，从而有效贡献了每原子磁矩。

5. 结论5. Conclusions

The present work developed a series of novel soft-magnetic MPEAs with a coherent microstructure consisting of ultrafine BCC ferromagnetic nanoprecipitates in the B2 matrix, by deliberately tailoring the chemical compositions via Al3(Co,Fe,Cr)14 (five alloys: Al3Co7Fe7, Al3Co6Fe7Cr1, Al3Co6Fe6Cr2, Al3Co7Fe4Cr3, Al3Co4Fe7Cr3). The main conclusions are: (1) The MPEAs exhibit excellent soft-magnetic properties (Ms 107.4 ~ 167.5 Am²/kg, Hc 143 ~ 303 A/m) as-homogenized; increasing Cr reduces Ms. The soft-magnetic properties of Al3Co7Fe7 are insensitive to aging temperature (high Ms 157.9 Am²/kg, low Hc 135.3 A/m even after 480 h at 773 ~ 873 K), whereas the coercivity of Al3Co6Fe6Cr2 increases with aging temperature (~150 A/m at 773–873 K to 270 ~ 374 A/m at 973 ~ 1073 K), and Al3Co4Fe7Cr3 increases drastically to 1974 A/m after 1073 K aging, ascribed to microstructural change. (2) The as-homogenized MPEAs contain ultrafine spherical BCC ferromagnetic nanoparticles (3 ~ 8 nm) in the B2 matrix, related to the small lattice misfit (ε < 0.21 %). Al3Co7Fe7 has the highest thermal stability (BCC nanoparticles do not coarsen after 480 h at 1073 K; slightly enriched in Co and Fe vs the Al-rich B2 matrix), giving Ms 134.7 Am²/kg and Hc 167.2 A/m at 973 K. (3) The coherent microstructure in Al3Co4Fe7Cr3 is also stable after 480 h at 873 K, giving comparable soft-magnetic properties (Ms ~130 Am²/kg, Hc ~150 A/m); these ultrafine nanoprecipitates have a core-shell structure (Cr core, Fe shell), and it shows Ms 104.4 Am²/kg, Hc 151.2 A/m at 873 K. Further increasing aging temperature/time coarsens BCC particles (~90 nm at 973 K/24 h, ~500 nm long × ~190 nm wide at 480 h), sharply raising coercivity to ~1950 A/m. (4) The coercivity is primarily dependent on the size of BCC nanoprecipitates; when the particle size is comparable to the domain wall width (~200 nm), coercivity increases drastically, degrading soft magnetism. (5) The BCC/B2 microstructure has high Curie temperature (1254 K for Al3Co7Fe7, 1052 K for Al3Co6Fe6Cr2) and electrical resistivity (262 ~ 285 μΩ·cm), showing great potential for HT soft-magnetic applications.
本工作通过团簇式 Al3(Co,Fe,Cr)14 有意调控化学成分，开发了一系列具有共格组织（B2 基体中含超细 BCC 铁磁性纳米析出相）的新型软磁 MPEA（五种合金：Al3Co7Fe7、Al3Co6Fe7Cr1、Al3Co6Fe6Cr2、Al3Co7Fe4Cr3、Al3Co4Fe7Cr3）。主要结论如下：(1) 均匀化态 MPEA 表现出优异软磁性能（Ms 107.4~167.5 Am²/kg，Hc 143~303 A/m）；增加 Cr 会降低 Ms。Al3Co7Fe7 的软磁性能对时效温度不敏感（即使 773~873 K 时效 480 h 后仍有高 Ms 157.9 Am²/kg、低 Hc 135.3 A/m），而 Al3Co6Fe6Cr2 的矫顽力随时效温度升高（773–873 K 约 150 A/m 到 973~1073 K 的 270~374 A/m），Al3Co4Fe7Cr3 在 1073 K 时效后急剧增至 1974 A/m，归因于组织变化。(2) 均匀化态 MPEA 在 B2 基体中含超细球形 BCC 铁磁性纳米颗粒（3~8 nm），与小晶格错配（ε < 0.21%）相关。Al3Co7Fe7 热稳定性最高（1073 K 时效 480 h 后 BCC 纳米颗粒不粗化；相对富 Al 的 B2 基体略富 Co、Fe），在 973 K 下 Ms 134.7 Am²/kg、Hc 167.2 A/m。(3) Al3Co4Fe7Cr3 的共格组织在 873 K 时效 480 h 后也很稳定，给出可比的软磁性能（Ms 约 130 Am²/kg，Hc 约 150 A/m）；这些超细纳米析出相为核-壳结构（Cr 核、Fe 壳），在 873 K 下 Ms 104.4 Am²/kg、Hc 151.2 A/m。进一步提高时效温度/时间会使 BCC 颗粒粗化（973 K/24 h 约 90 nm，480 h 长约 500 nm × 宽约 190 nm），使矫顽力急升至约 1950 A/m。(4) 矫顽力主要取决于 BCC 纳米析出相的尺寸；当颗粒尺寸与畴壁宽度（约 200 nm）相当时，矫顽力急剧增大，软磁性能退化。(5) BCC/B2 组织具有高居里温度（Al3Co7Fe7 为 1254 K，Al3Co6Fe6Cr2 为 1052 K）和高电阻率（262~285 μΩ·cm），在高温软磁应用中展现出巨大潜力。
