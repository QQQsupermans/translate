利用热磁场对纳米片层磁体进行磁硬化与力学硬化Magnetic and mechanical hardening of nano-lamellar magnets using thermo-magnetic fields

摘要 / Abstract
High-performance magnetic materials based on rare-earth intermetallic compounds are critical for energy conversion technologies. However, the high cost and supply risks of rare-earth elements necessitate the development of affordable alternatives. Another challenge lies in the inherent brittleness of current magnets, which limits their applications for high dynamic mechanical loading conditions during service and complex shape design during manufacturing towards high efficiency and sustainability. Here, we propose a strategy to simultaneously enhance the magnetic and mechanical performance of a rare-earth-free multicomponent magnet. We achieve this by introducing nano-lamellar structures with high shape anisotropy into a cobalt–iron–nickel–aluminum material system through eutectoid decomposition under externally applied thermo-magnetic fields. Compared to the conventional thermally activated processing, the thermo-magnetic field accelerates phase decomposition kinetics, producing finer lamellae spacings and smaller eutectoid colonies. The well-tailored size, density, interface, and chemistry of the nano-lamellae enhance their pinning effect against the motion of both magnetic domain walls and dislocations, resulting in concurrent gains in coercivity and mechanical strength. Our work demonstrates a rational pathway to designing multifunctional rare-earth-free magnets for energy conversion devices such as high-speed motors and generators operating under harsh service conditions.
基于稀土金属间化合物的高性能磁性材料对能量转换技术至关重要。然而，稀土元素成本高、供应有风险，亟需开发经济的替代方案。另一个挑战在于现有磁体的固有脆性，这限制了它们在服役中承受高动态机械载荷、以及在制造中实现复杂形状设计以追求高效与可持续方面的应用。本文提出一种同时提升无稀土多组元磁体磁性能与力学性能的策略。我们在钴–铁–镍–铝材料体系中，通过在外加热磁场下进行共析分解，引入高形状各向异性的纳米片层结构。与传统的热激活工艺相比，热磁场加速了相分解动力学，产生更细的片层间距和更小的共析团（colony）。经精心调控的纳米片层——其尺寸、密度、界面和化学成分——增强了对磁畴壁和位错运动的钉扎作用，从而同时提升了矫顽力和力学强度。我们的工作展示了一条设计多功能无稀土磁体的合理路径，可用于在严酷服役条件下运行的高速电机和发电机等能量转换装置。
Magnetic materials are key components for the green energy transition due to their role in sustainable wind energy conversion, electro-mobility, automation, and robotics, and provide stable high torque in motor applications. They can be classified based on their coercivity and energy products into soft, semihard, and hard magnetic materials. The critical rare earth (RE) elements are generally required in magnetically hard materials to provide strong spin-orbit coupling for achieving high magnetocrystalline anisotropy, which is the basis for large magnetic hysteresis. Alternative methods for enhancing anisotropy include introducing microstructural features with high shape anisotropy, stress anisotropy, chemical anisotropy, and other ways to enhance magnetocrystalline anisotropy. In addition, extensive efforts have been made to mitigate the ever-increasing requirement for applications under harsh environments, including high temperature, corrosion, hydrogen embrittlement, and mechanical loading conditions for sustainable electrification.
磁性材料是绿色能源转型的关键部件，因其在可持续风能转换、电动出行、自动化和机器人中的作用，并在电机应用中提供稳定的高扭矩。依据矫顽力和磁能积，磁性材料可分为软磁、半硬磁和硬磁材料。硬磁材料通常需要关键稀土（RE）元素来提供强自旋–轨道耦合，以获得高磁晶各向异性，而后者是大磁滞的基础。增强各向异性的替代方法包括引入具有高形状各向异性、应力各向异性、化学各向异性的组织特征，以及其他增强磁晶各向异性的途径。此外，人们还投入大量努力，以满足在高温、腐蚀、氢脆和机械载荷等严酷环境下、面向可持续电气化不断增长的应用要求。
For example, hard magnets with good mechanical properties are required in advanced flywheel energy storage systems that target an ultra-fast rotating speed of over 50,000 revolutions per minute for high-efficiency and sustainable electrification. This is because the rotation generates high dynamic mechanical and cyclic stress, and almost all conventional hard magnets fail at such speeds, thus limiting the efficiency of the whole system. However, most commercial hard magnetic materials are based on intermetallic compounds and are thus inherently brittle, rendering them incapable of bearing plastic deformation during processing and service. Therefore, the strongest hard magnets—e.g., Nd–Fe–B and Sm–Co alloys—are generally synthesized by powder metallurgy, which consists of a series of energy- and time-consuming and health-risky steps (metal powder production, mixing, compacting and sintering), as compared to conventional casting and forging. Although near-net-shape manufacturing can reduce machining and material loss, high-performance magnetic materials made by adjustable processing that can fill the cost and performance gap of current systems are needed for efficient and sustainable electrification. Current manufacturing methods still face challenges in minimizing material waste and ensuring product quality, because magnet properties are highly dependent on microstructure, which in turn depends on the manufacturing process. The inherent brittleness of intermetallic compounds is prone to forming microcracks during rapid cooling, increasing the risk of fracture during machining—for instance, Sm–Co-based alloys have a machining failure of ~20% because of their low fracture toughness (1.9–2.9 MPa·m^1/2). This design challenge is characterized by the need for a multifunctional profile: high remanence and coercivity; good mechanical performance (high hardness, yield strength, ductility, damage tolerance); and good oxidation/corrosion resistance under harsh chemo-mechanical operating conditions.
例如，先进飞轮储能系统为追求高效与可持续电气化，目标转速超过每分钟 50,000 转，这就需要力学性能良好的硬磁体。因为高速旋转产生高动态机械应力和循环应力，几乎所有传统硬磁体在此转速下都会失效，从而限制整个系统的效率。然而，大多数商用硬磁材料基于金属间化合物，因而固有脆性，无法在加工和服役中承受塑性变形。因此，最强的硬磁体——如 Nd–Fe–B 和 Sm–Co 合金——通常采用粉末冶金制备，相较传统铸造和锻造，它包含一系列耗能、耗时且有健康风险的工序（金属粉末制备、混料、压制和烧结）。尽管近净成形制造能减少机加工和材料损耗，但为实现高效、可持续电气化，仍需要通过可调工艺制造、能填补现有体系成本与性能差距的高性能磁性材料。当前制造方法在最小化材料浪费和保证产品质量方面仍面临挑战，因为磁体性能高度依赖组织，而组织又依赖制造工艺。金属间化合物的固有脆性在快速冷却时易形成微裂纹，增大后续机加工中的断裂风险——例如 Sm–Co 基合金由于断裂韧性低（1.9–2.9 MPa·m^1/2），机加工失效率约达 20%。这一设计挑战的特征是需要多功能组合：高剩磁与高矫顽力；良好的力学性能（高硬度、屈服强度、塑性、损伤容限）；以及在严酷化学–机械工况下良好的抗氧化/耐腐蚀性。
To overcome these challenges, multicomponent alloys (MCAs), which provide a wide range of chemical compositions and microstructures and thus a certain potential for realizing multifunctionality, have been proposed. MCAs can reconcile different magnetic and mechanical features in one material because continuous chemical adjustment allows tuning within a continuous phase space into regimes where magnetic properties (Curie temperature, coercivity, saturation magnetization) and mechanical properties (hardness, yield strength) can be simultaneously enhanced. In addition, the option to chemically decorate and functionalize microstructural defects at different length scales (dislocations, interfaces, precipitates) can be exploited to pin the movement of magnetic domain walls during magnetization and demagnetization, leading to a larger magnetic hysteresis integral. The enhanced internal stress level from introducing defects also increases mechanical strength by impeding dislocation movement. We translated these mutually conflicting properties into a design strategy by triggering ferromagnetic nano-lamellae with high shape anisotropy via thermo-magnetic annealing. We selected a Co28.6Ni28.8Fe30.6Al12.0 (at.%) MCA system: the ferromagnetic 3d elements (Co, Ni, Fe) blended near-equiatomically form a high-symmetry fcc solid-solution matrix (enabling ductile plasticity and high spontaneous magnetization), while Al (large atom size, negative mixing enthalpy with Co/Ni/Fe) triggers precipitates that enhance coercivity and mechanical strength, enabling hierarchical precipitation reactions coupled with tunable magnetic properties.
为克服这些挑战，人们提出了多组元合金（MCA）——它提供宽广的化学成分和组织范围，因而具备实现多功能性的一定潜力。MCA 能在同一材料中调和不同的磁性与力学特征，因为连续的成分调节可在连续相空间内调控到某些区间，使磁性能（居里温度、矫顽力、饱和磁化强度）和力学性能（硬度、屈服强度）同时得到提升。此外，还可利用在不同尺度上对组织缺陷（位错、界面、析出相）进行化学修饰与功能化的可能性，在磁化和退磁过程中钉扎磁畴壁的移动，从而获得更大的磁滞积分。引入缺陷带来的内应力提升，也通过阻碍位错运动提高力学强度。我们将这些互相冲突的性能转化为一种设计策略：通过热磁退火触发高形状各向异性的铁磁性纳米片层。我们选取 Co28.6Ni28.8Fe30.6Al12.0（原子分数）MCA 体系：铁磁性 3d 元素（Co、Ni、Fe）以近等原子比混合，形成高对称的 fcc 固溶体基体（可提供韧性塑性和高自发磁化），而 Al（原子尺寸大、与 Co/Ni/Fe 有负混合焓）触发析出相，增强矫顽力和力学强度，从而实现与可调磁性能相耦合的分级析出反应。

合金设计与组织分析Alloy design and microstructure analysis

First, we performed thermodynamic simulations to identify a phase with high magnetization and high anisotropy in the Co28.6Ni28.8Fe30.6Al12.0 (at.%) system. The results (Supplementary Fig. 1) show a two-phase fcc + bcc regime from 923 to 1623 K; the fcc phase further decomposes via a eutectoid transformation below 923 K, with the potential to form a lamellar structure with high aspect ratio and shape anisotropy. We synthesized the material by vacuum induction melting and conducted a two-stage isothermal heat treatment: homogenization at 1473 K for 2 h to obtain ~85 vol.% fcc, then thermal annealing (TA) at 873 K targeting a eutectoid lamellar microstructure for both mechanical and magnetic hardening (the lamellae and their high interfacial density enhance pinning of both domain walls and dislocations). Considering the external field's effect on decomposition kinetics, variants were processed by thermo-magnetic annealing (TMA) under a 9 T field at 873 K for 1–10 h (Fig. 1a). The external field aligns all magnetic moments in the ferromagnetic phases during annealing, enabling tailoring of crystallinity, texture, and chemistry toward high magnetization, high interface density, small lamellae spacing, and small colony size.
首先，我们进行热力学模拟，以在 Co28.6Ni28.8Fe30.6Al12.0（原子分数）体系中确定一种兼具高磁化强度和高各向异性的相。结果（补充图 1）显示，在 923 至 1623 K 存在 fcc + bcc 两相区；fcc 相在 923 K 以下通过共析转变进一步分解，有望形成高长径比、高形状各向异性的片层结构。我们采用真空感应熔炼制备材料，并进行两阶段等温热处理：先在 1473 K 均匀化 2 h 获得约 85 vol.% 的 fcc，再在 873 K 进行热退火（TA），目标是获得可同时实现力学与磁硬化的共析片层组织（片层及其高界面密度增强对畴壁和位错的钉扎）。考虑外加磁场对分解动力学的影响，各变体在 9 T 磁场、873 K 下进行热磁退火（TMA）1–10 h（图 1a）。退火时外加磁场使铁磁相中所有磁矩取向一致，从而可将结晶度、织构和成分调控到高磁化强度、高界面密度、小片层间距和小团尺寸的方向。
Ex-situ EBSD and ECCI analyses reveal eutectoid decomposition during TMA (Fig. 1b, c). The decomposition area gradually increased with TMA time until the remaining fcc phase was fully consumed after 5 h. The decomposition kinetics under TA are slower than under TMA (6 h to fully consume the fcc phase), also indicated by the slower migration of the decomposition interface. In-situ STEM at 873 K (Fig. 1d) with HAADF imaging revealed the formation and growth of nano-lamellae during TA. Figure 2a shows typical hysteresis loops for different treatments; the evolution of intrinsic coercivity (Hc) and saturation magnetization (Ms) with annealing time is in Fig. 2b. After 5 h of TMA (TMA5), Hc increased nearly 40-fold (~3930%) versus the homogenized H-MCA—from 0.7 to 27.5 kA·m⁻¹—which is twice the highest reported Hc (12.7 kA·m⁻¹) in the Co–Fe–Ni–Al system. Ms increased from 110.5 (H-MCA) to 123.8 Am²·kg⁻¹ (TMA5) then decreased to 118.4 (TMA10). TA-processed magnets follow similar trends but with lower peak Hc/Ms reached later (19.9 kA·m⁻¹ and 120.2 Am²·kg⁻¹ after 6 h).
离位 EBSD 和 ECCI 分析揭示了 TMA 过程中的共析分解（图 1b、c）。分解区域随 TMA 时间逐渐扩大，直至 5 h 后剩余 fcc 相被完全消耗。TA 下的分解动力学比 TMA 慢（需 6 h 才能完全消耗 fcc 相），分解界面迁移较慢也印证了这一点。873 K 下的原位 STEM（图 1d）配合 HAADF 成像，揭示了 TA 过程中纳米片层的形成与长大。图 2a 给出不同处理的典型磁滞回线；本征矫顽力（Hc）和饱和磁化强度（Ms）随退火时间的演化见图 2b。经 5 h TMA（TMA5）后，Hc 相比均匀化态 H-MCA 提高近 40 倍（约 3930%）——从 0.7 升至 27.5 kA·m⁻¹——是 Co–Fe–Ni–Al 体系已报道最高 Hc（12.7 kA·m⁻¹）的两倍。Ms 从 110.5（H-MCA）升至 123.8 Am²·kg⁻¹（TMA5），随后降至 118.4（TMA10）。TA 处理的磁体呈相似趋势，但峰值 Hc/Ms 较低且到达更晚（6 h 后为 19.9 kA·m⁻¹ 和 120.2 Am²·kg⁻¹）。
An Ashby-type plot (Fig. 2c) comparing Ms vs. Hc with commercial magnets shows that introducing and tailoring nano-lamellae by TMA tunes the Co–Fe–Ni–Al MCAs from a soft (H-MCA) to a semihard (TMA5) magnetic state while increasing Ms. The TMA5 magnet's high Hc outperforms all Fe–Co–Ni alloys studied thus far and is comparable to Fe–Co–V–Cr alloys, which have a lower fraction of the critical elements Co and Ni and thus lower cost—yet Fe–Co–V–Cr alloys generally contain 49–54 wt.% Co (Hc ~28 kA/m) whereas TMA5 contains only ~32 wt.% Co with comparable Hc, and TMA5's Ms (123.8 Am²·kg⁻¹) is much higher than that of Fe–Co–V–Cr alloys (<100 Am²·kg⁻¹). To understand the good combination of high Hc and Ms in TMA5, we studied its microstructure and chemistry from the microscale to the near-atomic scale (Fig. 3). High-resolution ECC imaging and transmission Kikuchi diffraction show the decomposition region consists of fine eutectoid colonies with an average size of 4.5 ± 3.4 μm. HAADF-STEM (Fig. 3a) shows alternating dark and bright lamellae with average spacings of 64.7 ± 22.9 nm and 182.2 ± 14.6 nm ("fine" and "coarse" layers); network structures with much finer widths (9.1 ± 2.3 nm) are distributed in the coarse layer.
将 Ms 与 Hc 同商用磁体对比的 Ashby 型图（图 2c）显示，通过 TMA 引入并调控纳米片层，可将 Co–Fe–Ni–Al MCA 从软磁态（H-MCA）调控到半硬磁态（TMA5），同时提升 Ms。TMA5 磁体的高 Hc 超过迄今研究的所有 Fe–Co–Ni 合金，并与 Fe–Co–V–Cr 合金相当——后者关键元素 Co、Ni 含量更低、成本更低；然而 Fe–Co–V–Cr 合金通常含 49–54 wt.% Co（Hc 约 28 kA/m），而 TMA5 仅含约 32 wt.% Co 却有相当的 Hc，且 TMA5 的 Ms（123.8 Am²·kg⁻¹）远高于 Fe–Co–V–Cr 合金（<100 Am²·kg⁻¹）。为理解 TMA5 中高 Hc 与高 Ms 的良好组合，我们从微观尺度到近原子尺度研究了其组织与化学（图 3）。高分辨 ECC 成像和透射菊池衍射显示，分解区由细小的共析团组成，平均尺寸 4.5 ± 3.4 μm。HAADF-STEM（图 3a）显示交替的暗、亮片层，平均间距分别为 64.7 ± 22.9 nm 和 182.2 ± 14.6 nm（即"细"层与"粗"层）；粗层中还分布着宽度更细（9.1 ± 2.3 nm）的网状结构。
Atomically resolved HAADF-STEM and FFT patterns identify both the network structure and the surrounding coarse layer as ordered B2 structures (Fig. 3b); the fine layer is a disordered fcc structure with a uniform distribution of coherently ordered L12 (Fig. 3c). The hierarchical structure from solid-state decomposition results from competition between severe lattice distortion and chemical ordering tendencies. APT (Fig. 3d–g) reveals the near-atomic chemistry: the network/coarse B2 layer is enriched in Ni and Al, while Fe and Co partition into the coarse layer (Co47.2Fe43.6Ni4.6Al4.5, at.%). The fcc and L12 phases were determined to be Ni33.0Co31.9Fe28.8Al6.3 and Ni65.7Al20.0Co7.3Fe7.0 (at.%), respectively.
原子分辨 HAADF-STEM 与 FFT 花样将网状结构及其周围的粗层都判定为有序 B2 结构（图 3b）；细层则为无序 fcc 结构，其中均匀分布着共格有序的 L12（图 3c）。这种由固态分解形成的分级结构，源自严重晶格畸变与化学有序化倾向之间的竞争。APT（图 3d–g）揭示了近原子尺度的化学：网状/粗层 B2 富集 Ni 和 Al，而 Fe、Co 分配进入粗层（Co47.2Fe43.6Ni4.6Al4.5，原子分数）。fcc 相与 L12 相的成分分别确定为 Ni33.0Co31.9Fe28.8Al6.3 和 Ni65.7Al20.0Co7.3Fe7.0（原子分数）。

磁结构表征Magnetic structure characterization

We used MOKE microscopy to characterize the interaction between magnetic domains and nano-lamellae during magnetization/demagnetization (Fig. 4a). Magnetizing from a demagnetized state to 300 mT and then to −200 mT revealed two domain sizes: coarse domains (average width 3.6 ± 1.5 μm) nucleate and grow inside the micron-sized bcc phase (formed during homogenization, ~15 vol.%), while fine domains, uniformly distributed in the eutectoid region, have widths below optical resolution. Domains grow unaffected in the bcc phase but are impeded at phase boundaries. Correlative MFM–AFM (Fig. 4b, c) shows a maze pattern in the bcc phase (anisotropy perpendicular to the surface) and strong magnetic contrast between fine (bright) and coarse (dark) layers; AFM confirms a smooth surface (max height difference 9 nm), so the distinctive domain patterns arise mainly from the difference in magnetization between alternating layers. Lorentz TEM at remanence (Fig. 4d, e) outlines domain walls; extrapolating defocus series gives an average domain wall width in the bcc phase of 43 ± 6 nm (typical for soft magnets), while the enlarged MFM image shows the alternating eutectoid layers act as pinning sites against domain wall motion.
我们用 MOKE 显微术表征磁化/退磁过程中磁畴与纳米片层的相互作用（图 4a）。从退磁态磁化到 300 mT 再到 −200 mT，观察到两种畴尺寸：粗畴（平均宽度 3.6 ± 1.5 μm）在微米级 bcc 相内部形核并长大（该相在均匀化时形成，约 15 vol.%），而在共析区均匀分布的细畴宽度低于光学分辨率。磁畴在 bcc 相内不受阻碍地长大，但在相界处受阻。相关性 MFM–AFM（图 4b、c）显示 bcc 相中呈迷宫状图案（各向异性垂直于样品表面），细层（亮）与粗层（暗）之间磁衬度强烈；AFM 证实表面平整（最大高差 9 nm），因此这些独特的畴图案主要源自交替层之间磁化强度的差异。剩磁态下的洛伦兹 TEM（图 4d、e）勾勒出畴壁；对离焦系列外推得到 bcc 相中平均畴壁宽度为 43 ± 6 nm（软磁材料的典型值），而放大的 MFM 图像显示交替的共析层充当了阻碍畴壁运动的钉扎位点。
Since coercivity is an extrinsic property largely determined by domain-wall/microstructure interactions, it strongly depends on the distribution of lattice defects and their pinning. The significant Hc increase of TMA5 during TMA is mainly attributed to strong pinning by the introduced defects—specifically eutectoid colonies with a small average size (4.5 ± 3.4 μm) and nano-lamellae with a fine spacing (123.5 ± 18.7 nm). The Hc increase until completion of eutectoid decomposition (5 h for TMA) correlates with the gradually increasing volume fraction of nano-lamellae and their interfacial density. Further annealing decreases coercivity due to coarsening of colony size and lamellae spacing (12.9 ± 10.1 μm and 192.6 ± 36.6 nm for TMA10), driven by a decrease in total interface energy. Future work could target variants with enhanced magnetic performance while preserving mechanical performance and lowering cost, by tailoring the chemistry, dimension, interlamellar spacing, and number density of the nano-lamellae—e.g., increasing pre-existing heterogeneous nucleation sites (grain boundaries, dislocations) via additive manufacturing to refine colony size and spacing, or promoting stronger grain texture and well-aligned lamellae via thermo-mechanical treatment (plastic forming, cold drawing, directional solidification) for enhanced remanence.
由于矫顽力是外禀性质，主要由畴壁与组织的相互作用决定，它强烈依赖于晶格缺陷的分布及其钉扎作用。TMA5 在 TMA 过程中 Hc 的显著增大，主要归因于所引入缺陷的强钉扎——具体为平均尺寸小的共析团（4.5 ± 3.4 μm）和片层间距细的纳米片层（123.5 ± 18.7 nm）。直到共析分解完成（TMA 为 5 h）之前 Hc 的增大，与纳米片层体积分数及其界面密度的逐渐增加相关。进一步退火则因团尺寸和片层间距的粗化（TMA10 为 12.9 ± 10.1 μm 和 192.6 ± 36.6 nm）而使矫顽力下降，其驱动力为总界面能的降低。未来工作可致力于在保持力学性能、降低成本的同时提升磁性能的变体——通过调控纳米片层的化学成分、尺寸、层间距和数量密度实现，例如借助增材制造增加预存的非均匀形核位点（晶界、位错）以细化团尺寸和间距，或通过热机械处理（塑性成形、冷拔、定向凝固）促进更强的晶粒织构和取向良好的片层以提升剩磁。
We studied the intrinsic magnetic properties by in-situ magnetization–temperature measurements. On heating from 300 to 1000 K at 20 K/min, the magnetization of H-MCA drops sharply to only 3.5% of its room-temperature value at 750 K, whereas TMA5 retains 37.5% at 1000 K. Slope analysis indicates H-MCA contains a ferromagnetic phase with Tc = 688 K, while TMA5 contains two ferromagnetic phases with higher Curie temperatures (Ni33.0Co31.9Fe28.8Al6.3, Tc ~ 740 K; Co47.2Fe43.6Ni4.6Al4.5, Tc > 1000 K). By casting these phases into individual ingots using APT-derived nominal compositions, we found that the enhanced Ms of TMA5 is mainly due to a ferromagnetic Co–Fe-enriched B2 phase with high magnetization (total magnetic moment 4.06 μB/f.u. by DFT), compared with the fcc phase in H-MCA before annealing (1.40 μB/f.u.).
我们通过原位磁化–温度测量研究本征磁性能。以 20 K/min 从 300 K 升温到 1000 K 时，H-MCA 的磁化强度在 750 K 急剧下降到仅为室温值的 3.5%，而 TMA5 在 1000 K 仍保留 37.5%。斜率分析表明，H-MCA 含有一个 Tc = 688 K 的铁磁相，而 TMA5 含有两个居里温度更高的铁磁相（Ni33.0Co31.9Fe28.8Al6.3，Tc 约 740 K；Co47.2Fe43.6Ni4.6Al4.5，Tc > 1000 K）。通过用 APT 得到的名义成分将这些相铸成单独的铸锭，我们发现 TMA5 增强的 Ms 主要源自一个高磁化的铁磁性、富 Co–Fe 的 B2 相（DFT 计算总磁矩 4.06 μB/f.u.），相比之下退火前 H-MCA 中的 fcc 相仅为 1.40 μB/f.u.。

力学性能Mechanical performance

We applied high-throughput nanoindentation to statistically analyze the hardness of the material after 3 h of TMA (TMA3), whose microstructures comprise micron-sized bcc, non-decomposed coarse-grained fcc, and eutectoid nano-lamellae. An array of 150 × 150 indents over 120 × 120 μm (100 nm depth, 800 nm spacing) was made at room temperature. An EBSD phase map (Fig. 5a) identified fcc and bcc; ECCI (Fig. 5b) contained 22,500 indents, each assigned to its microstructure (nano-lamellae treated as one phase; indents across microstructures termed "overlapping"). The reconstructed array (Fig. 5d) and hardness map (Fig. 5e) match the EBSD/ECCI results. Hardness–modulus analysis (Fig. 5f) revealed four clusters; the nano-lamellae and micron-sized bcc are much harder/stiffer than the undecomposed coarse-grain fcc. Median hardness values are 9.27 (nano-lamellae), 9.52 (micron bcc), and 6.06 GPa (coarse fcc); the overlapping region scatters from ~3 to 12 GPa. The high measured hardness reflects the indentation size effect (depth <100 nm); deeper indents (200 mN) still gave high hardness for the major phases, with dense <110>fcc slip lines forming in the coarse-grained fcc around the indents that stopped at fcc/bcc or fcc/nano-lamellae boundaries. Compressive tests show H-MCA and TA-5h can be plastically compressed up to 50%; the TA-5h alloy with eutectoid lamellae shows enhanced yield strength and strain hardening versus H-MCA, indicating that introducing nano-lamellae via eutectoid decomposition significantly hardens the magnetic material.
我们采用高通量纳米压痕，对经 3 h TMA（TMA3）后材料的硬度进行统计分析，其组织包含微米级 bcc、未分解的粗晶 fcc 以及共析纳米片层。在室温下于 120 × 120 μm 区域内做了 150 × 150 阵列压痕（深 100 nm、间距 800 nm）。EBSD 相图（图 5a）识别出 fcc 和 bcc；ECCI（图 5b）含 22,500 个压痕，每个压痕都归属到相应组织（纳米片层视为一相；跨组织的压痕称为"重叠"区）。重构阵列（图 5d）和硬度图（图 5e）与 EBSD/ECCI 结果吻合。硬度–模量分析（图 5f）揭示出四个聚类；纳米片层和微米级 bcc 远比未分解的粗晶 fcc 更硬、更刚。中位硬度分别为 9.27（纳米片层）、9.52（微米 bcc）和 6.06 GPa（粗晶 fcc）；重叠区在约 3 到 12 GPa 之间散布。所测得的高硬度反映了压痕尺寸效应（深度 <100 nm）；更深的压痕（200 mN）下主要相仍给出高硬度，压痕周围的粗晶 fcc 中形成了密集的 <110>fcc 滑移线，并终止于 fcc/bcc 或 fcc/纳米片层界面处。压缩试验表明 H-MCA 和 TA-5h 均可塑性压缩至 50%；含共析片层的 TA-5h 合金相比 H-MCA 表现出更高的屈服强度和应变硬化，说明通过共析分解引入纳米片层能显著硬化磁性材料。
These results show that introducing high-shape-anisotropy nano-lamellae via eutectoid decomposition also mechanically hardens the material. Nano-lamellae are unusual features of advanced magnets but provide an important additional benefit for mechanically loaded applications. Their size, density, interface, and chemistry—tailored by an external thermo-magnetic field—must be carefully controlled to increase structural defects to a level that enhances pinning of both magnetic domain walls and dislocations, while avoiding mechanical weakness and strain localization that would prevent the material from bearing plastic deformation during processing and use.
这些结果表明，通过共析分解引入高形状各向异性的纳米片层，同时也能对材料进行力学硬化。纳米片层是先进磁体中不常见的特征，但为承受机械载荷的应用提供了重要的额外益处。它们由外加热磁场调控的尺寸、密度、界面和化学成分必须精心控制，把结构缺陷增加到能增强对磁畴壁和位错双重钉扎的水平，同时避免造成力学薄弱和应变局部化，以免材料在加工和使用中无法承受塑性变形。

小结Summary

In summary, we developed a multicomponent magnet with magnetic properties (coercivity ~27.5 kA·m⁻¹, saturation magnetization ~123.8 Am²·kg⁻¹) matching those of commercial rare-earth-free hard magnets (e.g., Fe–Co–V–Cr alloys) and good mechanical performance in terms of high hardness. We realized this in a Co–Fe–Ni–Al system by introducing ferromagnetic nano-lamellae with high shape anisotropy via eutectoid decomposition under an external thermo-magnetic field. The additional field significantly accelerates decomposition kinetics and increases the effective pinning strength by reducing colony size and lamellae spacing. The nano-lamellae, with high average total magnetic moments, enhance saturation magnetization and act as barriers against the motion of both magnetic domain walls and dislocations, thus simultaneously enhancing coercivity and mechanical hardness. This strategy lays the groundwork for mechanically strong, magnetically hard multicomponent magnets for harsh service conditions, and is applicable to other magnetic families where external energy fields or pre-existing defects can modify microstructure for better multifunctionality. Future efforts could target higher coercivity and remanence while preserving mechanical properties and reducing dependence on critical elements for lower cost; secondary synthesis using scraps or waste with higher impurity content but lower cost could also serve as raw materials, with the impurity effects worth further investigation.
综上所述，我们研制出一种多组元磁体，其磁性能（矫顽力约 27.5 kA·m⁻¹，饱和磁化强度约 123.8 Am²·kg⁻¹）可与商用无稀土硬磁体（如 Fe–Co–V–Cr 合金）媲美，并在高硬度方面具有良好力学性能。我们在 Co–Fe–Ni–Al 体系中，通过外加热磁场下的共析分解引入高形状各向异性的铁磁性纳米片层实现了这一目标。附加磁场显著加速分解动力学，并通过减小团尺寸和片层间距提高有效钉扎强度。具有高平均总磁矩的纳米片层既提升饱和磁化强度，又充当阻碍磁畴壁和位错运动的障碍，从而同时增强矫顽力和力学硬度。该策略为开发面向严酷服役条件、力学强且磁性硬的多组元磁体奠定了基础，并可推广到其他磁体族——只要外加能量场或预存缺陷能修饰组织以获得更好的多功能性。未来工作可在保持力学性能、降低对关键元素依赖以降低成本的同时，追求更高的矫顽力和剩磁；采用杂质含量较高但成本更低的废料进行二次合成也可作为原料，其杂质影响值得进一步研究。
