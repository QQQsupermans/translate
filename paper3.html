摘要 / Abstract
Microstructural features across different length scales have a profound influence on the coercivity of magnetic alloys. Whereas the role of homophase boundaries on the pinning of magnetic domain walls is well established, the influence of heterophase interfaces on domain wall motion is complex and poorly understood. Here, we use state-of-the-art electron microscopy techniques to show that the magnetization reversal process in an Al0.3CoFeNi magnetic complex concentrated alloy (CCA), which is responsible for its coercivity, changes dramatically from a nucleation-type mechanism in the FCC+L12 state of the CCA, with a domain wall width of 171 nm, to a pinning type mechanism in the microstructure with colonies of FCC/L12 nanorods embedded in a BCC/B2 matrix, with a domain wall width of 35 nm. Our work reveals that heterophase FCC/BCC interfaces have a much stronger effect on coercivity than isostructural chemically ordered/disordered interfaces and provides a powerful guide to the rational design of microstructure to tune magnetic properties in both complex concentrated alloys and conventional magnetic alloys.
跨越不同尺度的组织特征对磁性合金的矫顽力有深刻影响。同相界（homophase boundary）对磁畴壁钉扎的作用已被充分认识，但异相界面（heterophase interface）对畴壁运动的影响却复杂而认识不足。本文采用最先进的电子显微技术表明：决定矫顽力的磁化反转过程在 Al0.3CoFeNi 磁性复杂浓缩合金（CCA）中发生了显著转变——从该 CCA 的 FCC+L12 状态下的形核型（nucleation-type）机制（畴壁宽度 171 nm），转变为 FCC/L12 纳米棒集束嵌于 BCC/B2 基体的组织中的钉扎型（pinning-type）机制（畴壁宽度 35 nm）。我们的工作揭示，异相 FCC/BCC 界面对矫顽力的作用远强于同结构的化学有序/无序界面，为通过合理设计组织来调控复杂浓缩合金与传统磁性合金的磁性能提供了有力指导。

1. 引言1. Introduction

Complex concentrated alloys (CCAs) or multi-principal-element alloys (MPEAs) or high entropy alloys (HEAs) are a novel class of materials that offer superior functional and structural performance in many applications. Their properties can be tuned over a very wide range as a result of the vast number of possible combinations of elements and processing strategies. Whereas most previous studies of CCAs have focused on their structural properties, much less effort has been paid to understanding their local functional properties.
复杂浓缩合金（CCA）、多主元合金（MPEA）或高熵合金（HEA）是一类新型材料，在许多应用中提供优异的功能与结构性能。由于元素组合与工艺策略数量极为庞大，其性能可在很宽范围内调控。此前对 CCA 的研究大多聚焦于结构性能，而对其局部功能性能的理解则投入不足。
Soft magnetic materials are key components in industrial applications that include electrical power generation, electrical machines, magnetic shielding and electromagnets. High frequency electrical motors require materials with high mechanical strength and superior magnetic properties. However, the low strength of conventional soft magnetic materials limits their use in such applications, particularly in high rotational frequency devices.
软磁材料是发电、电机、磁屏蔽和电磁铁等工业应用中的关键部件。高频电机需要兼具高力学强度和优异磁性能的材料。然而，传统软磁材料强度较低，限制了它们在此类应用中的使用，尤其是在高转速器件中。
Recent research has focused on identifying novel high-strength soft magnets in the HEA domain. Most magnetic CCAs contain Fe, Co and Ni, in combination with other alloying elements. Although some FeCoNi-X (X = Al, Si, Cu, AlSi, AlCu, AlMn, etc.) CCAs have promising combinations of magnetic and mechanical properties, their magnetic softness generally decreases with increasing mechanical strength, with their coercivity Hc depending sensitively on their composition and microstructure over various length scales. For example, the strength of the FeCoNi(CuAl)0.8Gax (0 < x < 0.08) HEA improved from 601 MPa for x = 0 to 1176 MPa for x = 0.08. However, its coercivity increased from 362 to 685.8 A/m. Another important property is saturation magnetization Ms, which increases with diamagnetic Ga content in FeCoNi(CuAl)0.8Gax HEAs. Such changes in magnetic and mechanical properties result from the heterogeneous microstructure of these alloys and are not yet understood experimentally.
近期研究着眼于在高熵合金领域寻找新型高强度软磁体。大多数磁性 CCA 含 Fe、Co、Ni，并配以其他合金元素。尽管一些 FeCoNi-X（X = Al、Si、Cu、AlSi、AlCu、AlMn 等）CCA 具有颇具前景的磁-力学性能组合，但它们的磁软性通常随力学强度提高而下降，其矫顽力 Hc 对成分及跨多个尺度的组织高度敏感。例如，FeCoNi(CuAl)0.8Gax（0 < x < 0.08）高熵合金的强度从 x = 0 时的 601 MPa 提高到 x = 0.08 时的 1176 MPa，但其矫顽力也从 362 增大到 685.8 A/m。另一重要性能是饱和磁化强度 Ms，它在 FeCoNi(CuAl)0.8Gax 高熵合金中随抗磁性 Ga 含量增加而升高。这些磁性与力学性能的变化源自这些合金的非均匀组织，实验上尚未得到理解。
Here, we study a model Al0.3CoFeNi CCA, whose magnetic and mechanical properties can be tuned by varying the processing conditions. The first sample was obtained by solutionizing the alloy at 1250 °C (cold rolling solution annealing or CRSA) to form an FCC+L12 microstructure. The second sample was obtained by annealing the solutionized alloy at 600 °C for 50 h to form a hierarchical microstructure (CRSA600). The magnetic and mechanical properties of the two alloys are given in Table 1. The large difference in properties (increase in Hc by a factor of >60 and increase in yield strength and hardness by a factor of >2) obtained by changing the heat treatment makes this alloy a model material to understand the influence of local variations in composition and microstructure on coercivity using quantitative in situ magnetic imaging techniques in the transmission electron microscope (TEM).
本文研究一种模型 Al0.3CoFeNi 复杂浓缩合金，其磁性与力学性能可通过改变工艺条件来调控。第一个样品通过在 1250 °C 固溶（冷轧固溶退火，CRSA）获得，形成 FCC+L12 组织；第二个样品通过将固溶态合金在 600 °C 退火 50 h 获得，形成分级组织（CRSA600）。两种合金的磁性与力学性能列于表 1。改变热处理所带来的性能巨大差异（Hc 增大 >60 倍，屈服强度和硬度提高 >2 倍），使该合金成为一种理想的模型材料，可借助透射电子显微镜（TEM）中的定量原位磁成像技术，理解成分与组织的局部变化对矫顽力的影响。

2. 实验方法2. Experimental

A plate-shaped arc-melted Al0.3CoFeNi ingot was procured from ACI Alloys Inc., CA, USA. The composition of the cast ingot was measured to be 9.4Al-30.1Co-31.8Fe-28.6Ni (at.%) using energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDXS) in a scanning electron microscope. The cast alloy was initially homogenized at 1250 °C for 1 h. Subsequently, all samples were cold rolled to 90%, encapsulated in quartz tubes backfilled with Ar and either solutionized at 1250 °C for 5 min (CRSA) or solutionized and annealed at 600 °C for 50 h (CRSA600), followed by water quenching. Magnetization (M) vs magnetic field (H) measurements obtained from the CRSA and CRSA600 samples are shown in Fig. S1 in the Supplementary Materials.
一块板状的电弧熔炼 Al0.3CoFeNi 铸锭购自美国加州 ACI Alloys 公司。经扫描电镜中的能谱（EDXS）测定，铸锭成分为 9.4Al-30.1Co-31.8Fe-28.6Ni（原子分数）。铸态合金先在 1250 °C 均匀化 1 h。随后，所有样品冷轧至 90%，封入充 Ar 的石英管中，或在 1250 °C 固溶 5 min（CRSA），或固溶后在 600 °C 退火 50 h（CRSA600），再水淬。CRSA 与 CRSA600 样品的磁化强度（M）随磁场（H）的测量结果见补充材料图 S1。
Electron-transparent (<200 nm thick) specimens for TEM investigations were prepared using focused ion beam (FIB) sputtering with Ga in a dual beam scanning electron microscope (FEI Helios 400). A protective C layer was deposited on the surface of the alloys, followed by a conventional lift-off preparation process. The specimens were transferred to Cu Omniprobe grids. Ga-ion-beam-induced surface damage was reduced by subsequently using low energy (<1 keV) Ar ion beam milling (Fischione 1040 Nanomill).
用于 TEM 研究的电子透明（厚度 <200 nm）样品，在双束扫描电镜（FEI Helios 400）中采用 Ga 聚焦离子束（FIB）溅射制备。先在合金表面沉积保护性 C 层，再进行常规的剥离（lift-off）制样。样品转移到 Cu Omniprobe 载网上。随后用低能（<1 keV）Ar 离子束减薄（Fischione 1040 Nanomill）以减轻 Ga 离子束引起的表面损伤。
Microstructural and chemical composition analyses were carried out using a probe-aberration-corrected scanning transmission electron microscope (FEI Titan 300-200) equipped with an in-column EDXS system (Super-X technology). High-angle annular dark-field (HAADF) scanning TEM (STEM) and EDXS spectrum images were recorded and processed using ThermoFisher Velox software. The inner semi-angle of the annular dark-field detector was 69.1 mrad for HAADF STEM imaging. Magnetic imaging of domain structures was carried out in magnetic-field-free conditions (Lorentz mode) by switching off the conventional objective lens in an image aberration-corrected TEM (FEI Titan 60-300). Images were recorded using a 4k direct electron counting detector (Gatan K2 IS) in counting mode. Off-axis electron holography was carried out using a single electron biprism, with a positive voltage of up to 120 V applied to the biprism wire, giving a 3.2 nm interference fringe spacing and ~40% fringe contrast. Holograms were analyzed using a standard fast Fourier transform algorithm in custom software written in the Semper image processing language.
组织与化学成分分析使用探针球差校正的扫描透射电镜（FEI Titan 300-200）进行，该机配备柱内 EDXS 系统（Super-X 技术）。高角环形暗场（HAADF）扫描 TEM（STEM）与 EDXS 谱图用 ThermoFisher Velox 软件采集与处理。HAADF STEM 成像时环形暗场探测器的内半角为 69.1 mrad。磁畴结构的磁成像在无磁场条件（洛伦兹模式）下进行——在像差校正 TEM（FEI Titan 60-300）中关闭常规物镜，用 4k 直接电子计数探测器（Gatan K2 IS）以计数模式采集图像。离轴电子全息采用单电子双棱镜，对双棱镜丝施加最高 120 V 的正电压，得到 3.2 nm 的干涉条纹间距和约 40% 的条纹衬度。全息图用以 Semper 图像处理语言编写的自定义软件、采用标准快速傅里叶变换算法分析。

3.1 CRSA 样品的组织3.1 Microstructure of the CRSA sample

Previous atom probe tomography and TEM studies of the CRSA sample revealed the presence of Al+Ni rich L12 (ordered FCC) precipitates of average size ~1.95 nm dispersed in an FCC matrix. Fig. 1(a) shows a composition-sensitive HAADF STEM image (Image intensity ~Z², where Z is atomic number) and a corresponding bright-field (BF) STEM image recorded from the same region. The HAADF STEM image confirms the presence of local fluctuations in chemical composition, while the BF STEM image, which is sensitive to diffraction conditions, reveals dense contrast variations that are attributed to the presence of the L12 precipitates. As this specimen was prepared for magnetic imaging with a large lateral size and a uniform thickness of ~100 nm, many particles contribute to the contrast. The fact that the precipitates are coherent with the matrix is evidenced by the absence of misfit dislocations in the high-resolution HAADF STEM image shown in Fig. 1(b).
此前对 CRSA 样品的原子探针层析和 TEM 研究表明，FCC 基体中弥散着平均尺寸约 1.95 nm、富 Al+Ni 的 L12（有序 FCC）析出相。图 1(a) 给出了对成分敏感的 HAADF STEM 像（像强度 ~Z²，Z 为原子序数）以及在同一区域记录的相应明场（BF）STEM 像。HAADF STEM 像证实存在化学成分的局部起伏，而对衍射条件敏感的 BF STEM 像则显示出密集的衬度变化，归因于 L12 析出相的存在。由于该样品为磁成像制备、横向尺寸大且厚度均匀（约 100 nm），大量颗粒都对衬度有贡献。图 1(b) 的高分辨 HAADF STEM 像中不存在错配位错，证明析出相与基体共格。

3.2 CRSA 样品的磁成像3.2 Magnetic imaging of the CRSA sample

The magnetic domain structure was first studied qualitatively using Fresnel imaging at a defocus of 1 mm in magnetic-field-free conditions. Magnetic domain walls are visible as black and white contrast lines (Fig. 1(c)). Splitting of vertical domain walls into pairs close to the specimen edge is associated with the formation of closure domains to reduce energetically-costly stray fields. As it is difficult to extract quantitative information from Fresnel defocus images, off-axis electron holography was used to further study the magnetic properties, recovering the phase shift φ of the electron wave induced by the projected in-plane component of the magnetic field. Fig. 1(d) shows a magnetic induction map of closure domains in the lower left corner of Fig. 1(c), confirming one 180° and two 90° magnetic domain walls. The width of the 180° domain wall was determined from the differential (∂φ/∂x) of the recorded phase shift using a fitting function y = y0 ± a·tanh((x − x0)/w). The domain wall width δw = πw is measured to be 171 ± 5 nm in the CRSA sample, larger than typical domain wall widths in Co (24 nm), Fe (64 nm) and Ni (82 nm). The electron beam direction is close to perpendicular to the field direction in the magnetic domains, providing a reliable way to determine the domain wall width in thin TEM specimens.
首先在无磁场条件下、以 1 mm 离焦量的菲涅耳（Fresnel）成像定性研究磁畴结构。磁畴壁表现为黑白衬度线（图 1(c)）。靠近样品边缘处竖直畴壁劈裂成对，与形成闭合畴以减少能量代价高的杂散场有关。由于难以从菲涅耳离焦像中提取定量信息，遂用离轴电子全息进一步研究磁性能，重建由磁场投影面内分量所诱导的电子波相移 φ。图 1(d) 给出图 1(c) 左下角闭合畴的磁感应图，证实存在一条 180° 畴壁和两条 90° 畴壁。180° 畴壁宽度由所记录相移的微分（∂φ/∂x）确定，采用拟合函数 y = y0 ± a·tanh((x − x0)/w)。CRSA 样品中畴壁宽度 δw = πw 测得为 171 ± 5 nm，大于 Co（24 nm）、Fe（64 nm）和 Ni（82 nm）的典型畴壁宽度。电子束方向接近垂直于磁畴中的磁场方向，为在薄 TEM 样品中确定畴壁宽度提供了可靠途径。

3.3 CRSA600 样品的组织与磁成像3.3 Microstructure & magnetic imaging of the CRSA600 sample

Heat treatment of the CRSA600 sample resulted in the formation of a striking nanorod structure comprising FCC+L12 and BCC+B2 regions, as reported previously. For magnetic imaging, a TEM specimen containing more than one grain was prepared so that magnetic domains oriented both parallel and perpendicular to the nanorods could be studied. Fig. 2(a) shows a low magnification BF TEM image. Region I contains multiple long nanorods of FCC(+B2)/L12 colonies in a BCC(+B2) matrix, whose width varies between 50 and 200 nm. Region II appears to contain equidimensional grains, which correspond to the nanorods viewed along their long axes. SAED patterns (Fig. 2(b, c)) show region I oriented along [110]FCC/[111]BCC and region II along [001]FCC/[001]BCC. The diffraction spots from the FCC and BCC phases overlap, indicating a close epitaxial relationship; a mistilt of ~3° was measured between the FCC and BCC columns. Red triangles mark weak superlattice reflections associated with L12 and B2 ordering.
如此前报道，CRSA600 样品的热处理形成了引人注目的纳米棒结构，包含 FCC+L12 与 BCC+B2 区域。为进行磁成像，制备了含多个晶粒的 TEM 样品，以便研究取向既平行又垂直于纳米棒的磁畴。图 2(a) 为低倍 BF TEM 像。区域 I 含有多根长纳米棒——BCC(+B2) 基体中的 FCC(+B2)/L12 集束，宽度在 50–200 nm 之间。区域 II 看似含等轴晶粒，实为沿长轴方向观察的纳米棒。SAED 花样（图 2(b、c)）显示区域 I 沿 [110]FCC/[111]BCC 取向、区域 II 沿 [001]FCC/[001]BCC 取向。FCC 与 BCC 相的衍射斑相互重叠，表明二者存在紧密的外延关系；测得 FCC 与 BCC 柱之间约 3° 的偏转（mistilt）。红色三角标记了与 L12、B2 有序相关的弱超晶格衍射斑。
Fig. 2(d, e) shows Fresnel defocus images of representative magnetic domain structures in the two regions in magnetic-field-free conditions. Strikingly, the magnetic domain walls in region I are regular and follow the straight heterophase interfaces closely, with fine black and white streaks visible inside the columns. In contrast, region II contains a highly dense random network of magnetic domain walls. Fig. S4 shows a large-field-of-view image of the projected in-plane magnetic induction in region I determined from a pair of Fresnel defocus images using the transport-of-intensity equation.
图 2(d、e) 给出无磁场条件下两区域中代表性磁畴结构的菲涅耳离焦像。引人注目的是，区域 I 中的磁畴壁规整，紧贴平直的异相界面走向，柱体内部可见细小的黑白条纹。相比之下，区域 II 含有极为密集的随机磁畴壁网络。图 S4 给出由一对菲涅耳离焦像经强度传输方程（TIE）确定的区域 I 投影面内磁感应的大视场图像。

3.4 磁畴与组织的关联3.4 Correlation between magnetic domains and microstructure

STEM EDXS spectrum imaging correlated the compositional variations of the FCC and BCC phases with the positions of magnetic domains in the CRSA600 sample. Fig. 3(a) shows a BF STEM image and corresponding Co, Fe, Ni and Al maps from region II, showing that the Co–Fe-rich BCC phase is the matrix, in which Ni–Al-rich FCC columns are distributed. The EDXS maps reveal compositional fluctuations in the Ni–Al-rich FCC columns and additional Ni enrichment parallel to the FCC/BCC interphase boundaries. Fig. 3(d) shows EDXS line scans across the interface; within the FCC column, an approximately 7-nm-wide Ni–Al-rich and Co–Fe-poor region runs parallel to the FCC/BCC interface. Based on these results and APT reconstructions (Fig. S2), the morphology of the FCC and BCC regions can be described as colonies of FCC/L12 nanorods embedded in a BCC/B2 matrix. Within both the FCC/L12 nanorods and the BCC/B2 matrix, there is a finer-scale decomposition into ordered and disordered lamellar regions. A high-resolution HAADF STEM image (Fig. 3(e)) shows a uniform lattice in both grains.
STEM EDXS 谱图成像将 FCC 与 BCC 相的成分变化与 CRSA600 样品中磁畴的位置关联起来。图 3(a) 给出区域 II 的 BF STEM 像及相应的 Co、Fe、Ni、Al 元素图，显示富 Co–Fe 的 BCC 相为基体，其中分布着富 Ni–Al 的 FCC 柱。EDXS 图揭示了富 Ni–Al 的 FCC 柱内的成分起伏，以及沿 FCC/BCC 相界平行方向的额外 Ni 富集。图 3(d) 给出跨界面的 EDXS 线扫描；在 FCC 柱内，一条约 7 nm 宽、富 Ni–Al 而贫 Co–Fe 的区域平行于 FCC/BCC 界面延伸。基于这些结果及 APT 重构（图 S2），FCC 与 BCC 区域的形貌可描述为嵌于 BCC/B2 基体中的 FCC/L12 纳米棒集束。在 FCC/L12 纳米棒和 BCC/B2 基体内部，还存在更细尺度的、分解为有序与无序片层区域的结构。高分辨 HAADF STEM 像（图 3(e)）显示两晶粒中晶格均匀。
The magnetic domains in the CRSA600 sample were further analyzed using off-axis electron holography. To separate the electrostatic and magnetic contributions to the phase shift, pairs of electron holograms were recorded before and after turning the specimen over, using a cartridge-based holder; half of the sum and half of the difference give the electrostatic and magnetic contributions, respectively. The downward-oriented magnetic domains in the FCC+L12 columns contain straight magnetic contour lines, with small variations that may be associated with ordered L12 regions. In contrast, well-defined regions with no magnetic field lines in the BCC+B2 columns indicate weakly magnetic or non-magnetic inclusions; EDXS confirms these correspond to Ni–Al-rich inclusions. The width of the domain walls pinned at grain boundaries was determined to be δw = 35 ± 3 nm (Fig. 4(c)), almost 5 times smaller than in the CRSA sample. The domain wall width scales as δw ~ √(A/Keff), where A is the exchange stiffness and Keff the effective magnetic anisotropy; however, the CRSA600 sample contains several phases with different magnetic properties, and the magnetic domain walls overlap with the Al–Ni-rich enrichment between the FCC and BCC columns.
进一步用离轴电子全息分析 CRSA600 样品中的磁畴。为分离相移中的静电与磁贡献，使用可翻转样品盒在翻转样品前后各记录一对电子全息图；二者之和的一半与之差的一半分别给出静电与磁贡献。FCC+L12 柱中向下取向的磁畴含有平直的磁等值线，其中的小幅变化可能与有序 L12 区域相关。相反，BCC+B2 柱中界限清晰、无磁力线的区域指示弱磁或非磁夹杂；EDXS 证实这些区域对应富 Ni–Al 夹杂。钉扎于晶界处的畴壁宽度测得为 δw = 35 ± 3 nm（图 4(c)），几乎比 CRSA 样品小 5 倍。畴壁宽度满足 δw ~ √(A/Keff)，其中 A 为交换刚度、Keff 为有效磁各向异性；但 CRSA600 样品含有多个磁性不同的相，且磁畴壁与 FCC、BCC 柱之间富 Al–Ni 的富集区相重叠。

4. 讨论4. Discussion

Magnetism in HEAs can be a complex phenomenon, especially in the presence of hierarchical multi-phase microstructures over multiple length scales, which in turn result from competing phase stabilities between solid solution and ordered phases. The possibility to combine transition metals with other elements and different processing parameters provides a wide range of opportunities to tailor the magnetic and functional properties of HEAs. In the present non-equimolar Al0.3CoFeNi CCA, solid-state decomposition induced by thermomechanical treatment results in a eutectoid-like microstructure of FCC+L12 nanorods in a BCC+B2 matrix. Thermomechanical treatment of the CRSA600 sample increases the coercivity by a factor of 64. High spatial resolution magnetic characterization reveals that the average magnetic domain size (Dc) is reduced by an order of magnitude in the CRSA600 sample, as a result of magnetic domain walls at heterophase boundaries separating the FCC and BCC phases. Furthermore, the domain wall width is reduced significantly from 171 ± 5 to 35 ± 3 nm, suggesting that homophase interfaces in the FCC alloy are ineffective at pinning domain walls, leading to low coercivity.
高熵合金中的磁性可能是一种复杂现象，尤其是在存在跨多个尺度的分级多相组织时，而这种组织又源自固溶体相与有序相之间相互竞争的相稳定性。将过渡金属与其他元素组合、并配以不同工艺参数，为调控高熵合金的磁性与功能性能提供了广阔空间。在本文非等摩尔的 Al0.3CoFeNi CCA 中，热机械处理诱导的固态分解形成了类共析组织——BCC+B2 基体中的 FCC+L12 纳米棒。CRSA600 样品的热机械处理使矫顽力增大 64 倍。高空间分辨磁表征揭示，由于磁畴壁位于分隔 FCC、BCC 相的异相界面处，CRSA600 样品的平均磁畴尺寸（Dc）减小了一个数量级。此外，畴壁宽度从 171 ± 5 nm 显著减小到 35 ± 3 nm，说明 FCC 合金中的同相界面对钉扎畴壁无效，导致低矫顽力。
In contrast, in the CRSA600 sample the presence of FCC and BCC phases with different compositions results in different local magnetic properties. In the FCC nanorods, the Ni–Al-rich ordered L12 phase displays straight magnetic field lines with only slight angular variations. In contrast, the Ni–Al-rich ordered B2 regions in the BCC phase strongly affect the magnetic induction, as the density of field lines is reduced at their locations. The saturation magnetization of the two samples is similar, with a slight increase in CRSA600 attributable to the BCC phase. The pinning of domain walls at heterophase FCC/BCC boundaries is influenced by crystallographic and compositional transitions across these boundaries, as well as by Ni and Al enrichment in the FCC phase along them, resulting in the relatively narrow domain wall width of 35 nm.
相比之下，在 CRSA600 样品中，成分不同的 FCC 与 BCC 相带来了不同的局部磁性能。在 FCC 纳米棒中，富 Ni–Al 的有序 L12 相表现出平直、仅有轻微角度变化的磁力线；而 BCC 相中富 Ni–Al 的有序 B2 区域则强烈影响磁感应——其所在位置磁力线密度降低。两样品的饱和磁化强度相近，CRSA600 略有升高，可归因于 BCC 相。异相 FCC/BCC 界面对畴壁的钉扎，受这些界面两侧的晶体学与成分过渡、以及沿界面 FCC 相中 Ni、Al 富集的影响，导致 CRSA600 样品相对较窄的 35 nm 畴壁宽度。
Our observations suggest that the magnetization reversal mechanism responsible for coercivity changes from nucleation-type in the CRSA sample to pinning-type in the CRSA600 sample. To validate this, in situ magnetization reversal experiments were carried out inside the TEM by exciting the objective lens to a pre-calibrated value and tilting the sample between −30° and +30° to apply an effective in-plane field. In the CRSA sample, domain walls nucleate at the specimen edge and move across the field of view without interruption, showing a lack of pinning. In the CRSA600 sample, even at the maximum in-plane field (~600 mT) a saturated state could not be reached; switching events occurred at discrete domain walls as the field increased from 0 to +81 and +133 mT, with the domain wall contrast reversing sign locally. Magnetization switching forms domain walls containing both black and white contrast—so-called Bloch caps in 180° domain walls. These experiments confirm that the reversal process changes from nucleation-type (CRSA) to pinning-type (CRSA600) as a result of phase decomposition after prolonged annealing at 600 °C for 50 h, producing a nanorod microstructure with heterophase FCC/BCC interfaces.
我们的观察表明，决定矫顽力的磁化反转机制从 CRSA 样品的形核型转变为 CRSA600 样品的钉扎型。为验证这一点，在 TEM 内进行了原位磁化反转实验——将物镜励磁到预校准值，并将样品在 −30° 到 +30° 之间倾转以施加有效面内磁场。在 CRSA 样品中，畴壁在样品边缘形核并不受阻地扫过视场，表明缺乏钉扎。在 CRSA600 样品中，即使在最大面内磁场（约 600 mT）下也无法达到饱和态；随磁场从 0 增大到 +81、+133 mT，在离散的畴壁处发生翻转事件，畴壁衬度局部反号。磁化翻转形成了同时含黑白衬度的畴壁——即 180° 畴壁中的所谓布洛赫帽（Bloch cap）。这些实验证实，经 600 °C 长时间退火 50 h 的相分解，形成了带异相 FCC/BCC 界面的纳米棒组织，使反转过程从（CRSA 的）形核型转变为（CRSA600 的）钉扎型。
The present study provides fundamental insight into the role of interphase interfaces on magnetization reversal through domain wall pinning. Heterophase interfaces between phases with different crystal structures (e.g., FCC/BCC) are effective at pinning magnetic domain walls and consequently increase coercivity, particularly when they occur at near-periodic intervals on deep-sub-μm length scales, as in the present nanorod microstructure. In contrast, homophase interfaces of order/disorder type (FCC+L12 or BCC+B2) do not significantly hinder domain wall motion. This insight can guide the design of both high entropy and conventional alloys with desired combinations of magnetic and mechanical properties: for high strength with low coercivity, introducing a large volume fraction of ordered precipitates within a parent solid solution is effective, especially if misfit strain is minimized; conversely, higher coercivity can be produced by a nanorod structure with heterophase boundaries, and uniaxial anisotropy can be tailored via strain- or magnetic-field-induced anisotropy during annealing—analogous to strategies used for Alnico-type and Sm–Co hard magnets.
本研究为界面（相间界面）通过畴壁钉扎对磁化反转的作用提供了基础性认识。晶体结构不同的相之间的异相界面（如 FCC/BCC）能有效钉扎磁畴壁，从而增大矫顽力，尤其是当它们在深亚微米尺度上近乎周期性出现时（如本文的纳米棒组织）。相反，有序/无序型的同相界面（FCC+L12 或 BCC+B2）不会显著阻碍畴壁运动。这一认识可指导设计具有所需磁-力学性能组合的高熵合金与传统合金：若要在低矫顽力下获得高强度，在母相固溶体中引入大体积分数的有序析出相是有效的，尤其是在错配应变最小化时；反之，带异相界面的纳米棒结构可产生更高矫顽力，而单轴各向异性可通过退火过程中应变或磁场诱导的各向异性来定制——这与 Alnico 型和 Sm–Co 硬磁体所用的策略类似。

5. 结论5. Conclusions

Our high spatial resolution investigation of the microstructural and magnetic behavior of an Al0.3CoFeNi complex concentrated alloy has revealed an intricate coupling between the presence of a hierarchical multi-length-scale multi-phase microstructure and the motion of magnetic domain walls. High-resolution in situ techniques show that the magnetization reversal process, which is responsible for coercivity, changes from a nucleation-type mechanism in the FCC+L12 microstructure of this alloy, with a domain wall width of 171 nm, to a pinning-type mechanism in the nanorod microstructure of the same alloy, with a domain wall width of 35 nm. Significantly, heterophase FCC/BCC nanoscale interfaces strongly pin magnetic domain walls, increasing coercivity when compared to homophase chemically ordered/disordered interfaces. These results provide a powerful guide for the rational design of microstructure to tune the magnetic and mechanical properties of both high entropy alloys and more conventional magnetic alloys.
我们对 Al0.3CoFeNi 复杂浓缩合金组织与磁行为的高空间分辨研究，揭示了分级、多尺度、多相组织的存在与磁畴壁运动之间的复杂耦合。高分辨原位技术表明，决定矫顽力的磁化反转过程，从该合金 FCC+L12 组织中的形核型机制（畴壁宽度 171 nm），转变为同一合金纳米棒组织中的钉扎型机制（畴壁宽度 35 nm）。尤为重要的是，异相 FCC/BCC 纳米尺度界面强烈钉扎磁畴壁，相较同相化学有序/无序界面显著增大矫顽力。这些结果为通过合理设计组织来调控高熵合金及更传统磁性合金的磁性与力学性能提供了有力指导。
