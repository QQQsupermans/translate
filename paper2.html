摘要 / Abstract
Next generation high speed, large torque, rotating electrical machines demand materials with an excellent balance of magnetic and mechanical properties. High entropy alloys (HEAs) can exhibit this unique combination of properties, e.g., CoFeNi based HEAs containing Ta. The high Ta content makes these alloys prohibitively expensive for industrial applications. Hence, we develop Ta free HEA using a thermodynamic approach. The addition of Al and Ti to the base CoFeNi alloy is studied. We investigate two compositions: Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 and Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi. In their solution treated and quenched condition these HEAs exhibit a low coercivity Hc - 1.8–2.4 Oe, high saturation magnetization Ms - 96.5-137.3 emu/g, coupled with a high yield strength, YS - 780-850 MPa, ultimate tensile strength, UTS - 1200 MPa, large tensile elongation, ε - 29-35%, and with desired high electrical resistivity ( - 269.5-308.3 μΩ·cm), making them attractive for high frequency electrical machines. Importantly, the latter HEA is further strengthened to a very high YS - 1140-1200 MPa (ε - 18-25%) and electrical resistivity (296.1-459.3 μΩ·cm), but at the cost of higher Hc values - 8.8-48.6 Oe.
下一代高速、大扭矩旋转电机要求材料在磁性能与力学性能之间取得优异平衡。高熵合金（HEA）能够呈现这种独特的性能组合，例如含 Ta 的 CoFeNi 基高熵合金。但高 Ta 含量使这类合金在工业应用中过于昂贵。为此，我们采用热力学方法开发无钽（Ta-free）高熵合金，研究在基础 CoFeNi 合金中添加 Al 和 Ti 的效果。我们考察了两种成分：Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 和 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi。在固溶处理并淬火的状态下，这些高熵合金表现出低矫顽力（Hc 约 1.8–2.4 Oe）、高饱和磁化强度（Ms 约 96.5–137.3 emu/g），同时具有高屈服强度（YS 约 780–850 MPa）、抗拉强度（UTS 约 1200 MPa）、大拉伸延伸率（ε 约 29–35%），以及理想的高电阻率（约 269.5–308.3 μΩ·cm），使其对高频电机颇具吸引力。值得注意的是，后一种高熵合金可进一步强化到很高的 YS（1140–1200 MPa，ε 约 18–25%）和电阻率（296.1–459.3 μΩ·cm），但代价是矫顽力升高（Hc 约 8.8–48.6 Oe）。
Soft magnetic materials (SMMs) play a pivotal role in rotating electrical machines, which underpin a wide range of modern energy conversion and mobility technologies. The rising global emphasis on reducing fossil fuel consumption and greenhouse gas emissions has accelerated the adoption of electric machines in electric vehicles, aircraft, and other technological sectors. According to the U.S. Energy Information Administration's Monthly Energy Review (April 2024), nearly 20% of the electrical energy generated in the U.S. power sector is lost before reaching its end use, underscoring the urgent need for improved efficiency in electrical systems. Enhancing efficiency requires materials that combine optimal magnetic, electrical, and mechanical properties, or materials with tunable multifunctional characteristics, to meet the demands of next-generation motors, e.g., for electric vehicles, designed for large torque, high-frequency operation, and more compact architectures. The essential requirements for SMMs include: (i) low coercivity (Hc) to minimize hysteresis losses during rapid magnetization–demagnetization cycles, (ii) high saturation magnetization (Ms) for maximizing magnetic flux density and torque, and (iii) elevated electrical resistivity (ρ) to reduce eddy current losses. Equally critical are superior mechanical properties, particularly high yield strength to prevent plastic deformation under cyclic stresses and adequate ductility for manufacturing flexibility and damage tolerance. Meeting this complex set of requirements would substantially expand the application envelope of SMMs and reduce energy losses across electrical machines.
软磁材料（SMM）在旋转电机中发挥着关键作用，而旋转电机支撑着现代众多的能量转换与出行技术。全球日益重视减少化石燃料消耗和温室气体排放，加速了电机在电动汽车、飞行器及其他技术领域的应用。据美国能源信息署《月度能源评论》（2024 年 4 月），美国电力部门发出的电能中有近 20% 在到达终端用途之前就已损失，凸显了提升电气系统效率的迫切需求。提高效率需要在磁、电、力学性能上均达到最优的材料，或具有可调多功能特性的材料，以满足下一代电机（如电动汽车用电机）对大扭矩、高频运行和更紧凑结构的要求。软磁材料的基本要求包括：(i) 低矫顽力（Hc），以最大限度减少快速磁化–退磁循环中的磁滞损耗；(ii) 高饱和磁化强度（Ms），以最大化磁通密度和扭矩；(iii) 较高的电阻率（ρ），以降低涡流损耗。同等关键的还有优异的力学性能，尤其是能在循环应力下防止塑性变形的高屈服强度，以及能提供加工灵活性和损伤容限的足够塑性。满足这一复杂的综合要求，将大幅拓展软磁材料的应用范围，并降低各类电机的能量损耗。
Despite decades of progress, simultaneously achieving this balance of properties remains a formidable challenge. Conventional SMMs—including Fe-Si silicon steels (3–6 wt.% Si), Fe–Ni permalloys, Fe–Ni–Mo supermalloys, and Fe–Co alloys such as Hiperco 50 and Hiperco 50HS—are widely used in electrical machines. These alloys exhibit low Hc (0.01–5.0 Oe) and high Ms (64–235 emu/g) values, yet they fall short in mechanical strength (maximum ~624–810 MPa in Hiperco 50HS) and electrical resistivity (27–90 μΩ·cm). Such limitations are particularly acute in high-torque, high-frequency motor applications in automotive and aviation use cases, where premature mechanical failure and excessive eddy current losses are prevalent. Therefore, future progress in electrical machine design hinges on engineering single-composition soft magnetic materials that possess the appropriate magnetic, electrical, and mechanical property set.
尽管已有数十年进展，同时实现上述性能平衡仍是一项艰巨挑战。传统软磁材料——包括 Fe-Si 硅钢（3–6 wt.% Si）、Fe–Ni 坡莫合金、Fe–Ni–Mo 超坡莫合金，以及 Hiperco 50、Hiperco 50HS 等 Fe–Co 合金——被广泛用于电机。这些合金具有低 Hc（0.01–5.0 Oe）和高 Ms（64–235 emu/g），但在力学强度（Hiperco 50HS 最高约 624–810 MPa）和电阻率（27–90 μΩ·cm）方面存在不足。这些局限在汽车和航空领域的高扭矩、高频电机应用中尤为突出，那里普遍存在过早的机械失效和过大的涡流损耗。因此，电机设计的未来进展有赖于开发出兼具适当磁、电、力学性能组合的单一成分软磁材料。
Recent advances in high-entropy alloys (HEAs) and complex concentrated alloys (CCAs) have created new opportunities for designing alloys with enhanced multifunctional properties. By combining multiple principal magnetic elements, HEAs offer a platform to simultaneously tailor magnetic, structural, and mechanical characteristics. The alloying elements and processing conditions are decisive: they influence phase stability, microstructural evolution, and interfacial characteristics, which, in turn, can affect the relevant properties. HEAs containing concentrated amounts of ferromagnetic elements, such as Co, Fe, and Ni, can exhibit promising soft magnetic properties. Further, the addition of atoms such as Al and Ti, which exhibit large negative enthalpies of mixing with these ferromagnetic elements, promotes chemical ordering within face-centered cubic (FCC) solid solutions, often through the formation of intermetallic nanoprecipitates such as the L12 or the B2 phases.
高熵合金（HEA）和复杂浓缩合金（CCA）的最新进展，为设计具有增强多功能性能的合金创造了新机遇。通过组合多种主量级磁性元素，高熵合金提供了同时调控磁、结构和力学特性的平台。合金元素与工艺条件起决定作用：它们影响相稳定性、组织演化和界面特征，进而影响相关性能。含有浓缩铁磁性元素（如 Co、Fe、Ni）的高熵合金可表现出优良的软磁性能。此外，加入 Al、Ti 等与这些铁磁性元素具有较大负混合焓的原子，会促进面心立方（FCC）固溶体内的化学有序化，通常通过形成 L12 或 B2 相等金属间化合物纳米析出相来实现。
The ternary CoFeNi alloy has emerged as a promising base for soft magnetic HEAs. It exhibits excellent magnetic properties, and the interaction energies of the three binary pairs (Fe–Ni, Fe–Co, Co–Ni) are nearly zero, yielding a near-ideal random solid solution. Equiatomic CoFeNi forms a single-phase FCC structure, which offers excellent ductility but insufficient mechanical strength. Various strengthening mechanisms have been applied, including solid solution strengthening, grain refinement, dislocation strengthening, and multiphase strengthening. Of these, nanoprecipitation strengthening via coherent L12-type precipitates is especially effective, as established in Ni-based superalloys (γ + γ′ structure), Co-based alloys (e.g., Co84Ti8V8), and Fe-based systems. Building on this principle, Al or Ti additions to CoFeNi can induce coherent L12 nanoprecipitates in the FCC matrix, yielding significant strengthening without severely compromising ductility. For instance, Han et al. reported a CoFeNi–Ta/Al HEA containing >42 vol.% ordered L12 nanoprecipitates, which achieved a high yield strength (~1202 MPa) and good tensile ductility (~15%), but exhibited relatively high coercivity (~10.7 Oe). Similarly, Xu et al. studied (FeCoNi)90–TixAlx alloys, with (FeCoNi)90–Ti5Al5 composition as optimal. This alloy contained <1 nm nanoprecipitates with ~9.8% volume fraction, yielding a balanced property set: YS = 463 MPa, ductility = 50.7%, Ms = 121.1 emu/g, and Hc = 6.5 Oe.
三元 CoFeNi 合金已成为软磁高熵合金颇具前景的基体。它具有优异的磁性能，且三个二元对（Fe–Ni、Fe–Co、Co–Ni）的相互作用能几乎为零，形成近乎理想的随机固溶体。等原子比 CoFeNi 形成单相 FCC 结构，塑性极佳但力学强度不足。人们已应用多种强化机制，包括固溶强化、晶粒细化、位错强化和多相强化。其中，通过共格 L12 型析出相实现的纳米析出强化尤为有效，这一点在镍基高温合金（γ + γ′ 结构）、钴基合金（如 Co84Ti8V8）和铁基体系中都已确立。基于这一原理，向 CoFeNi 中添加 Al 或 Ti 可在 FCC 基体中诱导出共格的 L12 纳米析出相，在不严重损害塑性的前提下带来显著强化。例如，Han 等报道了一种含 >42 vol.% 有序 L12 纳米析出相的 CoFeNi–Ta/Al 高熵合金，实现了高屈服强度（约 1202 MPa）和良好拉伸塑性（约 15%），但矫顽力相对较高（约 10.7 Oe）。类似地，Xu 等研究了 (FeCoNi)90–TixAlx 合金，以 (FeCoNi)90–Ti5Al5 成分为最优。该合金含有体积分数约 9.8%、尺寸 <1 nm 的纳米析出相，性能均衡：YS = 463 MPa、塑性 = 50.7%、Ms = 121.1 emu/g、Hc = 6.5 Oe。
Phase stability in Co- and Ni-based HEAs is highly sensitive to Al and Ti content. Prior work on Al-containing CoCrFeNi CCAs revealed compositional transitions from FCC/L12 to FCC/L12 + BCC/B2, and finally to BCC/B2 with increasing Al content. In FCC-based HEAs, Al often stabilizes metastable/stable L12 and/or B2 nanoprecipitates. The simultaneous addition of Ti has proven effective in stabilizing and increasing the mass fraction of the L12 phase. For example, a CoCrFeNi base alloy with 4 at.% Al + 2 at.% Ti achieved strength ≥1 GPa and ductility ~17%, while a CoFeNi base alloy with 8.5 at.% Al + 5.7 at.% Ti reached even higher ductility (~50%) alongside excellent strength.
Co 基和 Ni 基高熵合金的相稳定性对 Al、Ti 含量高度敏感。此前对含 Al 的 CoCrFeNi 复杂浓缩合金的研究表明，随 Al 含量增加，成分演化依次经历 FCC/L12 → FCC/L12 + BCC/B2，最终到 BCC/B2。在 FCC 基高熵合金中，Al 常常稳定亚稳/稳定的 L12 和/或 B2 纳米析出相。同时加入 Ti 已被证明能有效稳定并提高 L12 相的质量分数。例如，添加 4 at.% Al + 2 at.% Ti 的 CoCrFeNi 基合金实现了 ≥1 GPa 的强度和约 17% 的塑性；而添加 8.5 at.% Al + 5.7 at.% Ti 的 CoFeNi 基合金则在优异强度之外达到了更高的塑性（约 50%）。
Prior investigations into an Al0.3CoFeNi HEA underscored the pivotal role of Al in inducing ordered tendencies. This alloy exhibits a combination of long-range ordered (LRO) phases and a disordered FCC solid solution. At 500 °C, L12 nanoprecipitates enhanced hardness and tensile yield stress, while further thermo-mechanical processing yielded a hierarchical four-phase microstructure (FCC + L12 + BCC + B2). Depending on the morphology, this complex microstructure delivered exceptional mechanical performance, with the microstructure with multi-scale hierarchical precipitates achieving yield stresses up to ~1490 MPa, ultimate tensile strengths of ~1663 MPa, and ductility of ~12%. However, these mechanical gains were offset by high coercivity (~160 Oe), presenting an opportunity for further microstructure engineering and alloy development.
此前对 Al0.3CoFeNi 高熵合金的研究强调了 Al 在诱导有序化倾向中的关键作用。该合金呈现长程有序（LRO）相与无序 FCC 固溶体的组合。在 500 °C 时，L12 纳米析出相提高了硬度和拉伸屈服应力；进一步的热机械加工则产生了分级的四相组织（FCC + L12 + BCC + B2）。视形貌不同，这种复杂组织带来了出众的力学性能——具有多尺度分级析出相的组织实现了高达约 1490 MPa 的屈服应力、约 1663 MPa 的抗拉强度和约 12% 的塑性。然而，这些力学增益被高矫顽力（约 160 Oe）所抵消，这也为进一步的组织工程和合金开发留下了空间。
These findings highlight the central challenge: while introducing ordered nanoprecipitates significantly enhances strength, it often increases coercivity. Achieving a low-coercivity, high-strength microstructure thus requires careful stabilization of nanoscale L12 phases while avoiding large mass fractions of the magnetically deleterious B2 phase or coarse lamellar morphologies.
这些发现凸显了核心难题：引入有序纳米析出相在显著提高强度的同时，往往也会增大矫顽力。因此，要获得低矫顽力、高强度的组织，就必须精心稳定纳米级 L12 相，同时避免出现大量对磁性有害的 B2 相或粗大的片层形貌。
In this study, we build upon the previous work on CoFeNi and Al0.3CoFeNi alloys by introducing Ti as an additional alloying element to enhance the stability and volume fraction of ordered L12 nanoprecipitates within an FCC matrix. Solution thermodynamic modeling guided our alloy design. Our central hypothesis is that increasing the fraction of fully coherent nanoscale L12 precipitates will simultaneously provide high yield strength and preserve low coercivity. As established in superalloy design, Ti is a potent L12 stabilizer, and we hypothesized that its addition to CoFeNi and Al0.3CoFeNi-based alloys would yield HEAs with a unique combination of properties: high strength, high ductility, low coercivity, high saturation magnetization, and elevated electrical resistivity. Such alloys would represent a significant advance toward next-generation SMMs for high-frequency, high-torque electrical machines.
本研究在此前 CoFeNi 和 Al0.3CoFeNi 合金工作的基础上，引入 Ti 作为额外的合金元素，以增强 FCC 基体内有序 L12 纳米析出相的稳定性和体积分数。溶液热力学建模为我们的合金设计提供指导。我们的核心假设是：提高完全共格的纳米级 L12 析出相的比例，可同时提供高屈服强度并保持低矫顽力。正如高温合金设计中所确立的，Ti 是强效的 L12 稳定剂；我们据此假设，将其加入 CoFeNi 和 Al0.3CoFeNi 基合金，可获得具有独特性能组合的高熵合金：高强度、高塑性、低矫顽力、高饱和磁化强度和较高电阻率。此类合金将代表着面向高频、高扭矩电机的下一代软磁材料的重要进步。

结果 —— 热力学建模Results — Thermodynamic modeling

Pseudo-binary sections of the TixAl0.3CoFeNi and Ti–CoFeNi systems, calculated using Thermo-Calc ® with the TCHEA6 database, are shown in Figs. 1a and 1b, respectively. In both cases, alloy compositions were chosen to maximize Ti incorporation while preserving a stable FCC single-phase field at high temperatures: Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi (Al8.5Ti5.7Co28.5Fe28.5Ni28.5 in atom %) for the quinary system and Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 (Ti8Co33Fe33Ni26 in atom %) for the quaternary system. The corresponding phase fraction diagrams (Figs. 1b, d) indicate that both alloys transform from a high-temperature FCC phase into multiphase fields containing ordered intermetallics upon cooling. Specifically, the Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi alloy is predicted to exhibit FCC + L12 + B2 stability between 800 and 1150 °C, while the Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 alloy is expected to form FCC + L12 phases within this temperature range.
图 1a 和 1b 分别给出了用 Thermo-Calc ® 配合 TCHEA6 数据库计算的 TixAl0.3CoFeNi 与 Ti–CoFeNi 体系的伪二元截面。两种情形下，合金成分的选取都以在保持高温稳定 FCC 单相区的前提下最大化 Ti 的加入量为原则：五元体系取 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi（原子分数 Al8.5Ti5.7Co28.5Fe28.5Ni28.5），四元体系取 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78（原子分数 Ti8Co33Fe33Ni26）。相应的相分数图（图 1b、d）表明，两种合金在冷却时都从高温 FCC 相转变为含有序金属间化合物的多相区。具体而言，预测 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 合金在 800–1150 °C 之间稳定为 FCC + L12 + B2；而 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 合金在此温度区间内预期形成 FCC + L12 相。
To experimentally validate these predictions, homogenized and cold-rolled (90%) samples of both alloys were solutionized in the high-temperature FCC field (1200 °C for Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi, 1000 °C for Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78) and water-quenched. Subsequent annealing treatments were performed within the predicted multiphase regions: the Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi alloy was annealed at 800 °C for 4 h after solutionizing or, alternatively, annealed directly at 900 °C for 4 h from the cold-rolled state; the Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 alloy was annealed at 1000 °C for 15 min. These conditions were selected to promote the evolution of ordered L12 and B2 phases from the disordered FCC matrix.
为在实验上验证这些预测，将两种合金经均匀化并冷轧（90%）的样品在高温 FCC 相区固溶（Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 为 1200 °C，Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 为 1000 °C）后水淬。随后在预测的多相区内进行退火处理：Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 合金在固溶后于 800 °C 退火 4 h，或从冷轧态直接在 900 °C 退火 4 h；Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 合金在 1000 °C 退火 15 min。选取这些条件旨在促进有序 L12 和 B2 相从无序 FCC 基体中的演化。

拉伸性能Tensile properties

The tensile behavior of Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 and Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi HEAs at room temperature under three different conditions is summarized in Fig. 2a. In the solutionized and water-quenched state (CRSA), the Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 alloy exhibited a yield strength (YS) of ~850 MPa, ultimate tensile strength (UTS) of ~1200 MPa, and elongation to failure of 29%. Under the same condition, the Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi alloy showed slightly lower strength (YS ~ 780 MPa, UTS - 1110 MPa) but superior ductility (35%). Subsequent isothermal annealing of the solutionized Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi alloy at 800 °C for 4 h (CRSA800) markedly enhanced strength, with YS ~ 1200 MPa and UTS ~ 1420 MPa, while maintaining 25% ductility. In the cold-rolled (90%) plus directly annealed (900 °C/4 h) condition (CR900), the alloy displayed YS ~ 1140 MPa, UTS ~ 1500 MPa, and 18% elongation.
图 2a 汇总了 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 和 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 高熵合金在三种不同状态下的室温拉伸行为。在固溶并水淬的状态（CRSA）下，Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 合金的屈服强度（YS）约 850 MPa，抗拉强度（UTS）约 1200 MPa，断裂延伸率 29%。在相同状态下，Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 合金强度略低（YS 约 780 MPa，UTS 约 1110 MPa），但塑性更佳（35%）。随后将固溶态 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 合金在 800 °C 等温退火 4 h（CRSA800），强度显著提升，YS 约 1200 MPa、UTS 约 1420 MPa，同时保持 25% 的塑性。在冷轧（90%）加直接退火（900 °C/4 h）状态（CR900）下，合金呈现 YS 约 1140 MPa、UTS 约 1500 MPa、延伸率 18%。
For context, the tensile curve of a solutionized CoFeNi alloy is also plotted in Fig. 2a. The comparison highlights the substantial strengthening imparted by Al and Ti additions. Relative to solutionized CoFeNi, the Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi HEA in the CRSA state exhibits nearly a threefold increase in YS and a twofold increase in UTS. Remarkably, this strengthening was achieved without significant loss of ductility, pointing to a modified deformation mechanism that mitigates the typical strength–ductility trade-off. Prior studies have shown that while CoFeNi deforms through homogeneous dislocation-mediated plasticity, the incorporation of Al and Ti in 3d transition-metal HEAs promotes planar slip, enabling sustained work hardening and enhanced ductility.
作为参照，图 2a 还绘出了固溶态 CoFeNi 合金的拉伸曲线。对比凸显了 Al、Ti 添加所带来的显著强化。相对于固溶态 CoFeNi，CRSA 态的 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 高熵合金 YS 提高近三倍、UTS 提高约两倍。值得注意的是，这种强化并未伴随明显的塑性损失，说明存在一种改变了的变形机制，缓解了典型的强度–塑性权衡。此前研究表明，CoFeNi 通过均匀的位错主导塑性变形，而在 3d 过渡金属高熵合金中加入 Al 和 Ti 会促进平面滑移，从而实现持续的加工硬化和增强的塑性。
The corresponding work-hardening responses for CoFeNi, Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78, and the three conditions of Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi (CRSA, CRSA800, CR900) are presented in Fig. 2b, further illustrating the role of compositional and processing modifications in tailoring the strength–ductility balance.

To further tailor the microstructure, the CR900 route was applied: following homogenization at 1200 °C, the alloy was cold-rolled to 90% thickness reduction and directly annealed at 900 °C for 4 h. According to phase stability calculations, this composition is predicted to form a stable three-phase microstructure (FCC, B2, L12). However, as established for FCC-based HEAs, B2 nucleation is hindered by its high nucleation barrier, whereas ordered L12 nucleates more readily due to the low FCC/L12 interfacial energy. Consequently, B2 precipitates form only heterogeneously at defects such as grain boundaries and triple junctions, unlike the more uniformly distributed L12 precipitates. A low-magnification SEM image (Fig. 5a) with EBSD phase and IPF maps (Fig. 5b, c) reveal a refined FCC grain size of ~5 μm—a substantial reduction from the ~73 μm of the homogenized+quenched condition. 为进一步调控组织，采用 CR900 路线：在 1200 °C 均匀化后，将合金冷轧至 90% 减薄，并直接在 900 °C 退火 4 h。根据相稳定性计算，预测该成分形成稳定的三相组织（FCC、B2、L12）。然而，正如 FCC 基高熵合金所确立的，B2 因形核势垒高而形核受阻，而有序 L12 因 FCC/L12 界面能低而更易形核。因此，与分布较均匀的 L12 析出相不同，B2 析出相只在晶界、三叉点等缺陷处非均匀形核。低倍 SEM 像（图 5a）配合 EBSD 相图和 IPF 图（图 5b、c）显示 FCC 晶粒细化至约 5 μm——相较均匀化+淬火态的约 73 μm 大幅减小。次生的 BCC/B2 析出相（约 1 μm，面积分数约 5%）优先装点于 FCC 晶界和三叉点处。

TEM (Fig. 6) verified B2 ordering via (010) superlattice reflections in the [001] BCC diffraction pattern, with EDS maps showing strong Al partitioning to the B2 phase, Ti enrichment, and Fe depletion. A [112] FCC-zone SAED pattern (Fig. 7a) displayed (110)-type superlattice reflections indicative of L12 ordering; APT (Fig. 7b) identified Ni-rich L12 precipitates (number density 3.4 × 10²³/m). Proximity histograms gave an FCC matrix of ~7.1Al–3.7Ti–31.6Co–37.4Fe–20.1Ni (at.%) and L12 precipitates of ~8.7Al–15.7Ti–24.6Co–9.9Fe–41.1Ni (at.%).
更高倍的 SEM-EBSD（图 5d–f）证实，这些与晶界相关的析出相被标定为 BCC，与 B2 有序一致；而更细弥散的晶内析出相虽标定为 FCC，但很可能是 L12 有序相（EBSD 无法区分有序与无序 FCC）。TEM（图 6）通过 [001] BCC 衍射花样中的 (010) 超晶格衍射斑证实了 B2 有序，EDS 图显示 Al 强烈分配到 B2 相、Ti 富集、Fe 贫化。沿 [112] FCC 晶带轴的 SAED 花样（图 7a）呈现 (110) 型超晶格衍射斑，指示 L12 有序；APT（图 7b）识别出富 Ni 的 L12 析出相（数量密度 3.4 × 10²³/m）。邻近直方图给出 FCC 基体约为 7.1Al–3.7Ti–31.6Co–37.4Fe–20.1Ni（原子分数），L12 析出相约为 8.7Al–15.7Ti–24.6Co–9.9Fe–41.1Ni（原子分数）。

讨论Discussion

The present study demonstrates a novel strategy for the design of high-strength, soft magnetic, and high resistivity HEAs by carefully tuning composition and processing pathways to control nanoscale precipitation behavior. Specifically, Ti and Al additions to the equiatomic CoFeNi alloy promote the formation of ordered L12 precipitates within an FCC matrix, thereby achieving a remarkable balance between strength, ductility, electrical resistivity, and soft magnetic properties.
本研究展示了一种设计高强度、软磁、高电阻率高熵合金的新策略——通过精心调控成分与工艺路径来控制纳米尺度析出行为。具体而言，向等原子比 CoFeNi 合金中加入 Ti 和 Al，可促进 FCC 基体内有序 L12 析出相的形成，从而在强度、塑性、电阻率和软磁性能之间取得显著平衡。
Two distinct HEA compositions were designed—Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi and Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78—to exploit the effectiveness of ordered L12 precipitates in enhancing mechanical properties while maintaining soft magnetic behavior. The formation of ordered L12 precipitates in the random solid-solution FCC parent matrix occurs via a nucleation and growth process. At the earliest transformation, nanometer-scale ordered L12 domains nucleate within the parent FCC matrix. These domains are likely fully coherent with the FCC matrix and thus show no substantial lattice-parameter difference; they are early stages of the second phase with a distinctly different composition and chemical ordering. This corresponds to the CRSA condition for both HEAs and is manifested as superlattice reflections in electron diffraction (Figs. 3a, 8d) and compositional differences in APT (Figs. 3e, f, 8e, f). However, XRD patterns under CRSA show no discernible splitting of the FCC peaks, because the lattice parameters of FCC and L12 are not substantially different at this early stage. Upon further annealing (CRSA800, CR900 of Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi), the ordered L12 precipitates grow and coarsen, causing lattice-parameter differences and eventual loss of coherency, so splits appear in the XRD peaks.
本研究设计了两种不同的高熵合金成分——Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 和 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78——以利用有序 L12 析出相在提升力学性能同时保持软磁行为方面的有效性。有序 L12 析出相在随机固溶体 FCC 母相基体中的形成，通过形核与长大过程进行。在最早期的转变中，纳米级有序 L12 畴在母相 FCC 基体内形核。这些畴很可能与 FCC 基体完全共格，因而不表现出明显的晶格常数差异；它们是第二相的早期阶段，具有与母相明显不同的成分和化学有序。这对应于两种高熵合金的 CRSA 状态，表现为电子衍射中的超晶格衍射斑（图 3a、8d）和 APT 中的成分差异（图 3e、f、8e、f）。然而，CRSA 状态下的 XRD 图谱未见 FCC 峰的明显劈裂，因为在此早期阶段 FCC 与 L12 的晶格常数差异不大。进一步退火后（Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 的 CRSA800、CR900），有序 L12 析出相长大并粗化，导致晶格常数出现差异并最终丧失共格，因而 XRD 峰出现劈裂。

析出相主导的强化Precipitation-mediated strengthening

The precipitation of nanoscale L12 phases is central to the superior mechanical and physical performance of the investigated alloys. In the Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 alloy, the formation of ultrafine (~4.0 nm), uniformly distributed L12 precipitates at extremely high number densities (6.29 × 10²³/m³) provides significant strengthening by interacting with dislocations during plastic deformation. Despite such dense precipitation, the small precipitate size ensures minimal pinning of magnetic domain walls, resulting in very low coercivity (~1.8 Oe). The concurrent enhancement in yield strength (YS - 850 MPa), UTS (~1200 MPa), ductility (~29%), and resistivity (308.3 μΩ·cm), while retaining a high saturation magnetization (137.3 emu/g), positions this alloy among the best-performing soft magnetic alloys reported to date. By contrast, in the Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi alloy, larger L12 precipitates (~11.5 nm) form at lower number densities (1.24 × 10²³/m³). While the mechanical response remains impressive (YS - 780 MPa, UTS - 1110 MPa, elongation ~35%), the saturation magnetization decreases substantially (~96.5 emu/g) due to compositional partitioning, and the coercivity increases modestly (~2.4 Oe).
纳米级 L12 相的析出是所研究合金优异力学与物理性能的核心。在 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 合金中，形成超细（约 4.0 nm）、均匀分布、数量密度极高（6.29 × 10²³/m³）的 L12 析出相，通过在塑性变形中与位错相互作用提供显著强化。尽管析出如此密集，但小的析出相尺寸确保对磁畴壁的钉扎极小，从而带来很低的矫顽力（约 1.8 Oe）。在保持高饱和磁化强度（137.3 emu/g）的同时，屈服强度（YS 约 850 MPa）、UTS（约 1200 MPa）、塑性（约 29%）和电阻率（308.3 μΩ·cm）同步提升，使该合金跻身迄今报道的性能最佳软磁合金之列。相比之下，Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 合金中形成的 L12 析出相更大（约 11.5 nm）、数量密度更低（1.24 × 10²³/m³）。虽然其力学响应依旧出色（YS 约 780 MPa，UTS 约 1110 MPa，延伸率约 35%），但由于成分分配，饱和磁化强度明显下降（约 96.5 emu/g），矫顽力小幅升高（约 2.4 Oe）。
The L12 structure is an ordered FCC superlattice, analogous to the γ′ Ni3(Al, Ti) phase in Ni-based superalloys. The low lattice misfit between coherent γ′ precipitates and the disordered FCC matrix minimizes interfacial energy, yielding a low nucleation barrier and homogeneous nucleation, as observed here. γ′ precipitates strengthen the alloy by impeding dislocation motion via two mechanisms: (i) dislocation shearing—smaller precipitates (<50 nm) are sheared, impeded by anti-phase boundary formation which requires energy; (ii) Orowan bowing—for larger precipitates (>50 nm), dislocations bend and bypass them. Using Orowan's simplified model, YS ∝ √(Nv·D), where Nv is the number density and D the diameter: Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 has a higher number density and smaller diameter than Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi in the CRSA condition, which leads to its higher YS.
L12 结构是一种有序的 FCC 超晶格，类似于镍基高温合金中的 γ′ Ni3(Al, Ti) 相。共格 γ′ 析出相与无序 FCC 基体之间的低晶格错配使界面能最小化，从而形核势垒低、可均匀形核，正如本文所观察到的。γ′ 析出相通过两种机制阻碍位错运动来强化合金：(i) 位错剪切——较小的析出相（<50 nm）被剪切，而剪切过程中形成反相畴界需要能量，从而受阻；(ii) Orowan 弓弯——对较大的析出相（>50 nm），位错弯曲并绕过它们。用 Orowan 简化模型，YS ∝ √(Nv·D)，其中 Nv 为数量密度、D 为直径：在 CRSA 状态下，Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 的 L12 析出相数量密度更高、直径更小（相对 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi），因而屈服强度更高。

组织演化及其对磁、电性能的影响Microstructural evolution and its impact on magnetic/electrical properties

Annealing treatments further reveal how precipitate coarsening alters property balance. Growth of L12 precipitates to ~27 nm in the CRSA800 condition of Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi dramatically increases strength (YS ~ 1200 MPa) but also raises coercivity nearly 20-fold (~48.6 Oe) due to domain wall pinning. This illustrates the trade-off between mechanical properties and magnetic softness: coarser precipitates act as effective barriers to dislocation motion but simultaneously increase magnetostatic energy and coercivity, impede domain wall mobility, and compromise soft magnetic performance.
退火处理进一步揭示了析出相粗化如何改变性能平衡。在 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 的 CRSA800 状态下，L12 析出相长大到约 27 nm，使强度大幅提高（YS 约 1200 MPa），但也因畴壁钉扎使矫顽力升高近 20 倍（约 48.6 Oe）。这体现了力学性能与磁软性之间的权衡：更粗的析出相能有效阻碍位错运动，但同时会增大静磁能和矫顽力、阻碍畴壁移动，损害软磁性能。
Thermo-Calc predictions and experiments provide additional insight. At higher annealing temperatures, the alloy stabilizes a minor fraction of B2 precipitates, nucleating heterogeneously at grain boundaries and triple junctions. These coarse precipitates alter solute partitioning and reduce supersaturation within FCC grains, leading to fewer intragranular L12 precipitates. This is evident in CR900 of Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi, where direct annealing after cold rolling produces a three-phase microstructure: fine FCC grains (~5 μm), ~5% B2 precipitates (~1 μm), and a small fraction of coarse L12 precipitates (~30–50 nm). This yields a superior combination: high YS (~1140 MPa), UTS (~1500 MPa), low coercivity (~8.8 Oe), and moderate Ms (~107.6 emu/g). Although strength is comparable to CRSA800, the coercivity is nearly six times lower, underscoring the critical role of microstructural pathway engineering. The enhanced resistivity of these alloys arises from high inelastic electron scattering in the distorted lattice due to nanoscale precipitates; the CRSA Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 alloy shows slightly higher resistivity than CRSA Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi owing to its higher L12 number density (more scattering sites).
Thermo-Calc 预测与实验提供了进一步的洞见。在较高退火温度下，合金稳定出少量 B2 析出相，在晶界和三叉点处非均匀形核。这些粗大析出相改变溶质分配、降低 FCC 晶内的过饱和度，从而使晶内 L12 析出相减少。这在 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 的 CR900 中很明显——冷轧后直接退火产生三相组织：细 FCC 晶粒（约 5 μm）、约 5% 的 B2 析出相（约 1 μm）和少量粗大 L12 析出相（约 30–50 nm）。由此获得优异组合：高 YS（约 1140 MPa）、UTS（约 1500 MPa）、低矫顽力（约 8.8 Oe）和中等 Ms（约 107.6 emu/g）。尽管强度与 CRSA800 相当，矫顽力却低近六倍，凸显了组织路径工程的关键作用。这些合金电阻率的提升源于纳米析出相造成的畸变晶格中较强的非弹性电子散射；CRSA 态 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 的电阻率略高于 CRSA 态 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi，是因为前者 L12 数量密度更高（散射位点更多）。

强化机制与磁畴相互作用Mechanistic insights & magnetic domain interactions

The contrasting strengthening mechanisms in CRSA800 and CR900 show how different microstructural features govern deformation resistance. In CRSA800, strengthening arises from a dense population of coherent, shearable L12 precipitates (~27 nm), akin to γ′ in Ni/Co-based superalloys—cutting coherent ordered precipitates contributes to both strength and ductility. In CR900, strengthening is dominated by fine FCC grain size (~5 μm, Hall–Petch), with secondary contributions from coarse B2 boundary precipitates and a small fraction of intragranular L12. Both achieve high strength, but their differing impacts on coercivity reveal distinct trade-offs: precipitate shearing versus grain-boundary strengthening carry different penalties for soft magnetic properties.
CRSA800 与 CR900 中截然不同的强化机制，展示了不同组织特征如何主导变形抗力。在 CRSA800 中，强化来自密集的共格、可剪切 L12 析出相（约 27 nm），类似镍/钴基高温合金中的 γ′——切过共格有序析出相同时贡献强度和塑性。在 CR900 中，强化以细小 FCC 晶粒（约 5 μm，Hall–Petch）为主导，粗大 B2 晶界析出相和少量晶内 L12 提供次要贡献。两者都能达到高强度，但它们对矫顽力的不同影响揭示了不同的权衡：析出相剪切与晶界强化对软磁性能带来的"代价"各不相同。
The results also clarify how nanoscale precipitates influence magnetic softness. In CRSA Al0.3CoFeNi, L12 precipitates average ~11.5 nm, far smaller than the ~171 nm domain wall width reported for such FCC–L12 microstructures (Kovács et al.), so domain wall–precipitate interactions are minimized and coercivity stays low. The same argument explains the exceptionally low coercivity (1.8 Oe) of CRSA Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78, whose L12 precipitates are even finer (~4.0 nm). In contrast, coarsening to ~27 nm (CRSA800) produces stronger magnetostatic interactions and coherency stresses, pinning domain walls and drastically raising coercivity—more severe still because the domain wall width narrows (~35 nm) in FCC + L12 + BCC/B2 microstructures. CR900 is intermediate: grain boundaries contribute some pinning, but the absence of a high density of coarsened intragranular L12 prevents a severe coercivity increase.
这些结果还阐明了纳米析出相如何影响磁软性。在 CRSA 态 Al0.3CoFeNi 中，L12 析出相平均约 11.5 nm，远小于此类 FCC–L12 组织中报道的约 171 nm 畴壁宽度（Kovács 等），因此畴壁–析出相相互作用被最小化，矫顽力保持很低。同样的论据可解释 CRSA 态 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 极低的矫顽力（1.8 Oe），其 L12 析出相更为细小（约 4.0 nm）。相反，粗化至约 27 nm（CRSA800）会产生更强的静磁相互作用和共格应力，钉扎畴壁并大幅提高矫顽力——由于 FCC + L12 + BCC/B2 组织中畴壁宽度变窄（约 35 nm），这一效应更为严重。CR900 居于两者之间：晶界贡献一定钉扎，但由于不存在高密度的粗化晶内 L12，矫顽力未出现严重增大。

与其他合金对比、应用与可持续性Comparison, applications & sustainability

Benchmarking against commercial and literature soft magnetic alloys reveals the unique positioning of these Ta-free HEAs. Conventional alloys (Si steel, Ni–Fe permalloy, Ni–Fe–Mo supermalloy, Fe–Co–V Hiperco) exhibit low coercivity and high Ms but suffer from low strength and poor resistivity; e.g., Hiperco 50HS achieves YS ~415–624 MPa with ~10% ductility, but its resistivity (27–80 μΩ·cm) and coercivity (~1.8–8.0 Oe) are inadequate for emerging high-frequency, high-speed applications. Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 offers YS ~40% higher plus fourfold higher resistivity while keeping coercivity well below Hiperco. Recent Ta-containing CoFeNi HEAs reached YS up to 1930 MPa with low coercivity (0.97–10.7 Oe) and moderate Ms (~100–115 emu/g), but their resistivity (~106 μΩ·cm) and density are lower than desired, and Ta raises cost and limits scalability. In contrast, CRSA Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 combines a high specific YS (~105.5 kN·m/kg), low Hc (1.8 Oe), high Ms (137.3 emu/g), and exceptional resistivity (308.3 μΩ·cm); CR900 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi delivers even higher strength (YS ~1140 MPa, UTS ~1500 MPa) with low coercivity (8.8 Oe) and good ductility (18%).
与商用及文献报道的软磁合金进行基准对比，揭示了这些无钽高熵合金的独特定位。传统合金（硅钢、Ni–Fe 坡莫合金、Ni–Fe–Mo 超坡莫合金、Fe–Co–V Hiperco）具有低矫顽力和高 Ms，但强度低、电阻率差；例如 Hiperco 50HS 实现 YS 约 415–624 MPa、塑性约 10%，但其电阻率（27–80 μΩ·cm）和矫顽力（约 1.8–8.0 Oe）不足以应对新兴的高频、高速应用。Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 的 YS 高约 40%，电阻率高四倍，同时矫顽力远低于 Hiperco。近期含 Ta 的 CoFeNi 高熵合金 YS 高达 1930 MPa，矫顽力低（0.97–10.7 Oe）、Ms 中等（约 100–115 emu/g），但电阻率（约 106 μΩ·cm）和密度偏低，且 Ta 抬高成本、限制可规模化。相比之下，CRSA 态 Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 兼具高比屈服强度（约 105.5 kN·m/kg）、低 Hc（1.8 Oe）、高 Ms（137.3 emu/g）和出众的电阻率（308.3 μΩ·cm）；CR900 态 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 则提供更高强度（YS 约 1140 MPa，UTS 约 1500 MPa），且矫顽力低（8.8 Oe）、塑性良好（18%）。
These properties align with requirements for next-generation high-speed machines, flywheel energy storage, and advanced electromechanical devices. In high-speed motors, higher rotational velocity raises centrifugal stresses that demand higher yield strength in rotor laminations; Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78's YS ~850 MPa allows stable operation at higher rpm and power densities. High resistivity (>300 μΩ·cm) inhibits eddy-current losses—nearly an order-of-magnitude improvement over conventional alloys—reducing heat and improving efficiency; combined with low Hc and high Ms, hysteresis is minimized and induction improved. Ductility of 18–35% enables forming into complex shapes and damage tolerance, rarely met simultaneously with high strength and soft magnetic behavior. On sustainability: eliminating expensive, geopolitically constrained Ta reduces cost, supply risk, and embodied energy, while ~10% lower density reduces component mass. Although more expensive than conventional Fe–Si SMMs, the better strength, ductility, and resistivity are expected to extend service life and offset the higher processing cost.
这些性能契合下一代高速电机、飞轮储能和先进机电装置的要求。在高速电机中，更高的转速会增大离心应力，要求转子叠片具有更高的屈服强度；Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 约 850 MPa 的 YS 使其能在更高转速和功率密度下稳定运行。高电阻率（>300 μΩ·cm）抑制涡流损耗——相比传统合金提升近一个数量级——减少发热、提高效率；结合低 Hc 和高 Ms，磁滞被最小化、磁感应得到改善。18–35% 的塑性使其能成形为复杂形状并具有损伤容限，这在高强度与软磁行为同时具备的情况下十分罕见。在可持续性方面：去除昂贵且受地缘政治制约的 Ta，可降低成本、供应风险和隐含能耗，而约 10% 更低的密度可减轻部件质量。尽管比传统 Fe–Si 软磁材料更昂贵，但更好的强度、塑性和电阻率预期能延长服役寿命，抵消更高的加工成本。

两种合金的性能小结Summary of the two alloys

This study establishes a robust design pathway for high-performance soft magnetic HEAs through precipitation-mediated strengthening while retaining low Hc, appreciable Ms, and high resistivity in Ta-free CoFeNi-based systems. By judiciously adding Ti and Al, nanoscale ordering is harnessed to introduce a dense population of ultrafine L12 precipitates; if their length scale and distribution are appropriately controlled, mechanical strength and electrical resistivity are simultaneously enhanced while soft magnetic behavior is preserved. Two CoFeNi-based HEAs were designed: Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 and quinary Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi.
本研究确立了一条稳健的高性能软磁高熵合金设计路径——在无钽 CoFeNi 基体系中，通过析出相主导的强化，同时保持低 Hc、可观的 Ms 和高电阻率。通过合理添加 Ti 和 Al，利用纳米尺度有序化引入密集的超细 L12 析出相；若适当控制其尺度和分布，可在保持软磁行为的同时同步提升力学强度和电阻率。据此设计了两种 CoFeNi 基高熵合金：Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 和五元 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi。
The Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 alloy demonstrates a rare combination of low coercivity (~1.8 Oe), high saturation magnetization (~137.3 emu/g), and ultrahigh electrical resistivity (308.3 μΩ·cm), coupled with a yield strength of ~850 MPa, UTS of ~1200 MPa, and tensile elongation of ~29%. While state-of-the-art Ta-containing HEAs may exhibit marginally lower coercivity, they generally show lower Ms (~100 emu/g) and much lower resistivity (62–117 μΩ·cm). Therefore, the Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 composition offers clear advantages for reducing eddy current losses and meeting the magnetic requirements of future electrical machines.
Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 合金展现出罕见的性能组合：低矫顽力（约 1.8 Oe）、高饱和磁化强度（约 137.3 emu/g）和超高电阻率（308.3 μΩ·cm），并兼具约 850 MPa 的屈服强度、约 1200 MPa 的 UTS 和约 29% 的拉伸延伸率。虽然最先进的含 Ta 高熵合金可能矫顽力略低，但它们通常 Ms 更低（约 100 emu/g）、电阻率也低得多（62–117 μΩ·cm）。因此，Ti0.24CoFeNi0.78 成分在降低涡流损耗、满足未来电机磁性要求方面具有明显优势。
The quinary Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi alloy exhibits a marginally higher coercivity (~2.4 Oe) and Ms (96.5 emu/g) but provides more tunability. In the CRSA condition it combines high resistivity (~269.5 μΩ·cm) with very good mechanical properties (YS ~780 MPa, UTS ~1100 MPa, elongation ~30%). Controlled thermo-mechanical processing allows further optimization: precipitate coarsening at 800 °C boosts YS to ~1200 MPa and UTS to ~1420 MPa but substantially raises coercivity (~48.6 Oe); in contrast, direct annealing of the cold-rolled condition at 900 °C produces a refined three-phase microstructure with very high YS (~1140 MPa), UTS (~1500 MPa), elongation ~18%, high resistivity (296.1 μΩ·cm), and lower coercivity (~8.8 Oe). Therefore, these newly developed CoFeNi-based HEAs offer a competing alternative to Ta-containing HEAs and surpass conventional Fe-based soft magnetic alloys, especially in mechanical properties and electrical resistivity—providing a lightweight, cost-effective, and scalable alternative for next-generation high-speed electrical machines, flywheel energy storage, and other demanding applications.
五元 Al0.3Ti0.2CoFeNi 合金的矫顽力（约 2.4 Oe）和 Ms（96.5 emu/g）略高，但提供了更大的可调性。在 CRSA 状态下，它兼具高电阻率（约 269.5 μΩ·cm）与很好的力学性能（YS 约 780 MPa，UTS 约 1100 MPa，延伸率约 30%）。受控的热机械加工可进一步优化：800 °C 下析出相粗化使 YS 提升到约 1200 MPa、UTS 提升到约 1420 MPa，但矫顽力大幅升高（约 48.6 Oe）；相反，将冷轧态在 900 °C 直接退火可得到细化的三相组织，兼具很高的 YS（约 1140 MPa）、UTS（约 1500 MPa）、约 18% 的延伸率、高电阻率（296.1 μΩ·cm）和较低的矫顽力（约 8.8 Oe）。因此，这些新开发的 CoFeNi 基高熵合金为含 Ta 高熵合金提供了有力的替代方案，并在力学性能和电阻率方面超越传统铁基软磁合金——为下一代高速电机、飞轮储能及其他苛刻应用提供了一种轻质、经济、可规模化的替代材料。
