一种力学强度高、塑性好且矫顽力极低的软磁体A mechanically strong and ductile soft magnet with extremely low coercivity

Nature 608, 310–316 (2022) · doi:10.1038/s41586-022-04935-3 · Liuliu Han, Fernando Maccari, Isnaldi R. Souza Filho, Nicolas J. Peter, Ye Wei, Baptiste Gault, Oliver Gutfleisch, Zhiming Li, Dierk Raabe

摘要 / Abstract
Soft magnetic materials (SMMs) serve in electrical applications and sustainable energy supply, allowing magnetic flux variation in response to changes in applied magnetic field, at low energy loss. The electrification of transport, households and manufacturing leads to an increase in energy consumption owing to hysteresis losses. Therefore, minimizing coercivity, which scales these losses, is crucial. Yet meeting this target alone is not enough: SMMs in electrical engines must withstand severe mechanical loads; that is, the alloys need high strength and ductility. This is a fundamental design challenge, as most methods that enhance strength introduce stress fields that can pin magnetic domains, thus increasing coercivity and hysteresis losses. Here we introduce an approach to overcome this dilemma. We have designed a Fe–Co–Ni–Ta–Al multicomponent alloy (MCA) with ferromagnetic matrix and paramagnetic coherent nanoparticles (about 91 nm in size and around 55% volume fraction). They impede dislocation motion, enhancing strength and ductility. Their small size, low coherency stress and small magnetostatic energy create an interaction volume below the magnetic domain wall width, leading to minimal domain wall pinning, thus maintaining the soft magnetic properties. The alloy has a tensile strength of 1,336 MPa at 54% tensile elongation, extremely low coercivity of 78 A m⁻¹ (less than 1 Oe), moderate saturation magnetization of 100 A m² kg⁻¹ and high electrical resistivity of 103 μΩ cm.
软磁材料（SMM）广泛用于电气应用和可持续能源供给，能在低能量损耗下随外加磁场的变化产生磁通变化。交通、家庭和制造业的电气化因磁滞损耗而使能耗上升。因此，尽量降低决定这些损耗的矫顽力至关重要。但仅达成这一目标还不够：电机中的软磁材料必须承受严酷的机械载荷，也就是说，合金需要兼具高强度和高塑性。这是一个根本性的设计难题，因为大多数强化手段都会引入应力场，从而钉扎磁畴、增大矫顽力与磁滞损耗。本文提出了一种破解此困境的方法。我们设计了一种 Fe–Co–Ni–Ta–Al 多组元合金（MCA），其铁磁性基体中弥散着顺磁性的共格纳米颗粒（尺寸约 91 nm、体积分数约 55%）。这些颗粒阻碍位错运动，从而同时提升强度与塑性。它们尺寸小、共格应力低、静磁能小，使得相互作用体积低于磁畴壁宽度，从而将畴壁钉扎降至最低，得以保持软磁性能。该合金抗拉强度达 1336 MPa、拉伸延伸率 54%，矫顽力极低仅 78 A/m（小于 1 Oe），饱和磁化强度中等（100 A·m²/kg），电阻率高达 103 μΩ·cm。
Lowest possible coercivity and highest possible electrical resistivity are primary goals for SMMs, to reduce hysteresis-related and eddy-current-related energy losses, noise and the associated material damage. Also, new SMMs with higher strength and ductility are needed, to operate under mechanically demanding loading conditions for safety-critical parts in transport and energy. High strength and ductility also serve as measures for many other mechanical properties, such as high hardness and fracture toughness. This multi-property profile creates a fundamental dilemma. The mechanical strength of metallic materials is produced by lattice defects and their elastic interactions with linear lattice faults that carry inelastic deformation, referred to as dislocations. However, the defects also interact with the magnetic domain walls and pin them. The loss in domain wall motion increases coercivity, so that the materials lose their soft magnetic features. Therefore, current SMMs follow the design rule of avoiding lattice defects to minimize coercivity. On the other hand, increasing the mechanical strength of an alloy requires enhancing its internal stress level through defects such as dislocations, grain boundaries and precipitates. This means that the task of making soft magnets mechanically strong is a trade-off between two mutually exclusive design strategies, namely, mechanical strength versus unaffected domain wall motion.
尽可能低的矫顽力和尽可能高的电阻率是软磁材料的首要目标，用以减少磁滞和涡流相关的能量损耗、噪声以及由此带来的材料损伤。同时，人们还需要强度和塑性更高的新型软磁材料，以便在交通与能源领域的安全关键部件中承受严苛的机械载荷工况。高强度和高塑性也是许多其他力学性能（如高硬度和断裂韧性）的衡量指标。这种多重性能需求带来了一个根本性的两难。金属材料的力学强度来源于晶格缺陷，以及它们与承载非弹性变形的线状晶格缺陷（即位错）之间的弹性相互作用。然而，这些缺陷同样会与磁畴壁相互作用并将其钉扎。畴壁运动受阻会增大矫顽力，使材料丧失软磁特性。因此，现有软磁材料遵循的设计法则是尽量避免晶格缺陷以降低矫顽力。另一方面，提高合金的力学强度又要求通过位错、晶界、析出相等缺陷来提升其内应力水平。这意味着，让软磁体具备高力学强度，本质上是在两种互相排斥的设计策略之间——即力学强度与不受干扰的畴壁运动之间——进行权衡。
The theory of the grain size dependence of coercivity shows its proportionality to the sixth power of the grain size for the case of nanocrystalline materials, a relation that can also be applied to particles. Current design of SMMs has thus focused on using small particles (less than 15 nm) and grain sizes (less than 100 nm). According to magnetic strain theory, the coercivity depends on the energy required to displace domain walls to overcome lattice barriers. Here we introduce particles into a multicomponent massive solid solution matrix and increase their size from the commonly used range of 5–15 nm to 90–100 nm. With that, the internal stress level and the overall elastic coherency misfit energy are reduced through the smaller specific surface area (total surface area per unit of volume) of the particles, caused by the coarsening. We then propose that the particle design must follow four main rules. First, minimal pinning of domain walls requires a well-tuned and well-controlled particle size distribution with optimum balance between the decrease in specific surface area and the increase in magnetostatic energy during particle coarsening. Second, the particle size must remain smaller than the domain wall width to prevent domain pinning, that is, strong resistance against spin rotation. Third, the chemical composition and crystal structure of the particles determine their saturation magnetization; therefore, antiferromagnetic elements are usually excluded. Fourth, strengthening of the alloys is determined by the interaction between dislocations and particles and by the friction forces exerted on dislocations in the massive solid solution matrix. Thus, intrinsically strong intermetallic particles with minimal lattice misfit are targeted. These require high forces for dislocation cutting (providing strength), but repeated cutting by ensuing dislocations emitted by the same source shear them with gradual ease along the remaining and gradually reducing particle cross sections (providing ductility).
关于矫顽力随晶粒尺寸变化的理论指出，对于纳米晶材料，矫顽力与晶粒尺寸的六次方成正比，这一关系同样适用于颗粒。因此，现有软磁材料的设计一直着眼于采用小颗粒（小于 15 nm）和小晶粒（小于 100 nm）。根据磁应变理论，矫顽力取决于使畴壁移动以克服晶格势垒所需的能量。本文将颗粒引入多组元的大体量固溶体基体，并将其尺寸从常用的 5–15 nm 增大到 90–100 nm。如此一来，通过粗化所导致的颗粒比表面积（单位体积的总表面积）减小，内应力水平和总体弹性共格错配能都得以降低。据此我们提出，颗粒设计须遵循四条主要法则。第一，要使畴壁钉扎最小化，需要精心调控的颗粒尺寸分布，在颗粒粗化过程中比表面积的下降与静磁能的上升之间取得最优平衡。第二，颗粒尺寸必须始终小于畴壁宽度，以避免磁畴钉扎，即避免对自旋转动产生强阻力。第三，颗粒的化学成分和晶体结构决定其饱和磁化强度，因此通常要排除反铁磁性元素。第四，合金的强化由位错与颗粒之间的相互作用，以及大体量固溶体基体对位错施加的摩擦力共同决定。因此，我们瞄准的是本征强度高、与基体错配极小的金属间化合物颗粒。切过这类颗粒需要很大的位错力（提供强度），但同一位错源随后不断发射的位错反复切过时，会沿着逐渐减小的剩余颗粒截面越来越容易地剪切它们（提供塑性）。
These different mechanism considerations had to be translated into a corresponding compositional alloy design concept. This is mainly guided by the requirement for (1) a ferromagnetic matrix with (2) high solid solution contribution and components that trigger the formation of (3) strong and stable intermetallic phases with (4) small lattice misfit relative to the matrix. These considerations have led us to the non-equiatomic iron–nickel–cobalt–tantalum–aluminium (Fe₃₂.₆Ni₂₇.₇Co₂₇Ta₃.₀Al₇.₀ (at.%)) MCA. We synthesized the material in a vacuum induction melting furnace, followed by conventional hot rolling and homogenization (details of the processing procedures and chemical compositions are provided in Methods). Through further isothermal heat treatments (1–100 h at 1,173 K), we prepared samples with different average particle sizes, ranging from 24 ± 15 nm to 255 ± 49 nm (edge length is used to characterize the topological particle size). The particles have L1₂ structure and complex composition, as presented in detail below.
这些不同的机制考量必须转化为相应的成分合金设计理念。其主要依据是同时满足以下要求：(1) 铁磁性基体；(2) 高固溶强化贡献；(3) 能触发形成强而稳定的金属间化合物相的组元；(4) 该相相对基体的晶格错配很小。基于这些考量，我们确定了非等原子比的铁–镍–钴–钽–铝（Fe₃₂.₆Ni₂₇.₇Co₂₇Ta₃.₀Al₇.₀，原子分数）多组元合金。材料在真空感应熔炼炉中制备，随后进行常规热轧和均匀化处理（工艺流程与化学成分详见方法部分）。通过进一步的等温热处理（1173 K 下 1–100 h），我们制备了平均颗粒尺寸各异的样品，尺寸范围为 24 ± 15 nm 到 255 ± 49 nm（用棱长来表征颗粒的拓扑尺寸）。这些颗粒具有 L1₂ 结构和复杂成分，详见下文。

微观组织分析Microstructure analysis

Figure 1 shows the structural characterization of the MCA with medium particle size (M-MCA, in which 'M' stands for medium particle size) after annealing at 1,173 K for 5 h. The M-MCA shows an average grain size of 85.3 ± 25.6 μm (excluding annealing twin boundaries) according to electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD) analysis shown in Fig. 1a. The electron channelling contrast imaging (ECCI) analysis shows that the L1₂ particles have a high number density (7.2 ± 0.2) × 10²⁰ m⁻³ and a large volume fraction (55 ± 1%) in homogeneous distribution within the face-centred cubic (fcc) matrix (Fig. 1b). The lattice misfit (0.48%) between the fcc matrix and the L1₂ particles has been calculated using their lattice parameters acquired from the X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns (Fig. 1c) by Rietveld simulation. Such a small lattice mismatch reduces the driving forces for further coarsening and the uniform dispersion prevents plastic localization at high strength. The central beam dark-field (DF) transmission electron microscopy (TEM) analysis shows that the average size of the L1₂ particles is 90.8 ± 35.8 nm (Fig. 1d). The corresponding selected-area electron diffraction (see inset of Fig. 1d) and high-resolution (HR)-TEM (Extended Data Fig. 1d) confirm the high coherency between the particles and the matrix.
图 1 展示了在 1173 K 退火 5 h 后、具有中等颗粒尺寸的 MCA（记为 M-MCA，其中"M"代表中等颗粒尺寸）的组织结构表征。根据图 1a 的电子背散射衍射（EBSD）分析，M-MCA 的平均晶粒尺寸为 85.3 ± 25.6 μm（不计退火孪晶界）。电子通道衬度成像（ECCI）分析表明，L1₂ 颗粒数量密度高，为 (7.2 ± 0.2) × 10²⁰ m⁻³，体积分数大（55 ± 1%），并均匀弥散分布于面心立方（fcc）基体中（图 1b）。利用由 X 射线衍射（XRD）图谱（图 1c）经 Rietveld 精修得到的晶格常数，计算得 fcc 基体与 L1₂ 颗粒之间的晶格错配为 0.48%。如此小的晶格错配降低了进一步粗化的驱动力，而均匀弥散又能在高强度下抑制塑性局部化。中心束暗场（DF）透射电子显微镜（TEM）分析显示，L1₂ 颗粒的平均尺寸为 90.8 ± 35.8 nm（图 1d）。相应的选区电子衍射（见图 1d 插图）和高分辨 TEM（扩展数据图 1d）证实了颗粒与基体之间的高度共格。
The elemental partitioning between the L1₂ precipitates and fcc solid solution matrix is characterized by atom probe tomography (APT). Figure 1e shows the 3D distribution of the volume investigated by APT, highlighted by a set of isosurfaces delineating regions containing more than 25 at.% Fe. Figure 1f shows the 1D compositional profiles acquired along the cylinder in e. The profiles show that Fe partitions into the fcc matrix (36 at.%), whereas the L1₂ particles are enriched in Ni (40 at.%), Ta (13 at.%) and Al (9 at.%). The compositions of the fcc and L1₂ phases were determined by averaging over three APT datasets (including ten L1₂ particles) as Fe₃₆Co₂₉Ni₂₆Al₆Ta₃ and Ni₄₀Co₂₉Ta₁₃Fe₁₂Al₉ (at.%), respectively.
L1₂ 析出相与 fcc 固溶体基体之间的元素分配由原子探针层析（APT）表征。图 1e 给出了 APT 所考察体积的三维分布，其中用一组等值面勾勒出 Fe 含量高于 25 at.% 的区域。图 1f 给出了沿 e 图中圆柱体采集的一维成分分布曲线。曲线表明，Fe 分配进入 fcc 基体（36 at.%），而 L1₂ 颗粒中富集 Ni（40 at.%）、Ta（13 at.%）和 Al（9 at.%）。通过对三组 APT 数据（含十个 L1₂ 颗粒）取平均，确定 fcc 相与 L1₂ 相的成分分别为 Fe₃₆Co₂₉Ni₂₆Al₆Ta₃ 和 Ni₄₀Co₂₉Ta₁₃Fe₁₂Al₉（原子分数）。
Apart from these intragranular nanoparticles, we also observed two types of grain boundary variants: (1) coarse grain boundary particles (160.2 ± 55.3 nm) with the same crystal structure and composition as those inside the grains and (2) incoherent particles with a minor fraction (less than 0.3%) at the triple points of the grains with different structure (cubic Fd-3m, Fig. 1c) and composition (Ta₄₀Co₂₉Ni₁₅Al₃ (at.%), Extended Data Fig. 1). These two types of particle are promoted by the high diffusion rate along the grain boundaries.
除这些晶内纳米颗粒外，我们还观察到两类晶界变体：(1) 与晶内颗粒具有相同晶体结构和成分的粗大晶界颗粒（160.2 ± 55.3 nm）；(2) 位于晶粒三叉点、占比很小（小于 0.3%）的非共格颗粒，其结构（立方 Fd-3m，图 1c）和成分（Ta₄₀Co₂₉Ni₁₅Al₃，原子分数，扩展数据图 1）与前者不同。这两类颗粒都由晶界处较高的扩散速率促成。

力学性能Mechanical properties

The current strategy of tuning the particle size also allows overcoming the strength–ductility trade-off, which is notable for advanced alloys with gigapascal-level strength. Figure 2a shows the tensile stress–strain curves of the M-MCA at room temperature (red curve). The yield strength (σ_y) is 904 ± 11 MPa, with an ultimate tensile strength (σ_UTS) of 1,336 ± 21 MPa and an elongation to failure (ε_f) up to 53.6 ± 1.5%, averaged from four tests. Accordingly, the M-MCA has a high σ_UTS × ε_f value of 71.6 GPa%. To show the improvement in strength and ductility achieved by the well-controlled particle size distribution, the mechanical response of a material variant with identical chemical composition, that is, Fe₃₂.₆Co₂₈Ni₃₈Ta₃Al₇ (at.%), but smaller particle size (S-MCA, annealed for 1 h, producing an average particle size of 24 nm), larger particle size (L-MCA, annealed for 100 h, average particle size of 255 nm) and the particle-free Fe₃₅Co₃₀Ni₃₀Ta₅ (at.%) alloy are also presented in Fig. 2a.
本文调控颗粒尺寸的策略还能克服强度–塑性的权衡，这对具有吉帕级强度的先进合金尤为可贵。图 2a 给出了 M-MCA 在室温下的拉伸应力–应变曲线（红色曲线）。其屈服强度（σ_y）为 904 ± 11 MPa，极限抗拉强度（σ_UTS）为 1336 ± 21 MPa，断裂延伸率（ε_f）高达 53.6 ± 1.5%（四次测试的平均值）。据此，M-MCA 具有很高的 σ_UTS × ε_f 值 71.6 GPa%。为展示经良好调控的颗粒尺寸分布所带来的强塑性提升，图 2a 还给出了成分完全相同（即 Fe₃₂.₆Co₂₈Ni₃₈Ta₃Al₇，原子分数）但颗粒尺寸更小（S-MCA，退火 1 h，平均颗粒尺寸 24 nm）、更大（L-MCA，退火 100 h，平均颗粒尺寸 255 nm）的两种变体，以及无颗粒的 Fe₃₅Co₃₀Ni₃₀Ta₅（原子分数）合金的力学响应。
Compared with the single-phase Fe₃₅Co₃₀Ni₃₀Ta₅ (at.%) alloy with a relatively low value of σ_y of 501 MPa (ref.), the notable increase in yield strength of the M-MCA can be attributed to the precipitation strengthening of the L1₂ particles with a high volume fraction (55%). Especially, such an improvement in the strength of the M-MCA is achieved at no expense of ductility, which is fundamentally different from the case of the S-MCA, in which a pronounced loss in ductility is observed with increasing strength. The good ductility is correlated with the high work-hardening capability, as shown in Fig. 2b. Further increase of particle size at longer annealing time (100 h) in the L-MCA leads to the decrease of ductility (53%) and σ_UTS (14%) as compared with those of the M-MCA. This is related to the mechanical weakness, strain localization and embrittlement in the particle-free zone adjacent to the grain boundary caused by solute depletion (Extended Data Fig. 2a). This interfacial weakening has been confirmed by the associated fracture morphologies, with typical ductile fracture with fine dimples in the M-MCA material (see Fig. 2b inset) and intergranular fracture in the L-MCA material (Extended Data Fig. 2b).
与屈服强度较低（σ_y 为 501 MPa，见文献）的单相 Fe₃₅Co₃₀Ni₃₀Ta₅（原子分数）合金相比，M-MCA 屈服强度的显著提高可归因于高体积分数（55%）L1₂ 颗粒的析出强化。尤为重要的是，M-MCA 强度的这种提升并未以牺牲塑性为代价，这与 S-MCA 的情形有本质区别——后者随强度提高出现了明显的塑性损失。良好的塑性与高加工硬化能力相关联，如图 2b 所示。在 L-MCA 中，更长退火时间（100 h）导致颗粒尺寸进一步增大，与 M-MCA 相比其塑性（53%）和 σ_UTS（14%）均有所下降。这与溶质贫化在邻近晶界的无颗粒区所引起的力学薄弱、应变局部化和脆化有关（扩展数据图 2a）。这种界面弱化已由相应的断口形貌所证实：M-MCA 材料呈现典型的、带细小韧窝的韧性断裂（见图 2b 插图），而 L-MCA 材料则为沿晶断裂（扩展数据图 2b）。
To unravel the mechanisms responsible for the marked improvement in the strength–ductility combination of the M-MCA, we studied its deformation substructures using EBSD kernel average misorientation (KAM) analysis and ECCI at different hardening stages (Fig. 2c). In principle, high strength requires to impede dislocation movement, whereas good ductility needs mobility of dislocations and new production of dislocations. At the early deformation stage, the M-MCA deforms by planar dislocation glide on {111} planes (Extended Data Fig. 3a–c), as commonly observed in fcc alloys. The dislocations extend through the grains. Corresponding pile-up configurations at the grain boundaries are shown by the higher KAM values (for example, at ε_T = 5%; see KAM maps in Fig. 2c). The relatively large grain size (85.3 μm) of the current M-MCA enables higher mobility of dislocations compared with most of the previously reported strong MCAs that had smaller grain size (around 10 μm). Further straining refines the crystallographically aligned microbands and facilitates the shearing of L1₂ particles (for example, at ε_T = 15%; see ECC images in Fig. 2c).
为揭示 M-MCA 强塑性组合显著提升背后的机制，我们采用 EBSD 核平均取向差（KAM）分析和 ECCI 研究了其在不同硬化阶段的变形亚结构（图 2c）。原则上，高强度要求阻碍位错运动，而良好塑性又需要位错具有可动性并能不断增殖。在变形早期，M-MCA 通过位错在 {111} 面上的平面滑移变形（扩展数据图 3a–c），这在 fcc 合金中很常见。位错贯穿晶粒。晶界处相应的位错塞积以较高的 KAM 值体现（例如在 ε_T = 5% 时；见图 2c 的 KAM 图）。本文 M-MCA 相对较大的晶粒尺寸（85.3 μm）使位错具有比此前报道的多数强 MCA（晶粒尺寸约 10 μm）更高的可动性。进一步应变会细化沿晶体学取向排列的微带，并促进对 L1₂ 颗粒的剪切（例如在 ε_T = 15% 时；见图 2c 的 ECC 图像）。
The quantification of the evolution of the average microband spacing shows a microband refinement process during straining (Extended Data Fig. 3d). The gradually reduced microband spacing causes higher passing stress and, thus, enhanced strain hardening. This has been proposed to explain the good strength–ductility combinations in high-manganese steels and MCAs. Therefore, dynamic microband refinement and particle shearing are the prevalent deformation mechanisms in the current MCAs. No Orowan looping was observed, even when increasing the average particle size up to 255 nm for the L-MCA (Extended Data Fig. 2c), as the average particle spacing remains far below the critical value (3,094 nm, see Methods) for the activation of dislocation bowing around particles, a mechanism referred to as Orowan effect. Furthermore, the stress required for shearing particles in the M-MCA with a medium particle size (91 nm) with high volume fraction (55%) is 2.2 times larger than that for the S-MCA with a smaller particle size (24 nm) with low volume fraction (43 ± 1%) (see Extended Data Table 1). Therefore, the high critical shear stress required to cut the L1₂ particles and the dynamic microband refinement during plastic deformation lead to the strong strain-hardening capacity of the M-MCA.
对平均微带间距演化的定量分析显示，应变过程中存在微带细化过程（扩展数据图 3d）。微带间距的逐渐减小会提高位错通过应力，从而增强应变硬化。这一机制曾被用来解释高锰钢和 MCA 中良好的强塑性组合。因此，动态微带细化与颗粒剪切是当前 MCA 中占主导的变形机制。即使在 L-MCA 中将平均颗粒尺寸增大到 255 nm，也未观察到 Orowan 位错环（扩展数据图 2c），因为平均颗粒间距仍远低于激活位错绕过颗粒发生弓弯（即 Orowan 效应）所需的临界值（3094 nm，见方法）。此外，在具有中等颗粒尺寸（91 nm）、高体积分数（55%）的 M-MCA 中剪切颗粒所需的应力，是具有较小颗粒尺寸（24 nm）、低体积分数（43 ± 1%）的 S-MCA 的 2.2 倍（见扩展数据表 1）。因此，切过 L1₂ 颗粒所需的高临界剪切应力，加上塑性变形过程中的动态微带细化，共同造就了 M-MCA 强大的应变硬化能力。

磁性能Magnetic properties

Figure 3a,b shows the magnetic properties of the MCAs. All alloys show typical soft ferromagnetic behaviour. The M-MCA shows an excellent combination of extremely low coercivity (H_c) of 78 ± 3 A m⁻¹ (less than 1 Oe) and moderate saturation magnetization (M_s) of 100.2 ± 0.2 A m² kg⁻¹. We identified a higher M_s for the alloy variant with larger average particle size (see inset in Fig. 3a). The reason for this is the change in intrinsic magnetic behaviour, as indicated by the higher Curie temperature (T_c), shown by the thermomagnetic curves (Extended Data Fig. 4a). Two distinct changes of the slope, indicating two ferromagnetic phases, are observed in the S-MCA. By contrast, only one sharp drop is observed in the M-MCA and L-MCA materials, indicating the presence of only one ferromagnetic phase. This is further confirmed by measuring the magnetic behaviour of the MCAs at elevated temperatures (Extended Data Fig. 4b). Considering that both fcc and L1₂ phases contain high concentrations of ferromagnetic elements, we investigated their individual magnetic response through casting both phases as separate bulk samples with their respective nominal compositions acquired previously from APT analysis (see Methods for details). The results show that the L1₂ bulk phase is paramagnetic, whereas the fcc matrix is ferromagnetic in the M-MCA (Extended Data Fig. 4c). Owing to different partitioning, the magnetic behaviour of the L1₂ phase varies from ferromagnetic in the S-MCA material variant to paramagnetic in the M-MCA and L-MCA materials. The mechanism behind this transition is the change in the intrinsic spin alignment, which is related to the change in chemical composition and ordering during annealing. The overall increase in saturation magnetization of the MCAs with particle coarsening is attributed to the change in fcc matrix composition owing to elemental partitioning (Extended Data Fig. 5), that is, specifically to the resultant higher concentration of (Fe+Co) in the fcc matrix. This effect enhances the total average magnetic moment per formula unit and leads to a higher M_s.
图 3a、b 给出了各 MCA 的磁性能。所有合金均表现出典型的软铁磁行为。M-MCA 兼具极低的矫顽力（H_c，78 ± 3 A/m，小于 1 Oe）和中等的饱和磁化强度（M_s，100.2 ± 0.2 A·m²/kg），组合极佳。我们发现平均颗粒尺寸更大的合金变体具有更高的 M_s（见图 3a 插图）。其原因在于本征磁性行为的变化，热磁曲线所显示的更高居里温度（T_c）即为佐证（扩展数据图 4a）。在 S-MCA 中观察到斜率的两次明显变化，表明存在两个铁磁相；相比之下，M-MCA 和 L-MCA 中只观察到一次急剧下降，表明只存在一个铁磁相。通过测量各 MCA 在高温下的磁性行为进一步证实了这一点（扩展数据图 4b）。考虑到 fcc 相和 L1₂ 相都含有高浓度的铁磁性元素，我们将两相分别按其此前由 APT 分析得到的名义成分铸成独立块体样品，考察其各自的磁响应（详见方法）。结果表明，在 M-MCA 中 L1₂ 块体相为顺磁性，而 fcc 基体为铁磁性（扩展数据图 4c）。由于元素分配不同，L1₂ 相的磁性行为从 S-MCA 变体中的铁磁性变为 M-MCA 和 L-MCA 中的顺磁性。这一转变背后的机制是本征自旋排列的变化，而后者与退火过程中化学成分和有序度的变化有关。各 MCA 饱和磁化强度随颗粒粗化整体升高，归因于元素分配所致的 fcc 基体成分变化（扩展数据图 5），具体而言是 fcc 基体中 (Fe+Co) 浓度随之升高。这一效应提升了每化学式单元的总平均磁矩，从而带来更高的 M_s。
To gain further insight into the mechanism behind the magnetic response of the M-MCA, we investigated the domain structure using magneto-optical Kerr effect (MOKE) microscopy (Fig. 3c) under different applied magnetic fields. At the AC demagnetized state on an applied field of 40 kA m⁻¹, the nucleation of magnetic domains is uniformly distributed within the grain. Further increasing the applied field (155 kA m⁻¹) leads to domain wall movement and growth of energetically favourable domains. The domains grow unaffected inside the grains but get impeded at grain and twin boundaries (Extended Data Fig. 6). Figure 3d summarizes the statistically averaged particle size distribution with respect to the coercivity of all the MCA samples at different annealed states. The data are acquired by developing an automated processing protocol (see Methods). The coercivity first decreases from 763 A m⁻¹ (S-MCA, average particle size 24 nm) to 78 A m⁻¹ (M-MCA, average particle size 91 nm) and then increases to 1,745 A m⁻¹ (L-MCA, average particle size 255 nm). Both the average particle size and grain size increase monotonously with increasing annealing time (Extended Data Table 1). Because the grain size of the MCAs material is above the critical single-domain size, its coercivity decreases with grain coarsening, following the model for the grain size dependence of the coercivity as H_c ~ 1/D (in which D is the grain size). However, the magnitude of the decrease in coercivity owing to grain coarsening according to the model is negligible compared with the experimentally observed values: the difference according to the model calculation between the S-MCA and M-MCA variants is 2 A m⁻¹, but the experimentally observed difference is 775 A m⁻¹. Accordingly, the energy required for the irreversible displacement of domain walls within the grain is the determining effect for the extremely low coercivity.
为进一步深入理解 M-MCA 磁响应背后的机制，我们采用磁光克尔效应（MOKE）显微术研究了不同外加磁场下的磁畴结构（图 3c）。在外加 40 kA/m 的交流退磁态下，磁畴形核均匀分布于晶粒内部。进一步增大外加磁场（155 kA/m）会导致畴壁移动，能量上有利的磁畴长大。磁畴在晶粒内部长大时不受阻碍，但在晶界和孪晶界处受阻（扩展数据图 6）。图 3d 汇总了所有 MCA 样品在不同退火态下统计平均的颗粒尺寸分布与矫顽力的关系。这些数据通过我们开发的自动化处理流程获得（见方法）。矫顽力先从 763 A/m（S-MCA，平均颗粒尺寸 24 nm）下降到 78 A/m（M-MCA，平均颗粒尺寸 91 nm），随后又升高到 1745 A/m（L-MCA，平均颗粒尺寸 255 nm）。平均颗粒尺寸和晶粒尺寸都随退火时间的延长单调增大（扩展数据表 1）。由于 MCA 材料的晶粒尺寸大于临界单畴尺寸，其矫顽力随晶粒粗化而下降，符合矫顽力随晶粒尺寸变化的模型 H_c ~ 1/D（其中 D 为晶粒尺寸）。然而，按该模型，晶粒粗化引起的矫顽力下降幅度与实验观测值相比可忽略不计：模型计算给出 S-MCA 与 M-MCA 变体之间的差值为 2 A/m，而实验观测到的差值为 775 A/m。据此可知，使畴壁在晶粒内部发生不可逆位移所需的能量，才是决定极低矫顽力的关键因素。
Therefore, the notable decrease in coercivity at the early particle coarsening stage (≤91 nm) is attributed to the gradual reduction of the overall coherency stresses between matrix and particles, owing to their average size decrease. More specifically, the values of the specific surface area × integrated lattice misfit decrease from 1.09 × 10⁶ m⁻¹ in the S-MCA material to 4.08 × 10⁵ m⁻¹ in the M-MCA material. The dislocation density in the matrix decreases from 1.50 × 10¹⁴ m⁻² in the S-MCA material to 9.32 × 10¹³ m⁻² in the M-MCA material with increasing the annealing time from 1 h to 5 h (see Methods). The reduction in dislocation density lowers the associated elastic distortion fields that can pin the domain walls. Although the elastic distortion and dislocation density decrease with particle coarsening, the coercivity of the L-MCA increases. Two main mechanisms are proposed to explain this. First, the average particle size and the associated strain field in the L-MCA becomes larger than the domain wall width (δ_w), leading to domain wall pinning. The L-MCA has a δ_w of 117 nm and a much larger average particle size of 255 nm, whereas the average particle size of the M-MCA is 91 nm, which is below the δ_w of 112 nm (see Methods). Second, the increased magnetostatic energy (E_s) associated with the paramagnetic particles causes a stronger individual pinning effect of each particle on the domain wall motion. More specifically, the E_s of the L-MCA is estimated to be 23 times larger than that of the M-MCA (see Methods). When considering only particle size, it should be noted that, for the M-MCA, the coherent particles distributed along the grain boundaries with an average size (160 nm) above the δ_w (112 nm) are expected to have a stronger pinning effect on the domain wall than those in the grain interiors (91 nm). However, these coarser particles occupy only a small fraction (1.2 ± 0.2% in M-MCA), hence, with a negligible effect on domain wall motion.
因此，在颗粒粗化早期阶段（≤91 nm）矫顽力的显著下降，归因于随颗粒平均尺寸减小、基体与颗粒之间总体共格应力的逐渐降低。更具体地说，"比表面积 × 积分晶格错配"这一量值从 S-MCA 中的 1.09 × 10⁶ m⁻¹ 降至 M-MCA 中的 4.08 × 10⁵ m⁻¹。随退火时间从 1 h 增至 5 h，基体中的位错密度从 S-MCA 的 1.50 × 10¹⁴ m⁻² 降至 M-MCA 的 9.32 × 10¹³ m⁻²（见方法）。位错密度的降低减弱了可钉扎畴壁的相关弹性畸变场。尽管弹性畸变和位错密度随颗粒粗化而下降，L-MCA 的矫顽力却升高了。我们提出两个主要机制来解释这一点。其一，L-MCA 中平均颗粒尺寸及其相关应变场变得大于畴壁宽度（δ_w），从而钉扎畴壁。L-MCA 的 δ_w 为 117 nm，而其平均颗粒尺寸大得多，为 255 nm；相比之下 M-MCA 的平均颗粒尺寸为 91 nm，低于其 δ_w（112 nm）（见方法）。其二，与顺磁性颗粒相关的静磁能（E_s）升高，使每个颗粒对畴壁运动的单体钉扎作用更强。更具体地，估算 L-MCA 的 E_s 约为 M-MCA 的 23 倍（见方法）。若仅考虑颗粒尺寸则应注意：对 M-MCA 而言，沿晶界分布、平均尺寸（160 nm）高于 δ_w（112 nm）的共格颗粒，理应比晶内颗粒（91 nm）对畴壁具有更强的钉扎作用。然而这些较粗颗粒仅占很小比例（M-MCA 中为 1.2 ± 0.2%），因此对畴壁运动的影响可忽略。
These considerations show that the nanoscale size distribution of the particles must be carefully controlled to minimize their pinning effect on domain wall motion, which determines the coercivity of the alloy. This is achieved here by an optimal balance between the release of structural defects (for example, interfacial elastic distortion, dislocation density) just down to a level required to maintain high mechanical strength and the increase of the pinning effect from the magnetostatic energy, while keeping the particle size below the domain wall width during particle coarsening.
这些分析表明，必须精细调控颗粒的纳米尺度尺寸分布，以最大限度减小其对畴壁运动的钉扎作用，而这正是决定合金矫顽力的关键。本文通过如下最优平衡来实现这一点：一方面把结构缺陷（如界面弹性畸变、位错密度）释放到恰好维持高力学强度所需的水平，另一方面控制静磁能带来的钉扎作用增强，同时在颗粒粗化过程中始终保持颗粒尺寸小于畴壁宽度。

多项性能综合对比Overview of several property profiles

To highlight the good combination of mechanical and magnetic properties of the M-MCA with medium particle size, we compare it with existing SMMs in an Ashby plot showing the σ_UTS × ε_f values against the H_c (Fig. 4a). This comparison shows that the σ_UTS × ε_f value of the new M-MCA material outperforms all other SMMs. Notably, the H_c of the new material is lower than that of all Fe–Ni alloys and other MCAs, comparable with that of Fe–Si, Fe–Co alloys and pure Fe. Amorphous and nanocrystalline soft magnetic alloys can show relatively low H_c (less than 10 A m⁻¹) and high mechanical strength, yet their limited ductility, damage tolerance and workability limit their application in cases in which the load path changes or high stresses, forming or machining are applied. A radar plot comparing the various soft magnetic and mechanical properties of the current M-MCA with several typical commercial SMMs is shown in Extended Data Fig. 8. Although the saturation induction (B_s) of the current M-MCA is not comparable with that of typical commercial SMMs (Extended Data Fig. 9), it has high electrical resistivity (ρ) (103 ± 0.8 μΩ cm, see Extended Data Fig. 10), a feature that makes it suitable for applications with AC conditions. The high ρ of the M-MCA is expected to be derived from the high resistance to electron movement from the larger lattice distortion. Figure 4b compares the σ_UTS × ε_f values versus the grain size of the M-MCA alloy with recently reported strong and ductile MCAs. The analysis shows that the current alloy reaches high values of σ_UTS × ε_f even without the substantial contribution from grain boundaries, confirming the notable strengthening effect provided by the nanoparticles and the massive solid solution.
为凸显具有中等颗粒尺寸的 M-MCA 在力学与磁性能上的良好组合，我们在 Ashby 图中将其与现有软磁材料进行对比，横纵坐标分别为 H_c 与 σ_UTS × ε_f（图 4a）。对比表明，新型 M-MCA 材料的 σ_UTS × ε_f 值优于所有其他软磁材料。值得注意的是，该新材料的 H_c 低于所有 Fe–Ni 合金和其他 MCA，与 Fe–Si、Fe–Co 合金及纯 Fe 相当。非晶和纳米晶软磁合金可具有相对较低的 H_c（小于 10 A/m）和高力学强度，但其有限的塑性、损伤容限和加工性，限制了它们在载荷路径改变、或需承受高应力、成形或机加工场合的应用。扩展数据图 8 给出了将当前 M-MCA 的各项软磁与力学性能与若干典型商用软磁材料对比的雷达图。虽然当前 M-MCA 的饱和磁感应强度（B_s）无法与典型商用软磁材料相比（扩展数据图 9），但它具有高电阻率（ρ，103 ± 0.8 μΩ·cm，见扩展数据图 10），这一特性使其适用于交流工况。M-MCA 的高 ρ 预期源自较大的晶格畸变对电子运动的高阻碍。图 4b 将 M-MCA 合金的 σ_UTS × ε_f 值随晶粒尺寸的变化，与近期报道的强而韧的 MCA 进行了对比。分析表明，即使没有晶界的显著贡献，当前合金也能达到高的 σ_UTS × ε_f 值，从而证实了纳米颗粒和大体量固溶体所提供的显著强化效应。

结论Conclusions

In summary, we have developed a material that unifies so far mutually exclusive properties, namely, high mechanical strength (1,336 MPa), high tensile ductility (54%), low coercivity (78 A m⁻¹), moderate saturation magnetization (100 A m² kg⁻¹) and high resistivity (103 μΩ cm). We realized this in a new class of bulk SMMs through a nanoparticle dispersion with well-controlled particle size (91 nm), magnetic properties, coherency strain, strength and interface energy. The design strategy is opposite to that generally applied in conventional SMM design. Instead of using the smallest microstructure features (particle size <15 nm) to avoid magnetic wall pinning as in conventional SMMs, we have chosen a relatively coarse particle dispersion with tuned particle/matrix interfacial coherency stresses and paramagnetic properties to minimize magnetic pinning of domain walls on the one hand (soft magnetism) and maximize interaction strength with dislocations on the other (strength and ductility).
综上所述，我们研制出一种将迄今互相排斥的性能统一于一体的材料，即高力学强度（1336 MPa）、高拉伸塑性（54%）、低矫顽力（78 A/m）、中等饱和磁化强度（100 A·m²/kg）和高电阻率（103 μΩ·cm）。我们在一类新型块体软磁材料中，通过对颗粒尺寸（91 nm）、磁性能、共格应变、强度和界面能均加以良好调控的纳米颗粒弥散，实现了这一目标。该设计策略与传统软磁材料设计中普遍采用的思路相反。传统软磁材料使用最细小的组织特征（颗粒尺寸 <15 nm）来避免磁畴壁钉扎，而我们则选择相对粗大的颗粒弥散，并调控颗粒/基体界面共格应力及其顺磁性，一方面把畴壁的磁性钉扎降至最低（保证软磁性），另一方面最大化与位错的相互作用强度（保证强度和塑性）。
