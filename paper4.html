两吉帕高强度、可塑性变形的软磁体Two-gigapascal-strong ductile soft magnets

摘要 / Abstract
Soft magnetic materials (SMMs) are indispensable for electromechanical energy conversion in high-efficiency applications, but they are exposed to increasing mechanical loading conditions in electric motors due to higher rotational speeds. Enhancing the yield strength of SMMs is essential to prevent the degradation in magnetic performance and failure from plastic deformation, yet most SMMs have yield strengths far below one gigapascal. Here, we present a multicomponent nanostructuring strategy that doubles the yield strength of SMMs while maintaining ductility. We introduce morphologically anisotropic nanoprecipitates through dislocation-driven precipitation induced by preceding deformation during heat treatment in an iron–nickel–cobalt–tantalum material. With all dimensions of the precipitates below the magnetic domain wall width, we achieve a high precipitate number density with a large specific surface area, small interprecipitate spacing, and high lattice mismatch, which impede dislocation glide and strengthen the material. Both the matrix and precipitates are ferromagnetic, yielding a high magnetic moment. This nanostructuring approach offers a pathway to two-gigapascal-strong ductile SMMs with moderately increased coercivity that can be tolerated in exchange for significantly improved mechanical performance for sustainable electrification.
软磁材料（SMM）对高效应用中的机电能量转换不可或缺，但随着转速提高，它们在电机中承受的机械载荷日益增大。提升软磁材料的屈服强度对于防止磁性能退化和塑性变形失效至关重要，然而大多数软磁材料的屈服强度远低于 1 GPa。本文提出一种多组元纳米结构化策略，在保持塑性的同时使软磁材料的屈服强度翻倍。我们在一种铁–镍–钴–钽材料中，利用预先变形引入的位错，在热处理时驱动位错主导的析出，形成"形貌各向异性"的纳米析出相。由于析出相的各个维度都小于磁畴壁宽度，我们获得了高的析出相数量密度，兼具大的比表面积、小的析出相间距和高的晶格错配，从而阻碍位错滑移、强化材料。基体和析出相均为铁磁性，带来高磁矩。这种纳米结构化方法为实现两吉帕强度、可塑性变形的软磁体提供了途径——其矫顽力有适度升高，但为换取显著提升的力学性能，这一代价在可持续电气化中是可以接受的。
Soft magnetic materials (SMMs) exhibit rapid magnetic flux variations in response to changes in external magnetic fields. Electrification using SMMs in energy conversion applications is the fastest and most sustainable approach for counteracting climate change and solving the global energy crisis. Minimization of coercivity is crucial for reducing magnetic hysteresis losses in the form of dissipative heat during high-frequency magnetization and demagnetization. At the same time, increasing demand is required for SMMs with high yield strength and good ductility that can withstand harsh and dynamic mechanical loads in electrical motors and high-speed flywheel energy storage systems. The yield strength is the stress at which irreversible plastic deformation starts; thus, loading stresses must remain below this value for safe and efficient operation, otherwise high-speed electrical machines and their rotating parts undergo irreversible dimensional changes. SMMs with good ductility are also needed for manufacturing flexibility and damage tolerance. If the yield strength is too low, a build-up of inelastic deformation carriers will impede the free motion of magnetic domain walls, deteriorating the soft magnetic properties. Realizing a high yield strength requires preventing dislocation nucleation and motion through microscopic backstresses exerted by atomic-scale lattice distortion and lattice defects. This effect, termed mechanical strengthening, results in a fundamental design challenge because the internal stress fields of lattice defects can pin magnetic domain wall motion, thereby destroying the soft magnetic properties. Conventional microstructural strengthening measures often result in embrittlement and undesired domain wall interactions; coercivity and hysteresis-related energy losses may increase, and soft magnetic performance is lost.
软磁材料（SMM）能随外磁场的变化产生快速的磁通变化。在能量转换应用中使用软磁材料进行电气化，是应对气候变化、解决全球能源危机最快、最可持续的途径。尽量降低矫顽力对减少高频磁化–退磁过程中以耗散热形式出现的磁滞损耗至关重要。与此同时，人们对兼具高屈服强度和良好塑性、能承受电机和高速飞轮储能系统中严酷动态机械载荷的软磁材料需求日益增长。屈服强度是不可逆塑性变形开始时的应力；因此，载荷应力必须低于该值才能安全高效运行，否则高速电机及其旋转部件会发生不可逆的尺寸变化。塑性良好的软磁材料还能提供加工灵活性和损伤容限。若屈服强度过低，非弹性变形载体的积累会阻碍磁畴壁的自由运动，恶化软磁性能。要实现高屈服强度，就需要通过原子尺度晶格畸变和晶格缺陷施加的微观背应力来阻止位错的形核与运动。这一效应称为力学强化，它带来一个根本性的设计难题：晶格缺陷的内应力场会钉扎磁畴壁运动，从而破坏软磁性能。传统的组织强化手段常导致脆化和不利的畴壁相互作用；矫顽力和磁滞相关的能量损耗可能增大，软磁性能随之丧失。
Attempts have been made to address this challenge by introducing and tuning various types, sizes, and coherency states of second-phase precipitates into conventional SMMs and multicomponent-based magnets. Coherent cuboidal precipitates of well-tuned size can result in low magnetic domain wall pinning. However, the increase in attainable strength has proven to be limited to values far below one gigapascal. This is because the strengthening effect of coherent precipitates by dislocation shearing increases with size but cannot exceed the Orowan stress, at which dislocations circumvent precipitates. In contrast, incoherent precipitates are nonshearable obstacles for dislocations. Yet their large interfacial energy usually leads to coarse micron-sized precipitates that can strongly pin the motion of magnetic domain walls, thus losing soft magnetic features.
人们曾尝试通过在传统软磁材料和多组元磁体中引入并调控不同类型、尺寸和共格状态的第二相析出相来应对这一挑战。尺寸调控得当的共格立方（cuboidal）析出相可实现较低的磁畴壁钉扎。然而，可达到的强度提升被证明仅限于远低于 1 GPa 的水平。这是因为共格析出相通过位错剪切的强化效应虽随尺寸增大而增强，但不能超过 Orowan 应力——达到该应力时位错会绕过析出相。相反，非共格析出相是位错无法剪切的障碍，但其较大的界面能通常导致粗大的微米级析出相，会强烈钉扎磁畴壁运动，从而丧失软磁特性。
We propose a strategy to resolve this dilemma through a nanostructuring approach involving morphologically anisotropic nanoprecipitates. The synergistic effects of the morphology, distribution, size, and composition of the precipitates are achieved by: (a) The anisotropic morphology increases the surface area-to-volume ratio and lattice mismatch, strengthening the material via mechanical interactions with dislocations. (b) A finer size distribution reduces the interprecipitate spacing, enhancing strength. (c) The micromagnetically relevant length scales of the precipitates are kept below the magnetic domain wall width, minimizing interference with domain wall motion and thus reducing magnetic losses. (d) High concentrations of ferromagnetic elements with the highest possible magnetization are used. (e) We address the design challenge of triggering coherent, ferromagnetic, and anisotropic nanoprecipitates under thermodynamically nonequilibrium conditions while suppressing capillary-driven coarsening and avoiding the transition to incoherency.
我们提出一种策略，借助引入"形貌各向异性"纳米析出相的纳米结构化方法来化解这一两难。析出相在形貌、分布、尺寸和成分上的协同效应通过以下途径实现：(a) 各向异性形貌增大表面积/体积比和晶格错配，从而通过与位错的力学相互作用强化材料；(b) 更细的尺寸分布减小析出相间距，提高强度；(c) 析出相在微磁学上相关的尺度始终小于磁畴壁宽度，最大限度减少对畴壁运动的干扰，从而降低磁损耗；(d) 采用高浓度、尽可能高磁化强度的铁磁性元素；(e) 我们解决了在热力学非平衡条件下触发共格、铁磁、各向异性纳米析出相，同时抑制毛细驱动粗化、避免向非共格转变这一设计挑战。
These considerations have led us to a class of non-equiatomic iron–nickel–cobalt–tantalum multicomponent alloys (MCAs). The first three elements form a ferromagnetic matrix providing high magnetization, while the fourth triggers ordered precipitates. To achieve morphologically anisotropic and coherent nanoprecipitates with a fine distribution, we introduce microbands via plastic deformation along {111} planes to control the dislocation density and precipitate morphology. The microbands contain a high line density of planar dislocations, promoting rapid nucleation. Short-term isothermal annealing (1073 K, 10 min) triggers precipitates whose longitudinal edges lie along the fcc slip planes—a behaviour resulting from dislocation-assisted nucleation that promotes coherent-interface precipitates in a thermodynamic nonequilibrium state, enabling rapid nucleation, high number density, and small interprecipitate spacing. A minor fraction of partially coherent precipitates with similar chemistry/morphology but different structure coexists, formed during the evolution toward a more stable state. Extended treatment (1073 K, 60–300 min) tends to form incoherent precipitates until equilibrium; incoherent phase boundaries and coarsening cause an undesired decrease in strength and increase in coercivity.
基于这些考量，我们确定了一类非等原子比的铁–镍–钴–钽多组元合金（MCA）。前三种元素构成提供高磁化强度的铁磁性基体，第四种元素则触发有序析出相。为获得形貌各向异性、共格且细弥散的纳米析出相，我们通过沿 {111} 面的塑性变形引入微带（microband），以控制位错密度和析出相形貌。微带含有高线密度的平面位错，促进快速形核。短时等温退火（1073 K，10 min）触发析出相，其纵向棱边沿 fcc 滑移面分布——这一行为源于位错辅助形核，能在热力学非平衡态下促进形成具有共格界面的析出相，实现快速形核、高数量密度和小析出相间距。少量成分/形貌相近但结构不同的部分共格析出相与之共存，它们在向更稳定态演化的过程中相继形成。延长处理（1073 K，60–300 min）则倾向于形成非共格析出相直至达到平衡；非共格相界和粗化会带来不利的强度下降和矫顽力上升。

组织分析Microstructural analysis

To enhance the mechanical interaction between precipitates and dislocations, we designed an Fe35Co30Ni30Ta5 (at.%) alloy system using Thermo-Calc with the High Entropy Alloy database. This system aims for a supersaturated solid solution single-phase structure by solution treatment (1473 K) and a second phase with a different crystal structure by annealing (773–1173 K), to obtain a strong ferromagnetic solid solution matrix and an intermetallic phase with different crystal symmetry or anisotropic interfacial energies that can assume an anisotropic morphology. The base material "B-MCA" with a single-phase fcc structure was made by conventional casting, hot rolling, and homogenization at 1473 K (confirmed by XRD). B-MCA was cold rolled for 60% thickness reduction to introduce a high line density (3.2 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²) of planar dislocations on {111}fcc planes as nucleation sites (Fig. 1a, recorded by ECC imaging). The resulting single-fcc alloy with elongated grains along the cold-rolling direction is termed D-MCA ("D" = deformed). No obvious elemental segregation was observed from the microscale to the near-atomic scale, indicating chemical homogeneity.
为增强析出相与位错之间的力学相互作用，我们用 Thermo-Calc 配合高熵合金数据库设计了 Fe35Co30Ni30Ta5（原子分数）合金体系。该体系旨在通过固溶处理（1473 K）获得过饱和固溶体单相结构，并通过退火（773–1173 K）获得晶体结构不同的第二相，从而得到强铁磁性固溶体基体和一种具有不同晶体对称性或各向异性界面能、可呈现各向异性形貌的金属间化合物相。基础材料"B-MCA"（单相 fcc 结构）通过常规铸造、热轧和 1473 K 均匀化制得（经 XRD 证实）。将 B-MCA 冷轧减薄 60%，在 {111}fcc 面上引入高线密度（3.2 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²）的平面位错作为形核位点（图 1a，用 ECC 成像记录）。所得沿冷轧方向具有拉长晶粒的单相 fcc 合金记为 D-MCA（"D"代表变形 deformed）。从微观尺度到近原子尺度均未观察到明显的元素偏析，表明其化学均匀。
Nanoprecipitation was initiated by annealing D-MCA at 1073 K for 10 min. The resulting alloy contains a high number density (~(2.32 ± 0.34) × 10²² m⁻³) and volume fraction (~38%) of morphologically anisotropic nanoprecipitates (53 nm long, 8 nm wide) and is termed A-MCA ("A" = anisotropic; Fig. 1b). This gives a small effective planar inter-precipitate spacing (26.1 ± 6.1 nm) and a large specific surface area (3.3 × 10⁸ m⁻¹). The precipitates are distributed homogeneously in the fcc matrix (average grain size 101 ± 49 μm) with an {001}<211> texture and deformation-induced twins; their longitudinal edges are parallel to <111>fcc. STEM-HAADF imaging shows some precipitates in hcp arrangements (partially coherent), while others are L12 (ordered fcc) fully coherent with the matrix. The L12 nanoprecipitates constitute the major fraction, since no hcp diffraction peaks/superlattice spots appeared in XRD and SAED. The anisotropic morphology is attributed to anisotropic interfacial energies—0.11 and 0.64 J·m⁻² for the (111) and (100) planes, respectively (DFT). APT (Fig. 1d, 7 at.% Ta interface threshold) gives matrix Fe39.2Co30.5Ni27.6Ta2.7 and precipitate Ni37.7Co31.1Ta20.2Fe11.0 (at.%); Fe is depleted and Ta partitions strongly into the precipitates.
通过将 D-MCA 在 1073 K 退火 10 min 引发纳米析出。所得合金含有高数量密度（约 (2.32 ± 0.34) × 10²² m⁻³）和高体积分数（约 38%）的形貌各向异性纳米析出相（长 53 nm、宽 8 nm），记为 A-MCA（"A"代表各向异性 anisotropic；图 1b）。由此得到小的有效面内析出相间距（26.1 ± 6.1 nm）和大的比表面积（3.3 × 10⁸ m⁻¹）。析出相均匀分布于 fcc 基体（平均晶粒尺寸 101 ± 49 μm）中，基体具有 {001}<211> 织构和变形诱导孪晶；析出相的纵向棱边平行于 <111>fcc。STEM-HAADF 成像显示部分析出相为 hcp 排列（部分共格），另一些为与基体完全共格的 L12（有序 fcc）。L12 纳米析出相占主要比例，因为 XRD 和 SAED 中未出现 hcp 衍射峰/超晶格斑。各向异性形貌归因于各向异性的界面能——据 DFT，(111) 面和 (100) 面的界面能分别为 0.11 和 0.64 J·m⁻²。APT（图 1d，以 7 at.% Ta 作为界面阈值）给出基体成分 Fe39.2Co30.5Ni27.6Ta2.7、析出相成分 Ni37.7Co31.1Ta20.2Fe11.0（原子分数）；Fe 贫化，Ta 强烈分配进入析出相。
An intermediate treatment (973 K, 10 min) produced "I-MCA", expected to share A-MCA's precipitation mechanism at a lower kinetic factor. Its dislocation arrangement resembles D-MCA (lower line density 2.8 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²). Correlative TEM-APT (Fig. 1f) identified two dislocation lines as Ta-enriched regions (7 at.% Ta iso-surface), confirming preferential Ta segregation to dislocations that creates highly distorted regions initiating dislocation-assisted preferred precipitation. Pronounced superlattice diffraction from the ordered fcc lattice confirms the phase transformation of I-MCA is fundamentally the same as A-MCA. Notably, recrystallization was not observed even at 1173 K (0.7 Tm) and 300 min, because static recrystallization is suppressed by (1) precipitates pinning grain boundaries and (2) the high stacking fault energy (73.9 mJ·m⁻²) enabling dislocation double cross slip and screw annihilation. Prolonging the dwell or raising the temperature promotes transformation to more stable incoherent hcp precipitates. To confirm the role of preceding planar dislocations, B-MCA subjected to the same 1073 K/10 min treatment gave "S-MCA" ("S" = spherical) with coherent spherical precipitates (~11.1 ± 4.8 nm, volume fraction ~44.3 ± 3.6%).
中间处理（973 K，10 min）得到"I-MCA"，预期在较低动力学因子下与 A-MCA 共享析出机制。其位错排布与 D-MCA 相似（线密度较低，2.8 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²）。相关性 TEM-APT（图 1f）将两条位错线识别为富 Ta 区域（7 at.% Ta 等浓度面），证实 Ta 优先偏聚于位错、形成高度畸变区并引发位错辅助的择优析出。来自有序 fcc 晶格的明显超晶格衍射证实 I-MCA 的相变与 A-MCA 本质相同。值得注意的是，即使在 1173 K（0.7 倍熔点）和 300 min 下也未观察到再结晶，因为静态再结晶被以下因素抑制：(1) 析出相钉扎晶界；(2) 高层错能（73.9 mJ·m⁻²）使位错发生双交滑移并湮灭螺型分量。延长保温或提高温度会促进向更稳定的非共格 hcp 析出相转变。为确认预置平面位错的作用，对 B-MCA 施加相同的 1073 K/10 min 处理得到"S-MCA"（"S"代表球形 spherical），其含有共格球形析出相（约 11.1 ± 4.8 nm，体积分数约 44.3 ± 3.6%）。

力学与磁性能Mechanical and magnetic properties

The introduction of morphologically anisotropic precipitates yields a two-gigapascal-strong ductile soft magnet. Figure 2a shows the tensile stress–strain curves of A-MCA: yield strength (σy) 1.93 ± 0.02 GPa, total elongation 12.6 ± 0.9%. This high yield strength keeps the material elastic even under severe stresses without generating dislocations, preventing irreversible shape changes and undesired dislocation–domain-wall interactions during service, while its good ductility allows manufacturing flexibility and damage tolerance. Compared with variants of the same chemistry—B-MCA (homogenized, no precipitates), D-MCA (cold-rolled, no precipitates), and S-MCA (spherical precipitates)—A-MCA shows a nearly fourfold (~385%) increase in yield strength over precipitate-free B-MCA. D-MCA shows no strain hardening (necks immediately after yield) because dislocation motion/multiplication is impeded by the dense dislocation network (~3.2 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²). S-MCA has a yield strength below one gigapascal. A-MCA maintains a high dislocation density (2.5 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²) plus a high number density (2.3 × 10²² m⁻³) of anisotropic nanoprecipitates, giving 1.9 GPa yield strength while remaining ductile—resolving a long-standing dilemma—with a multi-stage work-hardening capability.
引入形貌各向异性析出相得到了两吉帕强度、可塑性变形的软磁体。图 2a 给出 A-MCA 的拉伸应力–应变曲线：屈服强度（σy）1.93 ± 0.02 GPa，总延伸率 12.6 ± 0.9%。如此高的屈服强度使材料即使在严酷应力下也保持弹性、不产生位错，从而防止服役中不可逆的形状变化以及不利的位错–畴壁相互作用；而良好塑性则提供加工灵活性和损伤容限。与成分相同的各变体——B-MCA（均匀化、无析出相）、D-MCA（冷轧、无析出相）和 S-MCA（球形析出相）——相比，A-MCA 的屈服强度较无析出相的 B-MCA 提高近四倍（约 385%）。D-MCA 无应变硬化（屈服后立即颈缩），因为位错运动/增殖被密集的位错网络（约 3.2 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²）阻碍。S-MCA 的屈服强度低于 1 GPa。A-MCA 既保持高位错密度（2.5 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²），又具有高数量密度（2.3 × 10²² m⁻³）的各向异性纳米析出相，屈服强度达 1.9 GPa 且仍保持塑性——化解了长期存在的两难——并表现出多阶段加工硬化能力。
Figure 2b shows the magnetic performance. A-MCA exhibits typical soft ferromagnetic behaviour with a saturation magnetization (Ms) of 115.4 ± 0.2 Am²·kg⁻¹ and coercivity (Hc) of 0.36 ± 0.03 kA·m⁻¹. Compared with our previously designed strong ductile soft magnet Fe32Co28Ni28Ta8Al7 (Ms 100.2 Am²·kg⁻¹), A-MCA shows a ~15% higher Ms. DFT (exact muffin-tin orbital method) gives a total magnetic moment of 1.50 μB/f.u. for Fe35Co30Ni30Ta5, ~15% higher than 1.32 μB/f.u. for Fe32Co28Ni28Ta8Al7, consistent with experiment. Since the local moments of Fe, Co, Ni are only slightly higher, the overall increase is attributed mainly to the higher concentration of ferromagnetic elements (95 at.% in A-MCA vs 88 at.%). Importantly, A-MCA has the lowest Hc among the variants of identical chemistry, achieved by controlling the micromagnetically relevant length scales—coherency, type, density, and morphology of defects—to reduce pinning from lattice distortion and internal stress. Ashby maps (Fig. 2c, d) show A-MCA combines high σy with moderate Hc, outperforming conventional SMMs and established MCAs; its yield strength exceeds all other MCAs (Supplementary Fig. 13). Its electrical resistivity is higher than pure metals, Fe-Si (<6.5 wt.% Si) and Fe-Co, and comparable to Fe-Ni and established MCAs—favorable for reducing eddy currents in high-frequency applications.
图 2b 给出磁性能。A-MCA 表现出典型的软铁磁行为，饱和磁化强度（Ms）115.4 ± 0.2 Am²·kg⁻¹，矫顽力（Hc）0.36 ± 0.03 kA·m⁻¹。与我们此前设计的强韧软磁体 Fe32Co28Ni28Ta8Al7（Ms 100.2 Am²·kg⁻¹）相比，A-MCA 的 Ms 高约 15%。DFT（精确 muffin-tin 轨道法）给出 Fe35Co30Ni30Ta5 的总磁矩为 1.50 μB/f.u.，比 Fe32Co28Ni28Ta8Al7 的 1.32 μB/f.u. 高约 15%，与实验一致。由于 Fe、Co、Ni 的局部磁矩仅略高，磁化强度的整体提升主要归因于更高的铁磁性元素浓度（A-MCA 中为 95 at.%，对比 88 at.%）。重要的是，在成分相同的各变体中 A-MCA 矫顽力最低，这是通过控制微磁学相关尺度——缺陷的共格性、类型、密度和形貌——以减少晶格畸变和内应力钉扎而实现的。Ashby 图（图 2c、d）显示 A-MCA 兼具高 σy 与中等 Hc，优于传统软磁材料和已有 MCA；其屈服强度超过所有其他 MCA（补充图 13）。其电阻率高于纯金属、Fe-Si（<6.5 wt.% Si）和 Fe-Co，与 Fe-Ni 及已有 MCA 相当——有利于在高频应用中降低涡流。

强化与增韧机制Strengthening and toughening mechanisms

To understand the combination of high strength and good ductility, we studied the deformation substructure using EBSD, ECCI and TEM at different straining stages (Fig. 3). Pile-up of geometrically necessary dislocations at grain boundaries is reflected by the kernel average misorientation (KAM), a local measure of inelastic lattice curvature (Fig. 3a). Microbands aligned along {111}fcc show pronounced dynamic refinement (Fig. 3b), inducing high dislocation passing stresses and continuous strain hardening that prevents damage initiation. Two types of discontinuous, incoherent, Ta-enriched grain-boundary precipitates form (promoted by Ta's high diffusion coefficient, 4.7 × 10⁻¹⁸ m²·s⁻¹ vs ~10⁻¹⁸–10⁻¹⁹ for Ni, Fe, Co at 1073 K). Although strain localization near these precipitates causes weakness and detachment at a later stage (Fig. 3c), A-MCA shows typical ductile fracture because the incoherent precipitates' small volume fraction (0.3%) keeps their influence on damage initiation small.
为理解高强度与良好塑性的组合，我们用 EBSD、ECCI 和 TEM 研究了不同应变阶段的变形亚结构（图 3）。晶界处几何必需位错的塞积由核平均取向差（KAM）反映，KAM 是对非弹性晶格曲率的局部度量（图 3a）。沿 {111}fcc 排列的微带表现出明显的动态细化（图 3b），产生高的位错通过应力和持续的应变硬化，从而防止损伤萌生。形成了两类不连续、非共格、富 Ta 的晶界析出相（由 Ta 的高扩散系数促成——1073 K 时 Ta 为 4.7 × 10⁻¹⁸ m²·s⁻¹，而 Ni、Fe、Co 约为 10⁻¹⁸–10⁻¹⁹）。尽管这些析出相附近的应变局部化在后期造成弱化和脱粘（图 3c），但由于非共格析出相体积分数很小（0.3%），其对损伤萌生的影响很小，A-MCA 仍表现出典型的韧性断裂。
TEM near the fracture surface revealed multiple parallel mechanical twins (number density (4.2 ± 0.7) × 10¹² m⁻², average width 14.7 ± 4.7 nm). Atomic-resolution STEM (Fig. 3d) shows twinning in the fcc matrix and at the matrix–precipitate interface, with FFT consistent with a <110>twin//<011>fcc system. Twinning is initiated by the high tensile stress (~2.0 GPa) reaching the critical value (~1.5 GPa) for activating twinning—generally difficult in bulk fcc materials whose SFE (73.9 mJ·m⁻²) far exceeds the typical regime (<~50 mJ·m⁻²), especially under quasistatic loading. This is enabled by the high strength from interactions between anisotropic precipitates and the dense dislocation network, providing an additional plastic mechanism at later strain that further ductilizes and toughens the material. The high strength of A-MCA arises from: (1) anisotropic nanoprecipitates with small inter-precipitate spacing (26.1 nm), high volume fraction (38%), specific surface area (3.3 × 10⁸ m⁻¹) and lattice mismatch (1.3%)—their anisotropic morphology giving a larger specific surface area than spherical precipitates (1.9 × 10⁸ m⁻¹) at the same number density/volume fraction (cf. cuboidal-precipitate Fe32Co28Ni28Ta8Al7 at 6.6 × 10⁷ m⁻¹); and (2) the high dislocation density (2.5 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²). Together these add ~1.2 GPa of strength.
断口附近的 TEM 揭示了多条平行的形变孪晶（数量密度 (4.2 ± 0.7) × 10¹² m⁻²，平均宽度 14.7 ± 4.7 nm）。原子分辨 STEM（图 3d）显示 fcc 基体内及基体–析出相界面处的孪生，FFT 与 <110>孪晶//<011>fcc 体系一致。孪生由约 2.0 GPa 的高拉应力达到激活孪生的临界值（约 1.5 GPa）而引发——这在层错能（73.9 mJ·m⁻²）远高于典型激活范围（< 约 50 mJ·m⁻²）的块体 fcc 材料中通常很难实现，尤其是在准静态加载下。之所以能实现，是因为各向异性析出相与密集位错网络的相互作用带来了高强度，使孪生应力得以达到，从而在后期应变提供了额外的塑性机制，进一步增塑增韧材料。A-MCA 的高强度来自：(1) 各向异性纳米析出相——小析出相间距（26.1 nm）、高体积分数（38%）、大比表面积（3.3 × 10⁸ m⁻¹）和晶格错配（1.3%）；在相同数量密度/体积分数下，其各向异性形貌带来的比表面积大于球形析出相（1.9 × 10⁸ m⁻¹）（对比立方析出相的 Fe32Co28Ni28Ta8Al7 为 6.6 × 10⁷ m⁻¹）；以及 (2) 高位错密度（2.5 × 10¹⁵ m⁻²）。二者共同贡献约 1.2 GPa 的强度增量。

磁性特征表征与模拟Magnetic feature characterization and simulation

We used correlative MOKE microscopy with EBSD (Fig. 4a, b) to characterize magnetic-domain/defect interactions. As the field varied from −80 to −40 kA·m⁻¹, magnetic domains nucleated preferentially at grain boundaries and deformation twins, which have higher elastic stress fields (~1°) than defect-free interiors (~0.2°). Coarse domains (25.3 ± 8.6 μm) grow unaffected in grain interiors, while significantly refined stripe-like domains (0.7 ± 0.1 μm) appear between deformation twins at 40 kA·m⁻¹. MFM (Fig. 4c) shows domain patterns near twin boundaries down to the nanoscale; AFM confirms the surface is smooth (<10 nm height difference), so the distinctive patterns arise mainly from cold-rolling-induced crystallographic misorientation. Fresnel imaging of a TEM lamella at remanence (Fig. 4d) shows domain walls that reform randomly after 1.5 T saturation, indicating an intrinsically soft matrix, with flux-closure 180° and 90° walls (Fig. 4e). The domain wall width (δw) is 105 ± 5 nm at a 180° wall (off-axis electron holography, Fig. 4f). Since conventional SMM δw ranges from 50 nm to a few μm, the precipitate dimensions (53 × 8 nm) are smaller than δw and do not act as strong pinning sites.
我们用相关性 MOKE 显微术配合 EBSD（图 4a、b）表征磁畴与缺陷的相互作用。当磁场从 −80 变到 −40 kA·m⁻¹ 时，磁畴优先在晶界和形变孪晶处形核——这些缺陷的弹性应力场（约 1°）高于无缺陷晶粒内部（约 0.2°）。粗大磁畴（25.3 ± 8.6 μm）在晶粒内部不受阻碍地长大，而在 40 kA·m⁻¹ 时形变孪晶之间出现显著细化的条带状磁畴（0.7 ± 0.1 μm）。MFM（图 4c）显示孪晶界附近直至纳米尺度的磁畴图案；AFM 证实表面平整（高差 <10 nm），因此这些独特图案主要源自冷轧诱导的晶体学取向差。剩磁态（图 4d）下 TEM 薄片的菲涅耳成像显示，畴壁在 1.5 T 饱和后随机重构，表明基体本征软磁，并呈现闭合磁通的 180° 和 90° 畴壁（图 4e）。180° 畴壁处的畴壁宽度（δw）为 105 ± 5 nm（离轴电子全息，图 4f）。由于传统软磁材料的 δw 在 50 nm 到数微米之间，析出相尺寸（53 × 8 nm）小于 δw，不会成为强钉扎位点。
Since lattice mismatch and interfacial elastic distortion can also locally pin domain walls, we ran 3D micromagnetic simulations combining APT/TEM microstructure with DFT magnetic parameters (Fig. 4f). This highlights the exchange interaction between the stronger ferromagnetic fcc matrix (Ms = 161.6 Am²·kg⁻¹) and weaker ferromagnetic precipitates (Ms = 38.7 Am²·kg⁻¹); the weak precipitate magnetism makes interphase-exchange pinning negligible, which is the determining effect providing high strength without sacrificing coercivity. Simulations show that introducing the weak ferromagnetic anisotropic precipitates decreases coercivity by 18% versus A-MCA, mainly due to the precipitates' decreased exchange stiffness, which lowers the external field needed for spin rotation. Thus size, morphology, structure and composition of the precipitates must be tuned carefully. Although the coercivity is slightly higher than conventional SMMs, the excellent mechanical properties make A-MCA suitable for severe-stress applications where robustness outweighs minimal coercivity—achieved by balancing the micromagnetically relevant length scales of the anisotropic precipitates (high strength) against releasing structural defects (lower coercivity).
由于晶格错配和界面弹性畸变也能局部钉扎畴壁，我们将 APT/TEM 组织数据与 DFT 磁参数结合，进行了三维微磁模拟（图 4f）。该模拟突出了较强铁磁性 fcc 基体（Ms = 161.6 Am²·kg⁻¹）与较弱铁磁性析出相（Ms = 38.7 Am²·kg⁻¹）之间的交换相互作用；析出相磁性较弱，使相间交换钉扎可忽略，这正是在不牺牲矫顽力的前提下提供高强度的决定性因素。模拟表明，引入弱铁磁性的各向异性析出相使矫顽力相比 A-MCA 降低 18%，主要归因于析出相交换刚度降低，从而降低了自旋转动所需的外场。因此，析出相的尺寸、形貌、结构和成分都必须精心调控。尽管矫顽力略高于传统软磁材料，但优异的力学性能使 A-MCA 适用于以稳健性优先于最小矫顽力的严酷应力场合——这通过在各向异性析出相的微磁学相关尺度（保证高强度）与释放结构缺陷（降低矫顽力）之间取得平衡来实现。

小结Summary

In summary, we designed a bulk multicomponent soft magnetic material with two-gigapascal strength, good ductility (12%), high saturation magnetization (115 Am²·kg⁻¹), relatively low coercivity (0.36 kA·m⁻¹) and high electrical resistivity (62.5 μΩ·cm). We realized this by engineering morphologically anisotropic, coherent nanoprecipitates with optimized spatial dimensions and patterns, chemical compositions, and coherency stresses. The magnetic properties and magnetization mechanism were revealed by correlative experimental and computational probes across a wide range of length scales. Multiple strengthening and toughening mechanisms are combined with soft magnetic features, realized so as not to interfere with domain wall motion, thus reconciling otherwise mutually exclusive design targets. The versatile, practically infinite compositional space of such MCAs and non-equilibrium nanostructuring concepts are critical for developing energy-efficient SMMs that endure severe mechanical loading without sacrificing low hysteresis loss. Although a high density and different types of lattice defects give a moderately higher coercivity, this concept enables highly efficient electro-mechanical energy conversion where mechanical robustness is prioritized—particularly for highly stressed magnetic parts under severe centrifugal loads in service or manufacturing, for which conventional SMMs are too brittle or too soft, such as electric-vehicle motors and high-speed flywheel energy storage.
综上所述，我们设计出一种块体多组元软磁材料，具有两吉帕强度、良好塑性（12%）、高饱和磁化强度（115 Am²·kg⁻¹）、相对较低的矫顽力（0.36 kA·m⁻¹）和高电阻率（62.5 μΩ·cm）。我们通过设计具有优化空间尺寸与排布、化学成分和共格应力的形貌各向异性、共格纳米析出相实现了这一概念。材料的磁性能及磁化机制通过跨越广泛尺度的相关性实验与计算探针得以揭示。多种强化和增韧机制与软磁特性相结合，并以不干扰畴壁运动的方式实现，从而调和了原本互相排斥的设计目标。此类 MCA 多变且几乎无限的成分空间，以及非平衡纳米结构化理念，对开发既能承受严酷机械载荷又不牺牲低磁滞损耗的高效软磁材料至关重要。尽管高密度、多种类的晶格缺陷带来了适度升高的矫顽力，但该理念为以机械稳健性优先的高效机电能量转换提供了途径——尤其适用于服役或制造中承受严酷离心载荷、高应力的磁性部件（传统软磁材料对此过脆或过软），如电动汽车电机和高速飞轮储能装置。
